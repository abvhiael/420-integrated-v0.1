# HC-ECON-02 — Edition issuance and verifiable modifiers

**Scope:** HC-ECON-00 E10, E11, E12 and E14, using the qualified HC-ECON-01 policy and the existing HighCountry economy serializable PostgreSQL state. This module is an ordinary-game issuance protocol, not an on-chain mint, asset import into R04.8, a public HTTP endpoint, or a license for ordinary mission rewards.

## Immutable edition and issuance lifecycle

The signed-certificate authority must authorize publication; every design has a unique immutable ID, type, policy, creator, certification reference and edition cap 1–420. A committed design cannot be republished or its edition cap reset. Issuance allocates an `issuedEver` slot in the same serializable transaction that records the item and consumes its independently verified source. Pending reservations count against the edition cap. Source IDs cannot be reused, including after a separately verified cancellation; cancellation releases only a pending reservation, never issuance credits or an existing item. Transfers require independently verified, one-time transfer receipt IDs. Burn permanently marks a copy removed without decreasing `issuedEver` or refunding any fees. Idempotent retrieval is limited to the original admitted owner. Revoked sessions are rechecked inside state mutation transactions. Ownership changes require an independently verified transfer receipt and are denied for quest-bound items.

## Randomness and verification

The user/creator cannot supply or select the outcome. The immutable intent commits the design, item, owner, purchase/quest source, policy revision, frozen catalog SHA and nonce **before** the item is purchased. The service then requires verified finalized source settlement/quest entitlement, plus an **independent finalized randomness verifier** for creator/quest outcomes. The verifier must attest the identical request context, deterministic precommitted request ID, entropy and finalized anchor. The request ID is fixed by the original reservation before purchase, eliminating alternate-request reroll attempts. Uniform rejection sampling over SHA-256 domain-separated expansion selects distinct weighted effects without replacement and uniform basis-point values within approved ranges. Pure replay verification checks the committed outcome, policy limits, and receipt hash; full external proof verification additionally re-queries the source and independent randomness authorities.

Creator outcomes remain unknown before finalized settlement. Creator policies are at most 500 bps (5%) and their configured range midpoint targets 2–3%; the weighted expected mean is also computed at publication and must fall between 200 and 300 basis points. Actual draws use integer rejection sampling, never floating-point monetary settlement. Developer/420Corp items have a fixed identical policy per SKU and **never request random entropy**. Quest items can use approved >5% policies but remain untradeable; their grant is a separately verified entitlement. HC-ECON-01 handles developer-role nonstacking, HC-ECON-03 handles installation/compatibility.

## Durable and trust boundaries

The issuer uses `PostgresBudsStore.transact` for serializable, row-locked JSONB state and five `econ*` maps (`econDesigns`, `econItemIntents`, `econItems`, `econTransferReceipts`, `econConsumedSources`) in the existing schema. Existing balances and BUDS economics are untouched. `authority` must provide registered principal, signed issuer authorization, verified paid purchase or quest event, verified authorized transfer/cancellation, and synchronous transaction-local session/issuer revocation guard. `entropyReader.verifyFinalized` must verify a pinned independent proof, deployment identity/code hashes and finalized chain anchor before returning `verified: true`. Do **not** wire this module to a public route or production issuance until these concrete independently verified adapters, identities, finalized RPC and code hashes are configured and qualified on HC-ECON-TN. A boolean or browser value is not a verifier.

## Acceptance

Targeted `node --test services/highcountry-economy/test/item-issuance.test.js services/highcountry-economy/test/object-taxonomy.test.js` and the dedicated Level 1 workflow validate caps, pending holds, no-reroll independent randomness, fixed developer bonus, >5% quest exception, quest nontransferability, burn-without-reissue, transfer proof, duplicate source, tampered outcomes, unauthorized ownership, valid proof and failed finality, verified cancellation, transfer receipt replay and weighted expected means. PostgreSQL serialization of the new state is included in the existing transaction adapter; integration with live issuer/purchase/VRF authorities remains a testnet-dependent activation gate, not a mocked successful release.
