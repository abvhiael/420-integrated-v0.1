What the terms mean

Source discovery
A link identifies an official instrument to review. It does not prove the instrument is current or that a rule applies to you.
Coverage
Coverage describes which topics and authorities have been reviewed. Missing coverage means important requirements may still be unknown.
Effective dates
A rule's start and end dates can differ from its publication or collection dates. Unknown dates cannot be filled in by assumption.
Authenticated evidence
An authorized service must validate the relevant identity, licence, policy and operation evidence, including expiry and revocation. Selecting an answer here does not do that.
Stage-specific evaluation
Order acceptance, pickup and handover need their own current checks. Return and safety recovery require separate reviewed workflows.

Public guidance, legal eligibility and actual provider availability are separate. A positive policy prerequisite alone cannot approve every applicable law, payment or operational action.
