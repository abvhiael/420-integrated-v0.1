# AI-RECOVERY-6.4.2 — native Vault deposit provenance (partial)

`420-ai-provider/src/vault-deposit-evidence.ts` adds a **read-only, native-asset-only** receipt validator. It checks the independently approved Vault destination, exact sender and transaction value, chain ID, confirmation depth and pinned block hash, successful transaction receipt, receipt block hash against canonical history, and a unique `NativeDeposited` event emitted by the approved Vault matching the sender and amount. The adversarial unit tests cover forged/missing/duplicate events, payer/Vault/value substitution, reverted transactions, chain mismatch, shallow confirmations and reorganization. The approved address, finality policy and RPC remain externally trusted security-critical inputs; one RPC is not independent finality verification.

**Not a funding adapter:** A confirmed deposit does not prove that a Wallet user authorized the intended AI job, that the deposited asset has been exclusively reserved, or that the payer's funds are attributed to an obligation. A pooled Vault balance or `NativeDeposited` event cannot be treated as a per-job reservation. The verifier does not sign, submit, reserve, bind escrow, move funds or support ERC-20 tokens. Do not call `AIJobEscrow.confirmVaultFunding` based only on the receipt. Paid AI stays disabled.

**Next implementation gate:** Wallet-approved, job-bound typed authorization; independently proven deposit and exact balance/reservation accounting under a governance-approved Vault adapter; unique job/funding reference and obligation IDs with replay protection; confirmed `AssetVault420.createObligation` receipt and matching canonical accounting `RESERVED` state; approved adapter-only `AIJobEscrow.confirmVaultFunding` and manager state change; deterministic recovery from partial or repeated submissions. A separate token flow must authenticate the ERC-20 allowance and exact vault deposit delta. Only testnet deployment evidence and economic/reorg/retry tests can qualify actual funding.
