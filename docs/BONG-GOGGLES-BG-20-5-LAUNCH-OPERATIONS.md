BG-19 closeout remains independent and outstanding. Proposed Cloudflare-hosted origin: `https://bonggoggles.420integrated.org`. ## Implementation

`bong-goggles/web/core/launch-operations.js` checks a release-specific operational packet against separately supplied BG-19 approval, BG-20.4 canary outcome, rollback readiness, current independently reviewed on-call/incident/retention controls and nine required checks. It binds records to the same environment, release ID, commit and artifact/config/policy hashes; rejects expired or incomplete records and self-approval; requires private ingress and unqualified features to remain disabled. Required evidence covers actual desktop and mobile accessibility, privacy/abuse response, incident escalation, on-call page delivery, backup retention, dependency/security review, degraded-service behavior and rollback rehearsal. `bong-goggles/web/test/launch-operations.test.js` exercises a recorded clean hold plus missing approval, mismatched release, stale evidence, incomplete checks and private-ingress failures.

All submitted URLs, operators, digests, approval claims and outcomes are **untrusted input** unless authenticated independently. The evaluator cannot establish that a test actually happened or a URL's contents are accurate; it cannot contact Cloudflare, page an operator, release traffic, enable a feature or approve launch. Every result contains `launchApproved:false`, `trafficChangeAllowed:false`, and `operatorActionRequired:true`. `hold_for_operator` is **not** authorization.
