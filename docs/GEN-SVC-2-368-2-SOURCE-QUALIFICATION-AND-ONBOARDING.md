## Geographic scope

Initial place-data onboarding is **City of Vancouver, British Columbia only**, not Metro Vancouver or the rest of British Columbia. The dataset's municipal boundaries, not a broad lat/lon box, must be the final region authority. The current adapter uses a conservative bounding-box *sanity check only* and MUST NOT by itself certify municipal coverage. Only publicly accessible visitor places are in scope; do not infer facility access, business ownership, private coordinates, or verified opening hours from a city map marker. No advertised inventory or coverage count until a qualified real extract and public-data review exist.

## Selected source and exact licensed acquisition route

City of Vancouver **Parks** Open Data Portal dataset (`parks`): https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/parks/ . The city describes park/facility information and says its extract is updated weekly. Prefer an operator-captured, complete, versioned **official portal dataset export** (not unbounded scraping, a third-party mirror, or an unrestricted live provider API). Official portal: https://opendata.vancouver.ca/ ; city publication/portal explanation: https://vancouver.ca/your-government/data-and-maps.aspx . Before a real run, verify the specific export format and its source ID/name/coordinate fields, official export request limits, completeness/pagination, extraction timestamp, snapshot SHA-256 and per-record source identity. The current Go adapter intentionally consumes a separately reviewed private normalized export contract rather than guessing raw portal field names. A data extractor and actual mapping from official source schema have **not** yet been qualified or implemented.

**Licence**: Open Government Licence – Vancouver, official licence redirect https://vancouver.ca/open-government-licence to https://opendata.vancouver.ca/pages/licence/ ; governing website terms https://vancouver.ca/your-government/terms-of-use.aspx . The City states open datasets are published under that licence, and that open-data material is excepted from the site's general non-commercial restriction. The OGL permits copying, modifying and redistributing information, including commercial use, subject to its conditions. On every consumer-facing data surface, acknowledge source with **“Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Vancouver.”** and link to the official licence and parks dataset where possible. Attribute the City as the *data source*, never as an endorser of 420Travel. Preserve licence attribution through downstream API clients and exports; clearly identify material changes and extraction time. No permission to reuse City trademarks/logos, third-party media, private personal information or rights outside the published dataset follows from this licence. Avoid pictures, editorial text, private/sensitive data and inferred verification status. Do not mix this extract into ODbL-derived databases without a separate compatibility review.

**Qualification boundary:** The source's dataset and licence route are identified for commercial public place facts, but no real export has been fetched here, the exact terms-at-acquisition revision has not been independently archived, and the deployment-controlled source grant remains absent. This document does not assert an approved operator or completed licence signoff. A historical or incomplete export cannot be treated as current inventory. A missing record in a partial/failed export is NOT a withdrawal notice. Verify corrections and removal against a separately captured complete refresh and operator review before canonical mutation.

## Implementation and tests

[Private parks staging adapter](https://github.com/abvhiael/420-integrated-v0.1/blob/feature/gen-svc-2-public-deployment/genesis/svc2/publisher/vancouver_parks.go) accepts a bounded explicitly mapped private `VancouverParksBatch` (`dataset`, `acquisition_id`, `revision`, `retrieved_at`, rows of `park_id`, `name`, `latitude`, `longitude`). It preserves opaque IDs including leading zeros, computes per-row raw and normalized SHA-256 digests, attaches dataset/terms/attribution identifiers and calls only `CandidateIntake.ImportCandidate`. It validates all rows before the first staging call, rejects missing or duplicate identifiers and invalid coordinates, and never interprets omitted rows as deletion. Its lat/lon box is a guardrail, not proof a row lies within Vancouver's municipal boundary. A real extract must supply an independently authenticated acquisition manifest, approved schema mapping and separately stored private normalized payloads before any canonical place can be approved.

[Adapter tests](https://github.com/abvhiael/420-integrated-v0.1/blob/feature/gen-svc-2-public-deployment/genesis/svc2/publisher/vancouver_parks_test.go) use **synthetic fixtures only**: pending-not-public behavior, provider-ID preservation, duplicate/invalid/out-of-region/partial invalid batch rejection and no source-grant rejection. They do not prove a live official export was parsed, nor certify a real grant or deployment. An unconfigured policy must reject the new source namespace. Candidate-by-candidate failure can stage a prefix: the operator must use a restricted transactional batch coordinator and reconciliation before real ingestion; there is no all-or-nothing multi-candidate transaction yet.

## Separation of event onboarding and release gates

No Tourism Saskatchewan or City of Vancouver event listings, organizer events, descriptions, event images or event credentials are imported under this place-source qualification. Event acquisition, licensing, organizer authority and recurrence cancellation require their **own** later qualification and tests. PR #368 remains draft: no trusted external checkpoint provisioned, no complete canonical recovery, no live operator/source credential grant, no qualified generation, no live public feed, and no Travel upstream activation. The public GEN-SVC-2 HTTP service is not wired to this staging adapter.
