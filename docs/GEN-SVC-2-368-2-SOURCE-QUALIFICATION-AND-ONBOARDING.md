An operator must approve and persist: `source_namespace`, immutable grant/version ID, qualified legal entity and independently verified access channel, acquisition endpoint/extract identifier and dated evidence, permitted commercial use + redistribution + storage + derivative use, attribution and license display obligations, prohibited fields, region and time bounds, source item ID/version semantics, freshness SLA, event cancellation / occurrence exception / place removal SLA, outage/partial batch semantics, revocation notification channel, contact and escalation, credential location (never credential value), retention/deletion policy, reviewer identity and timestamp, and signed/independently authenticated approval. Store source terms and rights evidence in a restricted ledger, not the public HTTP projection or repository secrets. **Missing, expired, contradictory or revoked evidence = no grant.**

1. Finalize and record region polygon or explicit municipal identifiers, public categories, event time horizon, coverage expectations and owner of source review. Do not advertise inventory beforehand.
2. For each proposed provider verify current terms at its official source; obtain written permission where required. Confirm licence compatibility for the actual public data product and downstream Travel client, including attribution and database export.
3. Qualify acquisition endpoint independently, with rate/availability policy and explicit allowed fields. Capture private source-manifest ID, grant version, exact acquisition revision/time, input digest, original provider identifiers (including leading zeros) and normalized digest. Credentials and source payload are not public.
4. Perform restricted dry-run import to **pending review only**; validate provenance, category mapping, dedupe and collision handling, model privacy/precision, independent organizer/place-control authority, and source withdrawal/correction coverage. No raw repository write bypasses canonical services.
5. Prove source refresh and revocation in tests: valid approved revision, changed source terms, revoked grant/credential, partial import, deleted source record versus temporary outage, place withdrawal and recurring event occurrence cancellation. Existing approved records must not reappear after replay or rollback.
6. Only after separate approved intake record, deployment-controlled trust grant and authenticated operator review may an exact candidate digest receive an approval. Canonical application must be durably confirmed and independently checkpointed before later publication phases may consider it.
