# GEN-11.18 — Genesis ordering and economic policy

Status: implemented; exact-head qualification is required after the last change.

Genesis Bundler candidate selection uses exactly `fifo-v1` (see [bundler/ordering/policy.go](https://github.com/abvhiael/420-integrated-v0.1/blob/feature/gen11-0-bundler-network-v1/bundler/ordering/policy.go)). The production builder selects candidates through this policy **before** applying the bounded per-cycle maximum (see [bundler/bundle/builder.go](https://github.com/abvhiael/420-integrated-v0.1/blob/feature/gen11-0-bundler-network-v1/bundler/bundle/builder.go)). The effective ordering key is the original admission timestamp, with the lowercased canonical UserOperation hash as a deterministic exact-timestamp tiebreaker. A fee, paymaster, source peer, operator identity, or sponsorship quote is not a priority lane. Unsupported ordering-policy settings fail at builder construction; no private fee-first/priority setting is supported at Genesis.

A qualified same-sender/full-nonce replacement may require a maxFeePerGas increase under the existing mempool replacement policy, but preserves the incumbent's admission time and expiration; it **does not purchase a new queue position**. Gas estimation does not set queue priority. A locally selected operation must still pass fresh chain-state validation/simulation and durable submission-intent checks before the execution RPC is called. A bundler can decline, delay, or fail relay; selection is not an inclusion, settlement, censorship-resistance, or finality guarantee. EntryPoint validation, chain ordering, transaction fees, and canonical receipts remain separate authorities.

Tests: `bundler/ordering/policy_test.go`, `bundler/ordering/genesis_policy_contract_test.go`, and the production-builder ordering/limit regression in `bundler/bundle/builder_test.go`. These verify FIFO under inverted input order, fee and sponsorship neutrality under capacity, exact-time hash tiebreaking, unsupported-policy rejection, and selecting before truncation. The exact current head must pass the repository CI checks before this phase is marked qualified.

Outstanding beyond GEN-11.18: third-party client/operator compatibility (GEN-11.19), adversarial closeout and main-branch reconciliation (GEN-11.20), and deployment-specific operational verification. This policy document is an auditable operator contract, not a claim that all operators or live production chains have been inspected.
