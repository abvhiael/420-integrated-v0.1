# 420 AI concepts

A **model ID** identifies a model family; a **model-version ID** binds artifact/runtime/schema/verification semantics. Material changes require a new version.

A **provider** is the qualified AI service identity. **ComputeMarket** owns execution-resource/economic matching, while 420AI owns AI request/model/result semantics.

A **result commitment** commits to off-chain output bytes/manifest. It is not universal proof of truth or quality.

A **verification profile** defines what evidence is required before settlement may proceed.
