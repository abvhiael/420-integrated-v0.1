# 420 AI FAQ

## Does 420 AI run inside consensus?
No. AI execution is off-chain and chain liveness does not depend on providers.

## Does a result hash prove the answer is true?
No. It commits to a result/manifest. Correctness depends on the bound verification policy.

## Can a provider spend from my Wallet?
No. Provider/job authority is bounded and does not create arbitrary Wallet capabilities.

## Are prompts and outputs public?
Not by protocol requirement. Private payloads may remain off-chain.
