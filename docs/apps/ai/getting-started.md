# Getting started with 420 AI

Choose the intended model/version and workload, review provider/deployment constraints, privacy policy, maximum spend, deadline and verification profile, then submit the request through Wallet.

A request progresses through bounded lifecycle states rather than a generic status flag. Funding does not mean execution completed; a provider result commitment does not mean the answer is universally correct; verification and settlement follow the policy bound to the job.

Never include secrets or private material in a public field. Large/private inputs and outputs should use the approved off-chain storage/delivery path.
