# 420 Arbitration FAQ

## Does Arbitration execute remedies automatically?
No. Originating protocols must explicitly consume finalized rulings.

## Can governance change my resolver mid-case?
No. Case policy is snapshotted when opened.

## Are evidence files public?
Not necessarily. The chain can store commitments while payloads remain private/off-chain.

## How many appeals are allowed?
The snapshotted domain policy controls this, capped at three by the current implementation.
