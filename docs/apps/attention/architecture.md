# 420 Attention architecture

Canonical Attention state covers consent, campaign lifecycle/economics, proof/nullifier state, reward entitlement and per-campaign Treasury liability.

Raw behavioral telemetry, media, private audience profiles and device data remain off-chain. Sponsors and verifiers do not gain Wallet signing authority or access to arbitrary private data by participating in Attention.

`AttentionTreasury` protects campaign liabilities from generic treasury spending and only releases unused sponsor funds after campaign closure and cleared reservations.
