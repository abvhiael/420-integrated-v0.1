# 420 Attention concepts

A **campaign** binds sponsor, metadata/audience commitments, verifier, budget, reward rate, per-account cap and time window.

A **consent record** is account-controlled, reversible state with a monotonic revision and policy commitment.

A **proof** commits campaign, participant, verifier, observation time, attention units and evidence hash. A single-use **nullifier** prevents replay.

A **reward entitlement** is reserved before it becomes claimable. Sponsor campaign funds are segregated liabilities.
