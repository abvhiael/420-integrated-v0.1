# 420 Attention FAQ

## Is participation mandatory?
No. Attention is explicitly opt-in.

## Is raw behavior stored on-chain?
No. Canonical state uses bounded commitments and accounting rather than raw telemetry.

## Can a sponsor spend from my Wallet?
No. Attention grants no arbitrary Wallet authority.

## Can the same proof be paid twice?
No. Single-use nullifiers and proof consumption prevent replay.

## Can sponsors reclaim reserved rewards?
Not while participant reservations remain outstanding.
