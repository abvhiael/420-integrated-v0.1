# FAQ

## Is Faucet available on mainnet?
No. 420 Faucet is explicitly testnet-only and never participates in mainnet genesis economics.

## How much do I receive?
The current policy distributes 42 testnet `$420` per successful request.

## Can I sell testnet `$420`?
No monetary value or redemption promise is attached to testnet `$420`.

## Why was I denied?
Common causes are the 24-hour address cooldown, 5/hour IP limit, failed abuse control, daily operator cap, hot-wallet depletion or emergency pause.

## Does the Faucet need my private key?
Never. It needs only a destination testnet address and normal service-side abuse-control input.

## Does a Faucet response prove my balance?
No. Verify the resulting transfer through canonical testnet chain state.
