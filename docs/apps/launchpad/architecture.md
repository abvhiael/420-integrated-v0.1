# 420 Launchpad architecture

The Launchpad family is composed of `LaunchpadAuthorization420`, `LaunchpadProjectRegistry420`, `LaunchpadSaleRegistry420`, `LaunchpadAllocationRegistry420` and `LaunchpadRouter420`, with stable identifiers in `LaunchpadIds420`.

The router coordinates bounded application flows but does not supersede Wallet authorization, Token ownership, Registry identity, Governance policy or canonical chain execution. Project, sale and allocation registries own their respective protocol state.

See also [canonical Launchpad protocol architecture](../../architecture/protocols/launchpad.md).
