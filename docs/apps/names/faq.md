# 420 Names FAQ

## Are `.420` names permanent?
No. Ownership is lease-based and records expire.

## Does a name prove identity?
No. It is a naming/resolution record.

## What happens on transfer?
The new owner accepts; resolution resets to that owner and prior profile/service links are cleared.

## Is reverse resolution enough to trust a name?
No. Canonical forward resolution and expiry are rechecked.
