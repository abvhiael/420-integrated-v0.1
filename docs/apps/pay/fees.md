# Fees and settlement

Review the quoted network and settlement costs, any tip, expected asset, minimum output, and expiry in 420Wallet. The frozen Pay rules set a 42-second quote lifetime. An expired quote needs a fresh review.

Invoices may be denominated in CAD, USD, or 420. Settlement uses an eligible asset and follows the invoice's acceptance policy. Swap conversion is atomic with settlement. See the [frozen Pay parameters](../../420PAY-GENESIS-PARAMETERS.md) for policy details.
