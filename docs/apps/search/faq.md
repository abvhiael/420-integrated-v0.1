# Search FAQ

**Does first place mean official?** No.

**Can Search certify ownership or rights?** No. It can link to the protocol that records those facts.

**Can private messages appear in Search?** They should not. Protected/private payloads are outside the public indexing contract.

**Can another search engine exist?** Yes. Ranking and indexing are replaceable.

**Why does Search show a result Explorer does not?** Search may include registered/public protocol domains beyond raw chain navigation. Verify provenance and freshness.
