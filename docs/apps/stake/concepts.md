# 420 Stake concepts

**Effective bond** is the validator collateral target. **Participant-owned bond** is the validator operator's own stake. **Protocol credit** is protocol-owned matched collateral and is not user-owned voting or withdrawal value.

**Activation**, **active service**, **exit**, **cooldown** and **withdrawal eligibility** are distinct lifecycle states.

**Accrued rewards** reflect canonical reward accounting; Stake420 does not mint or independently calculate issuance.
