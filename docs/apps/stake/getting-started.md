# Getting started with 420 Stake

At Genesis, validator participation requires the canonical validator bond model. The effective bond is 42,000 `$420`, composed of at least 21,000 participant-owned `$420` and at most 21,000 protocol credit where the canonical reserve rules permit it.

420 Stake is primarily a read-oriented facade. Registration, collateral, activation, exits, cooldown and reward state come from the underlying validator-economic contracts and consensus lifecycle.

Do not interpret a Wallet balance or a Stake UI status as validator eligibility unless canonical validator state agrees.
