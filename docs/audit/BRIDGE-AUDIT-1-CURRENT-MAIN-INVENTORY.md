### Release-boundary findings

- **Custody and economic settlement:** `GatewayRouter420.acceptInbound` currently verifies via an adapter, consumes risk and records a transfer; `initiateOutbound` consumes risk and emits a message. Neither path proves an actual lock/burn/mint/release or withdrawal from external custody on `main`. Responsibility lies with the approved asset-specific custody/gateway/issuer architecture and later end-to-end qualification; do not mark successful routing as a completed asset transfer.
- **Mock versus real:** Existing tests use simulated/mocked bridge proofs; source admission checks are useful but real upstream consensus/finality and downstream asset settlement are not proven.
- **Safety classification:** A missing route-adapter equality check and arbitrary transfer status are **known main-branch implementation defects**, not "blocked by testnet". Provider endpoint unavailability, true external-chain finality, issuer peer deployment, public testnet contracts and proof witnesses are genuinely external/live boundaries.
- **Risk limits:** `risk-limits.json` freezes CADC, USDC, ETH and BTC exposure caps; configured limits are not proof any route is active or funded. BTC must remain inactive until dedicated production withdrawal custody qualification.
- **External issuer support:** CADC issuer authorization/LayerZero peer/DVN configuration and USDC Circle CCTP on chain 420 cannot be inferred from local Solidity tests. No unofficial canonical representations may be activated.
