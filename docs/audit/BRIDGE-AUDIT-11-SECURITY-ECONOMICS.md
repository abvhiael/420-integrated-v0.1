## Confirmed corrections

1. The Wallet/SDK/Exchange shared client could finish its last risk RPC after a silent provider chain switch and reach submission without another live-wallet check. The corrected client rechecks expiry, chain and account after the last risk reads; the exact request also carries the reviewed EIP-155 `chainId`. The selected wallet remains the signing authority. This does not claim protection against a malicious wallet that intentionally lies and signs arbitrary bytes.
2. The client assumed an outbound route must have the local chain in its canonical source slot. Existing funded Bridge contracts also use foreign-to-local canonical routes with outbound enabled for their reverse leg. The client now checks either endpoint and presents the actual outbound source/destination assets, chain fingerprints and recipient family, retaining the canonical endpoint orientation in its binding. Previously valid reverse-leg routes were rejected; no contract admission checks are relaxed.
3. The client invoked router admission but discarded its qualification ID. Requalification under unchanged route labels could therefore survive a user review. The returned nonzero approval identity is now part of the frozen review binding and is checked again around confirmation.

Two original-behavior reproductions are retained in [client-original-reproduction.log](bridge-security-qualification/client-original-reproduction.log). All corrected client cases, including qualification rotation and the transaction's explicit chain ID, pass in [client-final.log](bridge-security-qualification/client-final.log). SDK packaging ships the same source and was rebuilt and tested; Exchange packaging and the actual browser-controller import were rebuilt successfully. This does not unlock the existing unqualified public browser form.
