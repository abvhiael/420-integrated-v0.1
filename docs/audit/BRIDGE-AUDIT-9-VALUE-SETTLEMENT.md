## Settlement, failure and recovery

An inbound transfer settles when actual authorized destination value movement and matching evidence are committed atomically with completion. An outbound transfer settles only after a qualified outcome verifier authenticates the actual remote payout, complete immutable transfer tuple, recipient hash, native transaction identity, unique proof identity and **original transfer qualification**. Local burns and SOURCE_PENDING requests remain outstanding obligations. BTC's established six-confirmation policy is probabilistic finality, not a claim of mathematical irreversibility. Its backing/signing provider must prove that miner fees are separately funded or explicitly charged under an approved fee policy, leaving source reserves sufficient for circulating and pending claims. The controlled backing double and payout SPV qualify interfaces/payment facts, not aggregate owned reserves or unspentness.

FAILED, EXPIRED, RETRYABLE, PAUSED and DISPUTED workflow labels do not return funds. Retry preserves the original funding and consumed source/message identity. A cancellation refund requires authenticated irreversible cancellation of every payment authorization, including signed replacements. Ethereum source cancellation writes a permanent barrier before any destination refund can occur; that source barrier must itself be authenticated as finalized. Old valid payout backing cannot cross it. An actual refund restores tokens/escrow and risk exposure exactly once. Native issuer recovery stays provider-owned and fails closed locally when unsupported.

An admitted but unminted inbound deposit can be irreversibly disputed. That dispute cannot be retried/resumed into a positive workflow. `sourceRefundIntent` requires the source replay key to remain consumed, no delivery, current qualification and the immutable transfer. The source gateway refunds only the original depositor after authenticating that irreversible intent. Only authenticated actual source repayment permits `resolveSourceRefund` to close the local reservation; no local tokens are minted. Delivered or issuer-native deposits cannot use this path.

Partial token failures, dispatch failures, failed proof verification and changed qualification roll back value, proof consumption, lifecycle and risk state together. Completed/refunded outcomes and used proof IDs are permanent. Revocation suspends new authority; a freshly approved outcome implementation can resolve an existing obligation against its original domain without resetting replay state. Custody checks the approved INBOUND/OUTBOUND permission mask at execution and rechecks the original reservation approval after dispatch. An inbound-only approval cannot burn or close outgoing claims; an outbound-only approval cannot mint incoming value. Ethereum source payouts/cancellations require outbound approval. Component replacement cannot inherit approval: custody pins the qualification registry/runtime and separately probes the router, transfer/route/risk/accounting registries and canonical asset registry. Each probe respects the existing fixed 50,000-gas bound. Direct custody identity is enforced; a proxy cannot silently replace its immutable controller. Any deployment replacement must retain authenticated liabilities and replay state under the existing qualification requirements.

## Exposure and authority

Route and asset maximum exposure includes circulating/backed liability plus pending outbound obligations. Admitted but unsettled inbound exposure is reserved and cannot fund withdrawals. Payment reduces outstanding obligations; cancellation restores circulating exposure without opening extra cap room. Source repayment removes only the associated unminted reservation. Provider fees count at the actual gross native supply created, while recipients are checked at their actual net amount.

Only the canonical qualified router may consume risk or move custody. The router independently requires current custody qualification before funding, fallback delivery or outcome recovery; Registry registration and a replacement contract claiming healthy balances are insufficient. Governance may configure, suspend, dispute or revoke; it cannot declare a refund/payment by supplying a label, fabricate token balances, bypass custody conservation, or silently inherit old approval after component/configuration substitution. Emergency withdrawal/recovery controls remain available under the established safety classes, without re-enabling normal transfers on suspended routes.
