# BET-AUDIT-5A.1 — Multiplayer Poker architecture, rules, trust and table authority

**Status:** Architecture proposal and testable local contract, NOT live poker, authorization to accept funds or completion of subsequent 5A steps. **Authority:** current 420Bet shared components and immutable game-version, capability and emergency policy on `main`. Preserve existing Video Poker V1/Phase 4 contracts and outcomes.

## Scope and invariants

This audit step defines an executable interface contract for *multiplayer poker tables*, not the single-player Video Poker machine. No on-chain custody, player seating, hand dealing, shuffled deck, betting engine, or tournaments are implied to exist.

1. **Rules and mode:** V1 proposal is cash-game no-limit Texas Hold'em, 52 cards, 2 private hole cards per seat, five shared community cards (flop 3, turn 1, river 1), 2–9 seats, small/big blinds ordered after dealer button, standard hand rank and tie splitting; no jokers or wild cards. **Tournament mode is a distinct, later version**, with no implicit switch between game modes. Table parameters, blind schedule, minimum/maximum buy-in, cap, eligible asset, ruleset hash, operator, vault and rake policy must bind at creation; amendment requires a new version/instance, never reinterpret an active hand.
2. **Authoritative state:** The future TableRegistry owns table definition, version, approved controller and terminal pause/revocation state. The future Seat/Ledger service owns single-writer seat assignment and chip accounting, subject to on-chain escrow authority. A single HandEngine owns state transition sequence and pot contributions for a hand. Only canonical authorized SettlementEngine/Vault components may move funds. Indexers, browser clients, observers and dealer transport have *zero* canonical write authority.
3. **Authority:** `BetAuthorization420.scopeForTable(tableId)` is already defined; per-action capability IDs `BET_POKER_BUY_IN`, `BET_POKER_TOP_UP`, `BET_POKER_ACTION`, `BET_POKER_SIT_OUT`, `BET_POKER_LEAVE`, `BET_POKER_WITHDRAW` exist. These are distinct from table registration, moderation and dealer/deck authority, which must be newly scoped and explicitly approved before any implementation is activated. No universal owner, no broad EOA settlement role, no privilege inherited solely from being a seated player. Revocation, emergency pause and operator suspension fail closed. `BetTypes420.EmergencyDomain.POKER_NEW_TABLES` and `POKER_NEW_HANDS` gate creation and hand starts separately; existing funded hands require dedicated preservation/refund policy.
4. **Private cards and trust:** A public-chain-only shuffled deck cannot keep hole cards secret. A named, independently controlled dealer/shuffle provider is a trust dependency, **not** inherently trustless. Require multi-party/decentralized shuffle with cryptographic commitments and selective proofs, or independently reviewed confidential computation/key-release protocol. The full deck and other players' hole cards must not be available on chain, in events, client logs or before authorized showdown; secrecy, selective withholding, dealer collusion and key recovery demand adversarial proof. On-chain commitments bind chain ID, table ID/version, hand ID, ruleset, seating epoch, deck root and the prior finalized state. Do not use public VRF seeds alone to pretend to hide cards.
5. **Hand state and ordering:** TABLE_OPEN → SEATED → HAND_COMMITTED → PREFLOP → FLOP → TURN → RIVER → SHOWDOWN → SETTLED. Terminal exceptions: ABORTED/REFUNDED/DISPUTED. Each transition binds monotonic hand nonce, action nonce, seat index, actor signature/session authority, turn order, deadline and state hash. Illegal actions, stale/duplicate signatures, unauthorized seat changes and cross-chain/table/version replays reject deterministically. Folds and all-in runouts may legitimately skip later player-action windows, but not pot-accounting or finality.
6. **Money and pots:** Per-hand chips conserved: starting table escrow + permitted buy-ins/top-ups − independently authorized withdrawals − rake/fees = current stacks + committed pots + liabilities. Bets cannot exceed available stack. Blinds and raises obey minimum raise rules, full/short all-in distinctions and last-aggressor reopening. Construct side pots from sorted contribution tiers; each pot awards only eligible non-folded contenders, tied pots split deterministically, odd chips assigned by committed seat-order rule. Zero-sum pots and fee caps; never allow rake on an uncalled bet; total payouts plus fees equal pot receipts. Settlement executes once with idempotent receipts, replay protection and segregated liability; multi-asset pots forbidden in V1.
7. **Disputes and failure:** Preserve signed actions, timestamp/height anchors, encrypted deal proofs and transcript commitments for independent replay; challenge/reveal window must precede non-reversible payment. Dealer unavailable, partial reveal, player disconnect, reorg, malicious coordinator, ambiguous timeout, forked transcript, dead table and unsettled pot **fail closed** to pause, independently adjudicated recovery or bounded refund according to a committed policy. Never let table operator unilaterally confiscate chips or forge a final hand.
8. **Collusion and abuse:** Sybil/related-player table joins, chip dumping, coordinated betting, bots, seat hunting and timing attacks need defined telemetry, player controls and independently governed sanctions. Enforcement cannot silently change historical certified cards/results. No misleading anti-collusion guarantees without monitoring evidence. Eligibility, responsible-play limits, privacy and region-based licensing precede any live table.
9. **Separation from Video Poker:** `VideoPokerV1420`, `VideoPokerDrawV1420`, and `VideoPokerViewsV1420` are single-player game contracts. Do not reuse their public initial-deal roots, hold mask, 9/6 paytable or replacement entropy as a multiplayer confidential shuffle. Share only qualified access, risk, custody, registry and fee abstractions with explicit conversion and separate test evidence.
10. **Deployment gate:** This step grants no deployable table authority. Production wager acceptance and gameplay remain **disabled** until local follow-on implementation, signed independent game/math/bankroll and security reviews, official funded testnet commitments/deal receipts, authenticated role binding, dispute recovery proofs, legal operator approval and phase closeout.

## Component ownership and interfaces for subsequent steps

| Canonical object | Single authority | Inputs / resulting evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Table/version and configuration | Future TableRegistry, scoped table administrator | Config hash, immutable rules, buy-in/blind/rake policy, authority and pause |
| Player seats / escrow liabilities | Future SeatLedger and authorized escrow adapter | Seat epoch, player eligibility, balances, buy-in receipts |
| Deal/hole-card protocol | Future committed dealer protocol independent of settlement | Pre-deal commitment, private proofs, public audit anchors, withholding resolution |
| Action log and clock | Future HandEngine (one canonical sequence) | Signed actions, nonce, turn, deadline, last accepted state hash |
| Pots and showdown | Future deterministic PotEngine/hand evaluator | Contribution tiers, side pots, eligible winners, proof hashes |
| Custody and disbursement | Existing restricted vault/risk/SettlementEngine interfaces only when specifically integrated | Single canonical ledger debit/credit, conservation, receipts and replay denial |
| Appeal/dispute and controls | Future adjudication roles separate from dealer/operator | Evidence-hash ledger, bounded pause/refund decision |
| Spectator/API | Read-only indexers bound to chain finality | Redacted public state, no secret hole cards or auth tokens |

## Local acceptance and unresolved gates

For 5A.1, a scoped verifier must assert: frozen architectural sections and trust-policy markers exist; canonical `BetIds420` poker action IDs, `BetAuthorization420.scopeForTable`, and `BetTypes420` poker emergency domains remain present; the proposal defines one writer per object, version/nonce isolation, confidential deal boundary, exact chip/side-pot conservation, replay and withheld-key recovery, eligibility, jurisdiction and release gates; and no runtime/deploy/permission artifacts are altered. Treat this as architecture-level qualification, **not** an executable poker table. Later 5A development must include substantive state-machine, cryptographic, integration, funded accounting, adversarial, and eventual phase-wide qualifications. Live acceptance is tracked in `docs/420BET-TESTNET-WORK-ROADMAP.md`.
