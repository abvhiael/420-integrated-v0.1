# BET-AUDIT-5A.4 — Workstream 3 authenticated, recipient-only private delivery

## Authoritative execution sequence

1. The initial encrypted deck and both full 52-card Groth16 re-encryption shuffles are independently accepted. PokerPrivateShuffleGate420 exposes an immutable stage-two final root only after both provider proofs and ciphertext publications succeed.
2. The funded hand and authoritative seating snapshot are reserved. PokerNLHEBetting420.prepareDealAssignment freezes the button, participating seat mask, seating epoch and first two physical dealing passes. No recipient proof can be admitted against an unprepared assignment.
3. Each player, from the actual owning seat account, registers a unique BabyJubjub encryption public key and supplies a production-version-pinned Groth16 proof of the corresponding secret scalar. The scope includes the chain, key-registry address, authoritative table ID, player address and live seat epoch. Every active seat must have a distinct proven key; mere legacy fingerprint registrations are insufficient.
4. Both independently identified providers prove their distinct public decryption keys sum to the exact threshold key committed by the accepted shuffle. Neither a governance assertion nor two addresses is a substitute for this actual group-equation proof.
5. For each of exactly the two physical positions belonging to each recipient, Provider A and Provider B each submit their own independently verified EncryptedShareDeliveryV1 Groth16 proof, signed by their separately admitted provider accounts. The on-chain proof book verifies membership of the exact final-deck ciphertext position, the provider private-key relation, randomized encryption of the provider's decryption share to the intended recipient key and the exact public points.
6. Each share proof is context-bound to chain, proof-book address, hand, final root, physical card position, frozen seat assignment, provider index, current recipient fingerprint, seating epoch and all 12 published ciphertext/proof point coordinates. The proof book rejects missing or duplicate shares, old epochs, changed keys, bad assignment or unpublished/unaccepted shuffle roots.
7. The real proof book derives a deterministic hash of the encrypted shares for both physically assigned cards and both providers. PokerDeckDisclosure420 refuses a dealer-selected envelope unless it matches that exact proof-derived hash, and exposes the resulting encrypted-share data through the public authenticated ledger.
8. The current owning player acknowledges the precise manifest, envelope, seat, epoch, player, hand, ownership fingerprint and chain with a domain-separated receipt digest submitted in their own Ethereum transaction. An arbitrary provider-supplied receipt, altered digest, other player's transaction, stale key or partial share set cannot authorize betting.
9. PokerNLHEBetting420.start checks both its own stage-two shuffle state and disclosure.allAcknowledged, which itself rechecks the actual proof-book completion, exact key fingerprints, all seat envelopes and both player receipts. The reserve/timeout/funded-recovery policies remain unchanged.

## Recipient-only recovery and information minimization

All 52 canonical encrypted deck ciphertexts are public, but plaintext cards and private provider shuffle permutations are not. The book stores the eight or more relevant *encrypted* threshold shares only for the canonical first two dealing passes (two shares per each of two cards per seat). All positions outside those passes cannot be admitted as private delivery. The public reads do not grant decryption authority.

For an assigned ciphertext `(C1,C2)` on BabyJubjub and a recipient with private key `x` and public key `Q=xG`, each provider publishes an independently proved randomized envelope `(U_i,V_i)=(r_i G, s_i C1 + r_i Q)`. That recipient alone can compute `D_i=V_i-xU_i=s_i C1`. Combining exactly both provider decryption shares yields `M=C2-D_A-D_B`, the originally encoded card point. The recipient checks the uniquely decoded one-of-52 card against their assigned physical position. The off-chain client must verify final root and position, the two provider proofs, the owner fingerprint, the manifest and the receipt hash before displaying it. A malicious client that sends its secret to another party waives this cryptographic privacy.

A nonrecipient or the other recipient cannot recover a share from its own independent secret. The real-workstream Go suite demonstrates successful recovery of both assigned cards for **two distinct recipients**, rejection of substituted recipient keys and independent two-provider privacy boundaries against the canonical 52-ciphertext stage-two final deck. The real Solidity suite verifies the same eight Groth16 share proofs, two owner key-possession proofs and provider aggregate with generated, codehash-pinned verifying keys, routed into the real recipient registry, real delivery proof book and real receipt consumer. Test-only stand-ins are limited to the externally proved hand/seat/shuffle state; funded native NLHE owner tests separately exercise the real settlement and admission engine.

## Replay, forgery and recovery

- Proof replay between hands, tables, chains, physical positions, providers, recipients, epochs, version IDs and final roots must fail. Proof-bit sets prevent reaccepting the same provider share at a position.
- Recipient key rotation invalidates the stored fingerprint for every prior encrypted share and envelope; any old player receipt is invalid for subsequent start. Duplicate live-seat encryption keys are rejected.
- Only the actual provider address may submit its own share-proof claim. A proof for the other provider's secret is not sufficient to impersonate its delivery action.
- Proof-book hashes, rather than provider assertions, authorize envelope publication. Only the seated player account may submit the canonical acceptance receipt. Dealer receipts by themselves never authorize gameplay.
- If any required share or receipt is withheld until the deadline, the existing funded, replay-protected no-betting cancellation restores the original escrow vector. No fabricated card or dealer-written receipt can substitute for cryptographic evidence.
- Repeating a transaction with an accepted receipt cannot transfer card data or reopen a hand. Distinct player keys and the first two canonical card positions are bound in the same immutable epoch.
