# P5.4.3 — Casino profit accounting

Custody-derived cumulative accounting, not an epoch finalizer or payment authority.
The canonical bankroll records actual settled stakes and gross payouts atomically;
equal returns require classification by the settlement owner, and unresolved
returns block reconciliation. Deposits, withdrawals, customer escrow, liabilities
and reserves remain separate. The Genesis adapter's retained shares are distinguished
from other LP equity. All share equity remains protected; no principal is revenue.

Fee funding, reservations, earned protocol/ operator fees, beneficiary obligations
and actual claims are tracked separately. Reward and promotion funding, outstanding
claims, actual paid expenses and reclaimed balances reconcile independently. Prefunded
fees are not newly generated protocol cash. An immutable asset-specific source
inventory rejects duplicate vaults, accounting identities and economics contracts.
Losses offset gains within an asset, never across assets.

This report covers the listed canonical bankroll and economics sources only.
Separate poker, sportsbook and Fantasy custody sources must not
be assumed represented. No production source inventory, retained protocol fee custody,
epoch closure, operating expense authority or distribution is established by this
report. Eligible distributable revenue is deliberately zero until those authorities
and subsequent waterfall integration are established. No funds are moved by reports.

Qualification: dedicated focused Foundry workflow, existing affected casino settlement
and capitalization regression owners; exact commit evidence required. No change to
Genesis allocation, payout tables, fee rates or LP redemption economics.

## Governed epoch accounting

`BetGovernedProfitEpoch420` snapshots cumulative counters from an immutable,
single-asset inventory. Its governance timelock can close an epoch once its
minimum duration has elapsed. The close deducts operating expenses and realized
losses, carries deficits forward, excludes separately routed revenue basis,
and checks new and prior allocations against independently reported free
protocol cash. A failed source check rolls back the entire close. The reserve
record is accounting only and grants no transfer or payout authority.

The epoch contract requires source coverage for bankroll, pools, cash poker,
tournaments, sportsbook, Fantasy, and expenses. The source
fixtures qualify the epoch arithmetic, authority, and state transitions.
Production must bind independently qualified custody adapters to the canonical
deployment. Constructor coverage is structural and does not certify an
adapter's claims or the completeness of its product inventory.

Sportsbook collateral is provisioned and withdrawn by each market's registered
operator in `SportsFundedTickets420`. Winning or losing settlement can change
that collateral without creating a protocol-owned receipt. Counting it as
protocol revenue requires an independently authorized protocol fee or proof of
protocol ownership and liabilities, including player credits and reservations.
