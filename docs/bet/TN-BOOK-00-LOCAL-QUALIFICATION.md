## Implemented executable engine

`clients/420bet-lobby/server/sportsbook-engine.mjs` implements a non-public, locally authoritative, serializable **simulation** of pre-match, exact-stake, single-event fixed-odds tickets using *integer atomic units only*. It does NOT use the public The Odds API as a settlement oracle.

- Versioned terms include operator, asset/vault identifier, event, competition, correlation group, immutable start/cutoff/deadline, schedule revision, market type, selection, signed doubled handicap/total, regulation/full-game and overtime policies.
- Supported v1 markets are two-way moneyline, three-way regulation moneyline, one-side point spread, and over/under total. No parlays, cashout, in-play, props, quarter/Asian/dead-heat or dynamic odds changes. Unrecognized types are rejected.
- Draft registration, independent risk approval, approved opening, suspension and resumption (revokes quotes through the epoch), irreversible market closure and finalized immutable result transitions.
- Exact rational decimal odds `gross=floor(stake*numerator/denominator)`, unsigned-256 overflow boundaries, positive stake and odds `n>d>0`, canonical exact stake/player/nonce/market/terms/expiry/observation/epoch/domain quote commitments. The local HMAC quote signer is a **test-only stand-in**, not an EIP-712 quote-signer key or activated chain capability.
- Prospective acceptance requires a separately verified player authorization and eligibility callback plus a live independent schedule/health callback: suspended providers, changed event start, wrong schedule revision, old observed timestamp and earlier actual start reject before local escrow/reserve. No client-provided settlement verdict is used.
- Serial acceptance transaction checks ticket, player, market, event, conservative correlation group, competition, operator and vault gross liability ceilings, player daily stake/loss exposure, source freshness, free operator capital and stake. *All markets for one event must share an event risk group.* Gross reserve is conservative: the operator independently reserves the **entire potential winning gross**, while the stake is tracked as separate customer escrow; opposing outcome exposure is not netted.
- Immutable accepted price and beneficiary. Player nonce and deterministic ticket identity reject repeated authorization; same ticket/nonce cannot cause a second reserve. Operator free capital plus reserved liability remains conserved. Settlement earns the retained stake on LOSS, loses net payout above stake on WIN, fully returns escrow on PUSH or VOID and releases reserve once. This is **local journal accounting only**; no ERC-20 transfers or canonical vault payments are executed.
- Authenticated source-fact candidate sequence, previous-digest binding, challenge hold, independent dispute resolution after challenge, approved finalizer, immutable final result, idempotent original-beneficiary settlement and conflict rejection. A policy-specified bounded event deadline enables a **permissionless NO_CONTEST void** even if the market operator refuses to close or submit result facts; it refunds the full original stake locally.
- `sportsbook-journal.mjs` provides single-writer owner-token fencing, fsync+atomic-rename persistence, restart recovery and denial of two simultaneous local writers. A deliberately injected persistence failure cannot acknowledge a ticket. This single-host JSON implementation is not a distributed transactional wager/settlement ledger.
- `sportsbook-engine.test.mjs` exercises winning, losing, push/void, three-way draw, spread, total, correlated market risk, insufficient capital, stale quotes, suspended source, changed schedule, result revisions/disputes, unilateral result rewriting, timeout, duplicate settlement, disk failure and restart. Scoped exact-head CI also runs the existing provider/UI and broader 420Bet integration tests.
