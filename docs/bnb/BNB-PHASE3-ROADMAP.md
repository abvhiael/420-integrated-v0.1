## Qualification milestones and exact-SHA rules

- Level 1: each ordinary step uses directly affected app-specific builds/tests/verifiers, negative/security/static/configuration/interface checks and exact checkout proof. Planning-only changes receive scoped document/traceability/boundary verification. New executable or substantive requirement changes require qualification at the applicable level; no skipped/cancelled/missing/stale check counts PASS.
- Level 2 M3-A: after BNB-3.6, retain app infrastructure/authority/Travel/Compliance/Pay integration and accounting/permission/failure matrices.
- Level 2 M3-B: after BNB-3.10, retain complete guest/host/trust/operations app regressions and resilience/security boundaries. BNB-3.11 is the final app-focused end-to-end milestone. Do not renumber steps or turn these milestones into global closeouts.
- Level 3: BNB-3.12 only, unless a material shared change independently requires broader coverage. Solidity Contracts owns the complete canonical source/test/script Foundry inventory once, approximately four balanced runner-aware shards, safe incremental caches and required bytecode/initcode checks. Genesis Address Authority separately owns addresses/namespaces/collisions/predeploy/frozen manifests, without duplicate full Foundry. Applicable Integrated/global Docs, retained app/client/Indexer/Search/RPC/security/deployment checks bind the same candidate SHA.
- Evidence-only commits may inherit qualified source only when they change no executable source/tests/workflows/dependencies/configuration/generated artifacts/interfaces/deployment state or substantive requirements. Phase 2 qualification is preserved as predecessor evidence; it does not qualify future Phase 3 behavior.
