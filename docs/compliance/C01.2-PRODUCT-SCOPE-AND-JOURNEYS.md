# C01.2 — Locked product scope, actors and journeys

Lock product scope, actors and journeys: traveller, consumer app, courier app, retailer, policy author, independent reviewer, publisher, operator and auditor. Record excluded domains.

Status: `LOCKED_DEVELOPMENT_SCOPE`; release: `INFRASTRUCTURE_ONLY`. This specification does not implement runtime authorization or approve a legal policy. BC/Vancouver is the candidate first pack, subject to qualified source interpretation and external acceptance.

Compliance owns reviewed policy and bounded evaluation. DOOBr owns order, dispatch, custody, handover, refusal and return enforcement. Travel and Maps present sourced guidance and coarse presence through Location; neither grants transactional permission. Automatic source monitoring creates proposals; independent review and approved publication remain mandatory.

## Journeys

### J01 — Browse jurisdiction and topic guidance

Actor: `traveller`. Owner: 420Compliance public guidance. Data boundary: `PUBLIC_REDACTED`.

Preconditions: Explicit region and domain; Reviewed informational coverage status available.

Success: Read dated, sourced guidance with effective intervals and coverage limits

Denial/failure: Missing or withdrawn coverage displays UNKNOWN; no legal-permission claim

### J02 — Run an informational travel scenario

Actor: `traveller`. Owner: 420Compliance public guidance. Data boundary: `PUBLIC_REDACTED`.

Preconditions: No raw identity, address or live order inputs; Scenario labelled informational.

Success: Show reviewed explanation, obligations and unresolved issues

Denial/failure: Unreviewed region/topic and incomplete facts show gaps; scenario cannot authorize an order

### J03 — View Travel/Maps regional guidance and DOOBr presence

Actor: `traveller`. Owner: 420Travel/Maps presence composer. Data boundary: `PUBLIC_REDACTED`.

Preconditions: Approved public Location projection; Current policy visibility and independently supplied coarse availability.

Success: Show permitted public guidance and coarse presence with handoff to DOOBr

Denial/failure: Stale supply/policy or anonymity failure suppresses presence; no identifiable courier or checkout

### J04 — Evaluate an intended consumer operation

Actor: `consumer_app`. Owner: DOOBr consumer workflow. Data boundary: `PRIVATE_MINIMIZED`.

Preconditions: Authenticated app and tenant/subject binding; Origin/destination, actor class and required evidence.

Success: Receive a short-lived action-bound decision and explicit obligations

Denial/failure: Absent evidence, unsupported scope or non-ALLOW blocks the consuming operation

### J05 — Reevaluate after policy or relevant fact change

Actor: `consumer_app`. Owner: DOOBr consumer workflow. Data boundary: `PRIVATE_MINIMIZED`.

Preconditions: Existing bound operation; Changed facts, expiry or invalidation notice.

Success: Request fresh applicable evaluation without altering historical evidence

Denial/failure: Cached approval, unavailable evaluator or policy conflict cannot preserve permission

### J06 — Read a decision explanation

Actor: `consumer_app`. Owner: 420Compliance authenticated API. Data boundary: `PRIVATE_MINIMIZED`.

Preconditions: Caller owns the bound tenant/subject/action; Protected evidence access independently checked.

Success: Read reason codes, evaluated versions and relevant obligations

Denial/failure: Cross-tenant/subject request or replayed receipt denied; no public private-decision lookup

### J07 — Evaluate pickup and dispatch

Actor: `courier_app`. Owner: DOOBr dispatch/custody workflow. Data boundary: `PRIVATE_MINIMIZED`.

Preconditions: Assigned courier and retailer evidence; Current order facts, region/time and stage.

Success: Return current decision and obligations for DOOBr to enforce

Denial/failure: Wrong courier/actor class, revoked credential or stale policy prevents dispatch

### J08 — Reevaluate at recipient handover

Actor: `courier_app`. Owner: DOOBr handover workflow. Data boundary: `PRIVATE_MINIMIZED`.

Preconditions: Assigned authenticated courier; Current recipient and stage-specific evidence.

Success: Return current decision; courier completes required human checks in DOOBr

Denial/failure: Unknown recipient eligibility, failed evidence, policy change or outage blocks handover

### J09 — Handle refusal and lawful return

Actor: `courier_app`. Owner: DOOBr safety/return workflow. Data boundary: `PRIVATE_MINIMIZED`.

Preconditions: Failed delivery or operational halt; Separate approved recovery/return workflow.

Success: Provide applicable return obligations; DOOBr manages custody and safe recovery

Denial/failure: Sale denial never implies abandonment; no reuse of a delivery approval for return or new sale

### J10 — Supply exact retailer credential references

Actor: `retailer`. Owner: 420Compliance evidence adapter / retailer. Data boundary: `PRIVATE_MINIMIZED`.

Preconditions: Authenticated tenant retailer; Independent approved credential source and exact store scope.

Actor: `retailer`. Owner: Retailer/DOOBr records; Compliance decision audit. Data boundary: `PRIVATE_MINIMIZED`.

## Binding product controls

Only a current, operation-bound ALLOW with satisfied obligations can permit the requested action. DENY, REVIEW_REQUIRED and UNKNOWN block transactional progression. An outage returns UNKNOWN. A simulation or guidance result cannot authorize delivery. DOOBr reevaluates at consumer operation, dispatch and handover, and on material changes, expiry or revocation. A blocked handover invokes a separate safety/return workflow; the old receipt cannot authorize continued delivery.

Receipts bind policy digest/version, evaluator/geography versions, evidence currentness, caller/tenant/actor/action, operation, evaluation/expiry time, outcome, obligations, reasons and coverage gaps. They are neither payment authorization nor universal certificates. Historical receipts remain immutable.

Author, independent reviewer and publisher are distinct. Approval binds immutable bytes; changed bytes invalidate approval. Reviewer scope and qualifications are required. Operators may halt permission and restore only current valid approved state; no administrator can override a denial. Audit access is read-only and scoped.

Public outputs contain source citations, coverage, effective dates, review status and guidance. Birth dates, identity documents, private addresses, recipient signatures, precise tracking, private orders and reversible personal hashes never enter public projections. No personal data or full evidence is stored on chain. Raw ID capture and mandatory personal record retention belong to the approved identity/DOOBr/retailer boundary. Exports are redacted and retention must be set through qualified interpretation.

Compliance does not execute payments or create independent billing in the first release. Canonical Pay/Swap/Wallet, Registry, Identity, Verify, Oracle, Governance, Notifications, Arbitration and Dev Compensation boundaries remain authoritative; their existence does not establish regulatory permission.
