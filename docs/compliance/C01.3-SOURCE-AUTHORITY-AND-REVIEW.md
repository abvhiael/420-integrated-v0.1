## Source taxonomy

| Type | Interpretation boundary |
|---|---|
| STATUTE | Enacted primary law; jurisdiction and commencement must be established. |
| REGULATION | Delegated instrument; enabling authority and operative sections must be verified. |
| AMENDMENT_COMMENCEMENT | Gazette, enactment and commencement evidence; announcement alone is insufficient. |
| MUNICIPAL_BYLAW | Official municipal instrument; geographic and activity applicability require review. |
| INDIGENOUS_INSTRUMENT | Instrument from the applicable Nation or authority; applicability is not inferred from map nesting. |
| LICENCE_CONDITION | General or entity-specific binding conditions where legally established; licence class and holder scope matter. |
| OFFICIAL_GUIDANCE | Official explanatory material; cannot replace operative provisions or licence conditions. |
| AUTHORITY_RESPONSE | Scoped regulator or counsel correspondence; record legal basis, limitations and confidentiality. |
| JUDICIAL_ORDER | Verified judgment or order; binding effect and appeal/stay status require qualified interpretation. |
| PRIVATE_RESTRICTION | Contract, property or partner restriction; may constrain operations, cannot waive mandatory law. |
| GEOGRAPHIC_EVIDENCE | Boundaries and address evidence; identifies geography, not legal permission. |
| SECONDARY_MATERIAL | Research lead only; AI, vendor pages and commentary cannot supply operative authority. |

Legal status is separate from document type: PROPOSED, NOT_IN_FORCE, IN_FORCE, REPEALED, SUPERSEDED, UNKNOWN. Retrieval date, consolidation date, enactment and commencement are distinct. Proposed, unknown, repealed or superseded material cannot implicitly produce permission.

There is no universal numeric hierarchy that resolves conflicts. Qualified review composes operative federal/provincial instruments, applicable municipal/Indigenous instruments and entity conditions. Geography, private contracts, guidance and software/governance approvals cannot enlarge mandatory legal permission. Unknown applicability and conflicts block affected permission pending review.

|---|---|
| legal | Independent scoped qualified review of cited interpretation and immutable policy digest |
| governance | Canonical protocol/configuration permission; never legal approval |
