# C01.4 — Shared policy authority amendment (DOOBr / 420Travel)

Status: PROPOSED CROSS-ROADMAP AUTHORITY AGREEMENT; no legal or operational approval. This document is the explicit amendment ledger for C01.4 and retains all existing requirement and step identifiers.

## Canonical ownership decision

- **420Compliance** is the sole shared owner of regulatory source provenance, reviewed legal interpretations, applicable jurisdiction-policy composition, policy pack versions, approval/publication/revocation and bounded evaluation outcomes. An automated source observation, inference, client-side rule, static table or governance vote never confers legal permission.
- **DOOBr** retains ownership of seller-of-record integration, courier/retailer eligibility evidence acquisition, operational order and custody state machines, customer authorization, assignment, dispatch, handover, refusal, return/recovery, payment integration and the enforcement of current Compliance decisions at each material transition. DOOBr may impose additional operational/safety restrictions but cannot create an alternate legal ALLOW or overwrite the reviewed rule pack.
- **420Travel** owns public presentation of independently reviewed, dated Compliance informational guidance and privacy-safe DOOBr coarse presence obtained under 420Location/Maps publication controls. Travel cannot turn guidance, a map tag or a regional availability signal into permission, expose private courier/order details, or execute DOOBr transactions. Its Genesis transaction gateway stays disabled.
- **420Location/Maps** owns geospatial resolution and approved public location projections, not jurisdictional legal interpretation.
- Identity/Verify/Registry own independently verified identity and credential provenance, Oracle owns observations where applicable, Governance owns protocol configuration, Pay/Swap/Wallet own financial authority. None substitutes for independent legal review.
- Separate legal approval, software qualification, governance authorization and operational/partner release acceptance remain mandatory and non-substitutable.

## Cross-service interface agreement for future C01.6 / R01.5

Transaction evaluation request must bind authenticated caller/tenant, operation/action/stage, subject/order, origin/destination jurisdiction proof and uncertainty, actor and carrier class, relevant independently verified credentials, evaluated event time and immutable input commitment. Response includes explicit ALLOW, DENY, REVIEW_REQUIRED or UNKNOWN, policy/evaluator/geography versions, effective interval, expiry, dependency/revocation epoch, reason codes, obligations and signed reference. Consumers verify issuer, audience, digest, operation binding, freshness, revocation and consistency; fail closed on missing/invalid/stale/contradictory data or outage. Re-check at dispatch, handover and material changes. An ALLOW is necessary but not sufficient for operation: current retailer/partner/supply/payment prerequisites remain independently enforced. Refusal and safe return have separately reviewed recovery branches; a transaction halt cannot require abandonment of custody.

Public read projection separates LEGAL_COVERAGE, INFORMATIONAL_GUIDANCE and SUPPLY_PRESENCE, with bounded reviewed citations and timestamps, coarse aggregation, anonymity thresholds, expiry and explicit unknown states. Public availability can never be interpreted as a private eligibility receipt.
