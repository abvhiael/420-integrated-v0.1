# C01.5 — 420Compliance threat and privacy model

**Status:** Design-qualified only; no deployed security control or legal approval. The machine-readable [threat register](C01.5-THREATS.json) is authoritative for individual threat IDs, abuse paths, controls, negative outcomes, and downstream requirement traceability.

## Architecture trust boundaries

External publisher/website → sandboxed collector → immutable raw snapshot store → untrusted parser/AI proposal → scoped policy author → independent qualified legal reviewer → separate publisher with signed digest → authenticated evaluator → bound DOOBr stage enforcement. A distinct redacted public guidance/presence projection feeds 420Travel/Maps. 420Identity/Verify/Registry supply separately verified evidence; Oracle readings and Governance configuration are not legal interpretation. Payment stays within Pay/Wallet authority. No transaction gateway is enabled by this threat model.

## Privacy and data minimization

Collect only necessary scoped evidence; keep raw ID images, names, signatures, birth dates, exact addresses, precise locations, orders, and regulated event histories off-chain and outside public guidance. A cryptographic hash of a predictable personal identifier is not anonymization. Require field-level access control, purpose limitation, explicit access logging, retention/deletion review and encrypted backups. Signed policy digests may be public only if they cannot be used to infer personal facts. Redacted exports must not include recoverable personal data or unbounded tenant history.

## Mandatory invariants

1. No source fetch, AI output, software PASS, governance vote or operator action can activate unreviewed legal permission.
2. Author, independent reviewer and publisher are distinct and separately authenticated. A changed source/policy digest invalidates review.
3. No regulated action progresses on missing, stale, unknown, conflicted, revoked, or unsigned mandatory evidence; ALLOW is stage/action/tenant/order/epoch bound and still requires external prerequisites.
4. No public guidance/presence response is treated as operational authorization. Travel compatibility gateway remains disabled.
5. A stop or denial does not authorize abandonment of custody; a separately reviewed safe return/refusal path is required.
6. No private data or guessable personal hashes on-chain, in public projections or public logs.
7. Threat register IDs T01–T16 and downstream target IDs are retained until covered by later implementation/negative tests; C01.5 qualifies design traceability, not their runtime execution.

## Release gates and residual risks

Regulatory counsel, municipal and Indigenous authority reviews, retailer/carrier evidence, privacy retention requirements and platform operations remain unapproved. C01.6 must embody trust-boundary and status semantics in APIs; C02–C07 must implement and adversarially exercise controls; C01.8 reviews integrated architecture, C08.5 owns phase Level 3, and C09/C10 gate deployed/legal/partner acceptance. Unknown source authenticity, delayed revocation, low anonymity counts, publication races and coercion are high residual risks: prevent operational enabling until mitigated and tested. No new Genesis authority or token is created.
