# C01.6 — API, event, time and deployment architecture decision

Status **DESIGN CONTRACT — NOT A RUNNING SERVICE OR LEGAL APPROVAL**. Canonical step and requirement IDs are unchanged. The machine-readable contract is [C01.6-CONTRACT.json](C01.6-CONTRACT.json).

## Architecture choice
Go `net/http` typed API, generated OpenAPI document, PostgreSQL/PostGIS for durable scoped state and reviewed geography, encrypted versioned object storage for permitted raw evidence, transactional outbox and isolated source-ingestion / optional AI processes. Deploy stateless service replicas separated into public guidance, authenticated internal evaluation and private review/publishing trust zones, with least-privilege credentials, TLS, backups and production/staging/testnet separation. This is the C01.6 technology/deployment **selection**, not a claim of deployed availability. C01.7 retains authority over discovery, signature and optional chain commitment decisions.

## Contracts and owners
- `POST /v1/evaluations` service-authenticated and tenant/actor/order/stage-bound; the response is non-transferable, signed, short-lived and revocation-aware. It is not a Pay, Wallet or licensing permission.
- `GET /v1/decisions/{decision_id}` enforces caller/tenant/subject access independently. No public lookup.
- `GET /v1/public/guidance` and `/v1/public/coverage` expose reviewed citations/dated redacted information only; they never expose transaction receipts or user/order/GPS data. Travel consumes these through its own approved public projection; DOOBr owns stage enforcement.
- `GET /v1/policy-status` is service-authenticated. Private review, publication and emergency-halt endpoints have separate reviewer/publisher/operator identities and authorization.
- Explicit `ALLOW`, `DENY`, `REVIEW_REQUIRED`, `UNKNOWN` outcomes: only current correctly bound `ALLOW` can satisfy the **policy prerequisite**, and DOOBr independently checks all operational and payment prerequisites. Others block regulated action. Failures cannot be converted into ALLOW by missing data or timeout.
- Events use versioned signed envelopes, per-domain/region monotonic sequence and durable outbox semantics. Duplicate delivery is idempotent; out-of-order, missing or revoked epochs force canonical re-read. Events are invalidation hints, not permission grants.

## Time and fault semantics
All instants RFC3339 UTC; IANA local zones and daylight-saving transitions apply only with reviewed jurisdiction provenance. Distinguish collection, publication, commencement, activation, review expiry, credential expiry, decision expiry and historical knowledge time; never collapse them into one timestamp. For mandatory evidence, the effective freshness bound is the minimum applicable dependency validity interval. Replay, expired signatures, wrong audience/tenant/stage, uncertain geography, source outage and stale policy deny progress. Separate refusal/return recovery cannot inherit dispatch or handover ALLOW.

## Security and privacy
Collector/web data and optional AI analysis remain untrusted and isolated from the approval plane. No self-review, raw customer data on-chain, user-selected upstream URLs or public private-decision responses. Audit and provenance use opaque scoped IDs and minimal digests; enforce authorization at every boundary. This document designates controls; C02–C07 must implement and prove runtime behavior.
