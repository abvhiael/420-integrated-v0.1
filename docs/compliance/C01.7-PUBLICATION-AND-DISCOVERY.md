**Decision:** APPROVED DESIGN ONLY. No operational legal rule, production key, live endpoint, or new Genesis identifier/address is authorized. Existing C01 requirement IDs remain unchanged.

## Decision and authority boundary
Use explicit, environment-scoped approved service discovery records for the Go/PostgreSQL C01.6 architecture. ## Publication and signed receipts
Independent qualified legal review binds immutable provenance and policy digests. A **different** authorized publisher schedules the exact reviewed bytes and signs a canonical manifest with domain, jurisdiction, actor class, environment, source/review digests, effective/review intervals, sequence and revocation epoch. Off-chain Ed25519 is the selected cryptographic design, with environment-isolated managed signing keys and audited rotation; this document does not claim an actual key, signer, HSM or live enforcement. Consumers must validate issuer, signature, key status, digest, audience, effective interval, revocation epoch and monotonic sequence before trusting a decision. Emergency operators can halt, not approve. Outages and ambiguities block new regulated actions; lawful safe-return handling requires its separately reviewed authority.

Commit only independently reviewed nonpersonal digests, never personal data or low-entropy identifying hashes. Do not create a parallel token, currency, settlement or permission authority.
