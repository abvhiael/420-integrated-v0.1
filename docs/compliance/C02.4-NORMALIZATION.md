# C02.4 — Provision and amendment normalization

The Go normalization component at `services/complianceauth/provision.go` retains each original provision and amendment verbatim, links its source digest and legal locator, and separately records normalized whitespace-only wording, instrument identity, externally attested legal status, and temporal applicability.

Permitted statuses: `PROPOSAL`, `BILL`, `ENACTED_NOT_IN_FORCE`, `IN_FORCE`, `REPEALED`, and `UNKNOWN`. Only an independently evidenced, effective `IN_FORCE` provision may be nominated for a later policy review. A proposed/bill/not-yet-commenced amendment cannot replace operative text. An in-force amendment must have a verified effective interval, source digest and authority reference. Neither normalized output nor a fixture status constitutes the qualified legal reviewer approval required at C04 and the external C09/C10 gates.

The original source text is never overwritten. Contradictory or missing authority/effective evidence fails closed. CI exercises pending bills, proposals, not-in-force amendments, amended text, future commencement, invalid digests, and missing status evidence.

**Scope:** pure normalization of trusted structured source records, not automated extraction of statutory wording, interpretation, true legal commencement verification, or a live policy activation path. Those remain independently gated. Level 2 C02.10 and Level 3 C08.5 remain deferred.
