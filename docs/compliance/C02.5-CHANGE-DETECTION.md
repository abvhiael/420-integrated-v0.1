# C02.5 — Semantic and structural official-source change detection

Canonical requirement: **Semantic and structural change detection; suppress navigation-only noise without hiding meaningful changes; identify removal, relocation and silent amendments.**

The `services/complianceauth/source_change.go` comparator evaluates independently captured section locators, normalized section wording, commencement/effective intervals and immutable snapshot hashes. It emits auditable `SILENT_AMENDMENT`, `EFFECTIVE_DATE_SHIFT`, `REMOVED`, `RELOCATED`, `ADDED`, `SOURCE_OUTAGE` and `NAVIGATION_ONLY` events. Matching normalized wording at a different locator is classified as relocation rather than deletion plus addition. Duplicate, ungrounded or cross-source sections fail closed.

Navigation-only changes are distinguished from provision changes **only when complete and trustworthy section extraction is available**. An unparseable source is not an empty list: the collecting adapter must signal unavailable/invalid and quarantine it, not suppress the source change. The comparator's outputs never publish, replace, or authorize a legal policy. Meaningful changes and outages are review work items; production decisions retain existing independent sign-off/freshness gates.

This step is scoped to deterministic Go comparisons of validated section records and fixture-based tests. Persistent review-queue storage, live provider outages and multi-instance delivery remain downstream C02/C07/C09 responsibilities. Level 2 is C02.10 and Level 3 is C08.5.
