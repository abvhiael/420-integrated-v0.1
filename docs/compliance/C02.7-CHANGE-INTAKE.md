# C02.7 — Change intake with accountable review ownership

Canonical requirement: **Change intake queue with severity, affected apps/regions/rules, effective dates and named review ownership.**

The implementation at `services/complianceauth/change_intake.go` provides a concurrency-safe local intake queue for independently identified official-source changes. Each work item includes an immutable snapshot digest, change type, severity, affected applications, regions and rules, effective date, detection time and a nonempty reviewer identity. Identical items are deduplicated by deterministic SHA-256 identity. Pending items are listed by owner, and acknowledgment requires a matching owner and non-backdated time. Replayed acknowledgment is rejected.

**Authority boundary:** An acknowledgement is only an acknowledgment of intake receipt: `ACKNOWLEDGED_PENDING_LEGAL_REVIEW`, not legal approval or policy publication. No intake operation sets `PolicyAuthorized` to true. Unknown reviewers, missing scope, invalid hashes or severity, and forged caller authorization fail closed.

**Qualification:** Targeted concurrency, replay, ownership, scope, invalid input and review-not-approval tests; retain prior Compliance checks. Level 2 remains C02.10; Level 3 remains C08.5.

**Operational gap:** The queue is process-local and does not claim durable database-backed delivery, authenticated externally attested reviewer identities, multi-instance leasing or regulator/legal sign-off. Deployment/storage and independent authorization are gated at C07/C09/C10; C02.8 covers collection failures, expiry and escalation behavior.
