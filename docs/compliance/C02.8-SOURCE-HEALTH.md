# C02.8 — Official-source health, escalation and legal-review expiry

Canonical requirement: monitor failed collection, source disappearance, expired reviews and unreadable updates; define escalation and expiry behaviour. A successful fetch alone must never renew legal review.

The `SourceHealthInput` and `AssessSourceHealth` boundary tracks collection failure, disappearance, unreadable updates, last successful source collection, freshness SLA, independent legal-review expiration and reviewed-policy digest. All missing, stale, unavailable or expired mandatory evidence reports `BLOCKED_REVIEW_REQUIRED` and `BLOCK_TRANSACTION_AND_ESCALATE_TO_SOURCE_OWNER_AND_INDEPENDENT_REVIEWER`. A healthy source with a current review produces `SOURCE_HEALTHY_REVIEW_CURRENT_AUTHORIZATION_UNPROVEN`, not permission to transact.

Review expiration is an independent clock. An HTTP 200/304 response does **not** refresh independent reviewer authorization, issue credentials or publish a legal policy. Source owner alerts, actual independently authorized reviewer workflows and distributed alert delivery require the later C07/C09 integrations. The Go unit tests cover failure, disappearance, unreadable content, stale-source, expired/missing reviews, bad digest and healthy-but-still-unauthorized behavior. C02.10 Level 2 and C08.5 Level 3 remain deferred.
