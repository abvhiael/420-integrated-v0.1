# C03.1 — Versioned authority graph and jurisdiction boundaries

Canonical step: Versioned authority graph and boundaries; distinguish operating zone from legal jurisdiction and resolve uncertain/border addresses conservatively.

`services/complianceauth/authority_graph.go` validates an explicitly versioned source-grounded graph of federal/provincial/municipal/local authority, effective intervals, ancestry, parent references and cycles. The resolution boundary accepts an operating-zone identifier for logistics **separately** from legally applicable jurisdiction IDs. Only a single independently evidenced, verified legal jurisdiction may resolve. All borders, unknown/ambiguous locations, multi-jurisdiction candidates, missing boundary proof, expired nodes, dangling parents and cycles return `REVIEW_REQUIRED` and no authorization. A successful resolution collects applicable ancestor legal jurisdictions but **never** establishes legal permission.

This package does not geocode real street addresses, map property boundaries, or claim independently authenticated boundary data. Real map/polygon/address inputs require a verified jurisdiction publisher or licensed geospatial provider and conservative deployment acceptance. The tests use **fixture identifiers only**, not a claim that these are fully verified official legal boundaries. Nor does any policy become active merely because a jurisdiction is resolved.
