# C03.11 — Engine Level 2 integration milestone

Canonical requirement: **independently reproducible historical and current decisions; cross-layer and carrier-class integration evidence**.

## Scope and invariant

The retained `TestC0311EngineLevel2ReplayAndCarrierBoundary` links independently introduced C03 authority graph, time/knowledge version selection, reviewed signed policy evaluation, shared publication-fence validation, stage-bound decision receipts, and read-only impact reporting. It runs a historical policy/authority snapshot twice and compares decision outcome and cryptographic commitments, preserving history after advancing the current publication sequence and revocation epoch.

A publication fence is a prerequisite to live action authorization. Raw `EvaluationService.Evaluate` results alone are evidence rather than a carrier workflow capability: downstream action dispatch must separately validate the authoritative current publication fence and operation-stage state. The integration test confirms stale cached publication claims, replayed receipts, forged tenant/facts or audience changes, unknown boundaries and unavailable publication storage cannot establish action eligibility.

The test's synthetic trusted issuer, legal-review approval, location graph and policy signatures are **test fixtures**. They do not qualify or invent legal licensing, a real independent reviewer, a real regulator, or a deployed DOOBr/Travel/Maps adapter. Travel public informational projection must never act as a delivery authorization. The process-local receipt ledger remains unsuitable for multi-instance deployment; persistent transactional replay fencing and complete operation-state integration require separate acceptance.

## Qualification boundary

This is the **Level 2 C03 milestone** and must pass the dedicated app-specific Go race, retained C01–C03 regressions, PostgreSQL publication/reconnect test steps, C03.11 integration gate and exact-SHA assertion. Do not mark PASS until the CI job passes. Preserve the implementation SHA in `docs/compliance/qualification/C03.11-level2.json` with traceable run/job evidence. Do not trigger global Foundry, Genesis, Docs or repository-wide Level 3 at this milestone. Level 3 remains C08.5.

## Remaining production acceptance

Before production authorization: independently established legal review, secure provisioned identities and credentials, verified geography and licensed source evidence, deployed verified TLS/PostgreSQL production-primary and failover acceptance, durable multi-instance receipt/nonce consumption, signed deployment trust and current carrier-operation state integration. These cannot be represented as satisfied by deterministic fixtures.
