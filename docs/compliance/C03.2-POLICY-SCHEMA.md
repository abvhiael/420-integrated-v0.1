# C03.2 — Versioned regulatory policy schema

The Go schema and validator in `services/complianceauth/policy_schema.go` represent actor roles and credential issuers, product classes and exact rational quantity equivalency, action stages and their credential requirements, local business hours with IANA timezone and overnight declaration, recipient verification, payment prerequisite, return duties and records. Each policy ties to a legal jurisdiction, graph version, cited source digest and effective interval. Conflicting or missing required fields, invalid timezone or time, duplicate identifiers, invalid ratios and overflow fail closed.

These are **structural policy declarations**, not law or approved permissions. A mandatory `DRAFT_REVIEW_REQUIRED` review state prevents a schema from impersonating a signed legal decision. Actual regulated thresholds, operating-hour rules, approved issuers and legal reviewers cannot be inferred from fixtures and remain independently controlled. Subsequent C03 steps must implement temporal/equivalency/rule evaluators, real authority binding, and independent approval. C03.2 Level 1 uses the targeted Go test with retained Compliance suites; Level 2 at C03 milestone, Level 3 at C08.5.
