# C03.4 — Reviewed composition and fail-closed authority conflicts

Canonical requirement: **Reviewed composition and conflict resolution; omissions and unresolved authority conflicts cannot silently produce ALLOW.**

`services/complianceauth/policy_composition.go` composes the Cartesian coverage of explicitly required regulatory scopes and applicable legal jurisdictions. Every scope/jurisdiction must be accounted for by a source-digest-bound and review-digest-bound rule. Missing cells produce `OMITTED_REQUIRED_SCOPE`; duplicate or disagreeing cells produce review reasons. Explicit `DENY` wins over any candidate permissive result, while unreviewed or omitted data remains `REVIEW_REQUIRED`. A fully covered set of independently reviewed `ALLOW_CANDIDATE` inputs produces **`REVIEWED_CANDIDATE_AUTHORIZATION_REQUIRED`**, never `ALLOW` or transaction authorization.

This structure does not attest legal review: the supplied review state and digests must be independently verified by the separate publishing authority. No synthesized result may become a signed production policy by itself. The implementation is intentionally not a legal conflict-precedence algorithm; unresolved disagreement remains blocked, and a restrictive rule can deny. The subsequent C03.5 compiler and evaluator must apply authorization gates. Level 1 tests include omissions, conflict, denial, duplicate, missing approval evidence and foreign jurisdictions; C03 Level 2 and C08.5 Level 3 are deferred.
