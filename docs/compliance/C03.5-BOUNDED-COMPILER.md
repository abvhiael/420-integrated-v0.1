# C03.5 — Bounded deterministic policy compiler and evaluator

Canonical step: safe policy compiler and deterministic evaluator with bounded execution, fixed-precision quantities, explicit units, reason codes and obligations.

`CompileBoundedPolicy` accepts only a versioned, non-Turing-complete sequence of no more than 128 simple quantity ceilings. It rejects duplicate IDs, invalid unsigned integer values, unsupported decimal notations and oversized numbers; sorts instructions canonically for deterministic execution. `EvaluateBoundedPolicy` accepts no more than 128 facts and compares exact decimal integers with explicit units (no binary floating-point rounding). Reason codes distinguish invalid programs, fact limits, missing facts, unit mismatch, invalid quantities and exceeded maxima. Applicable obligations are sorted deterministically.

The engine has no loops supplied by policy inputs, jumps, calls, recursion, external I/O or user-authored scripts; the host's loop is bounded. An exceeded maximum yields DENY. Missing facts and invalid units yield REVIEW_REQUIRED. A compliant numerical candidate yields CANDIDATE_AUTHORIZATION_REQUIRED, **never ALLOW or signed policy authorization**. This is a bounded *subset* of regulatory rule execution and does not independently authorize real operations, validate external policy approval, or express every jurisdiction's legal semantics. Broader predicates and safe-authority acceptance belong to subsequent canonical steps.

C03.5 Level 1 retains existing app security and race tests alongside dedicated numeric precision, ordering, replay and instruction-boundary cases. Level 2 is reserved for the C03 app milestone and comprehensive Level 3 for C08.5.
