# C03.6 — Canonical credential references and evidence validity

Canonical step: **Canonical credential references with validity, verification freshness, revocation and explicit unavailable states; no fabricated live licensing adapter.**

`services/complianceauth/credential_reference.go` records a canonical credential reference with scheme, issuer, subject, credential ID, credential type, legal jurisdiction and source-evidence digest. It deliberately contains no privileged private attributes or claims of approval.

An independently trusted `CredentialVerifier` interface may return a referenced response with state, issuer-trust assertion, evidence hash, validity interval, verification timestamp and revocation timestamp. The boundary rejects mismatched references, source digests, untrusted issuer assertions, missing/future/stale verification, unbounded freshness, expired or not-yet-valid documents and contradictory revocation facts. Known revocation or a known missing credential returns `DENIED`; absent, failing or interrupted providers yield `UNAVAILABLE`; indeterminate evidence is `REVIEW_REQUIRED`. Even successfully verified current references yield only `VALID_REFERENCE`, **never legal authorization**.

No regulator endpoint, legal licensing authority, approved credentials, managed signing keys or authenticated production reviewer has been fabricated or represented as live. The caller must establish that the verifier is an independently authorized regulator/licensing source; treating a local fixture or a generic 420Verify software verification as a retail license is prohibited. Authentication of actual issuer assertions, legal coverage, availability SLAs, operator access and deployed live revocation feeds remain integration/testnet/external acceptance gates.

Level 1: focused fail-closed tests (revoked, expired, fresh/stale, unavailable, cancelled, mismatched issuer/evidence, no ALLOW) under Go race detector, plus retained Compliance application checks. Level 2 at canonical C03 milestone; Level 3 at C08.5.
