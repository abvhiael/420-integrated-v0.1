# C03.7 — Bound decision receipts and stage-specific replay protection

Canonical step: **Bound decision receipts and replay prevention; define expiry, decision input commitments and action-stage re-evaluation.**

`ReceiptLedger.Issue` accepts only a fresh `ALLOW` decision calculated *internally* by the existing independently gated `EvaluationService`, not a caller-supplied ALLOW claim. A receipt binds tenant, operation, stage, action, audience, jurisdiction, facts digest, canonical request digest, approved policy digest, issue instant and expiration. A caller-supplied nonce contributes to the receipt ID, and reuse of a nonce for an operation is denied. TTL is strictly positive and capped at five minutes, and legal policy expiry further caps the receipt.

`ConsumeAndReevaluate` checks that the receipt was issued by the local ledger, its complete contents have not changed, it is not expired, the original input matches the receipt's cryptographic commitment, and the operation/tenant/domain/audience/jurisdiction remain unchanged while the stage changes. The receipt is consumed atomically even when a new independent evaluation fails; unavailable authorities never preserve a reusable entitlement. Concurrent double-spend tests verify at-most-once consumption.

**Trust limitation:** Receipt IDs and a process-local store do not provide production multi-instance replay protection. A deployed design must use one durable transactional nonce/receipt ledger across instances, with authenticated signing and current policy/revocation validation. No bearer entitlement, blockchain transaction authorization, or live retailer licence is established by this component. Stage ordering beyond "different stage" and independent source-of-truth operation state must be enforced by the transaction workflow at integration acceptance. C03.8 addresses multi-instance publication/invalidation. Level 2 remains scheduled for the C03 milestone, Level 3 for C08.5.
