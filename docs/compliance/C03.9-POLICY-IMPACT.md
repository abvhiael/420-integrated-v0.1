# C03.9 — Policy simulation and impact reports

`SimulatePolicyImpact` is a bounded read-only classifier (maximum 10,000 flows) over before/after policy evaluation outcomes from the same independently established pair of policy digests. It sorts by flow ID for deterministic reports, rejects duplicate flows, unsupported decisions, mixed policy digests and incomplete contexts. Categories: `NEWLY_BLOCKED`, `NEWLY_ALLOWED_CANDIDATE`, `CHANGED_REVIEW_REQUIRED`, `UNCHANGED`, and `IN_FLIGHT_REEVALUATION_REQUIRED`. Every in-flight operation is explicitly marked for fresh action-stage reevaluation; report output is never transaction authorization.

**Trust boundary:** The production-oriented `SimulateReviewedPolicyImpact` obtains before/after outcomes from the existing independently gated `EvaluationService` for identical request facts and reviewed policy snapshots; the lower-level classifier accepts caller-supplied outcomes and is not itself a trusted ingestion boundary. The simulation API does not authenticate the requesting operator. A production caller must evaluate identical authoritative facts against two reviewed policy snapshots with authenticated source provenance and retain the input commitments. In-flight results cannot be acted on until C03.7 stage-specific fresh reevaluation. Any newly allowed flow is only a candidate, never a legal approval. This is deterministic simulation infrastructure, not live regulatory permission. C03.9 Level 1 includes bounded adversarial input, mixed-policy, duplicate-flow and deterministic ordering tests; Level 2 is deferred to the C03 integration milestone, Level 3 to C08.5.
