# C04.1 — Controlled policy lifecycle

Canonical sequence: draft → independently reviewed → approved → scheduled → active → superseded or revoked. Scheduled policies may be revoked before activation. Superseded/revoked policies are terminal and must not be reactivated.

`PolicyLifecycle` applies revision-fenced transitions to a single policy digest. Author, independent reviewer, separate approver and publisher must have distinct actor identities. The review digest must equal the draft's policy digest; an unreviewed or differently hashed policy cannot be approved. Scheduled activation is prohibited before the specified time; publication without an independent review is not permitted. Each permitted transition produces a snapshot event with actor, time and new revision. Concurrent same-revision transitions cannot both win. Invalid transitions do not modify the state or event history.

This process-local state machine is **not** a production policy registry, identity source or legal authorization. Actor identities must originate from authenticated roles and externally authorized reviewers. Production publication must use the independently signed C03.8 publication authority and durable PostgreSQL monotonic fences with atomic state changes; lifecycle ACTIVE does not authorize regulated operations by itself. Durable audit-event storage, cross-instance concurrency, reliable scheduler execution, independent reviewer appointment, approvals and regulator/legal verification remain deployment/integration acceptance requirements. No external legal authority is implied by fixtures.

`TestC041PolicyLifecycleSeparationAndReplay` tests authorship/review conflicts, approval/publishing separation, altered digest, premature activation, terminal supersession/revocation, full event revisions and 32 concurrent review attempts. The existing Compliance `go test` and `go test -race` gates cover this test, in addition to C01–C03 retained qualification. C04.1 is Level 1; the broader phase-level integration milestone and C08.5 Level 3 remain deferred under the canonical roadmap.
