# C04.10 — Approved policy corrections, lawful rollback and historical error evidence

Canonical requirement: *Approved policy corrections and supersession; rollback may restore only a currently lawful, valid approved version. Preserve the historical error record.*

`PlanCorrection` is a fail-closed correction proposal, not an unconditional rollback. Any correction or restoration is **published as a new strictly increasing sequence** and monotonic revocation epoch, with current legal verification, independent reviewed source and review digests, scope verification, non-expired approval and author/reviewer/publisher separation. Restoring previous content never restores expired authorization, prior sequence or a revoked epoch.

The planner preserves the old publication fence and new fence plus a SHA-256 digest binding the old fence, error explanation and error evidence. It never rewrites the historical error state. `ApplyCorrection` re-checks the original and next fences, requires the existing `IndependentPublicationAuthority.VerifyPublication` boundary and uses `AdvancePublication` for atomic strong-store compare-and-swap. An unavailable authority or stale concurrent publication fails closed. It does not issue operational ALLOW.

`TestC0410CorrectionSupersessionAndHistory` covers lawful newly reviewed restoration, failed current-law attestation, revoked/expired review, self-review, scope mismatch, unchanged/reversed sequence, epoch rollback, missing error history, independent denial, unavailable store, CAS replay and cancelled requests.

**Operational limitation:** The source-level history digest is evidence *of the supplied historical record*, not itself durable archival. An authenticated reviewer source, actual current-law/expiry validation, signed immutable original evidence and an append-only persistent correction audit are required for deployed acceptance; test fixtures do not claim legal validity. Source-independent review and KMS/identity provider work remain testnet and production dependencies. The existing publication fence and independent signed publication service must not be bypassed by downstream APIs.

**Handoff:** C09.3 independently validated live legal sources; C09.4 durable signed publication and append-only historical error ledger; C09.7 rollback/revocation and concurrency adversarial tests; C09.9 independent legal correction interpretation; C10.1 recheck current legal validity, C10.3 managed production key acceptance, C10.7 formal release. The broader app **Level 2** boundary is the canonical **C04.11**, and **Level 3** remains at **C08.5**.
