# C04.2 — Reviewer qualification, delegation, MFA and approval invalidation

Canonical step: Reviewer qualifications and delegation; MFA, least privilege, short-lived sessions and approval invalidation when reviewed content changes.

`AuthorizeReview` checks a grant and session retrieved separately from a `ReviewerAuthority` adapter. An authenticated, qualified reviewer or approver must be individually identified, outside the author/publisher role, restricted to the precise domain and jurisdiction, and bound to the **exact reviewed policy digest**. Approval of a different document digest is denied rather than carried over. The external authority must revoke grants when delegation or qualifying credentials change.

Sessions are limited to fifteen minutes, the MFA proof must be no older than five minutes, and all grants, qualifications, sessions and MFA assertions must be valid strictly at evaluation time. No long-lived capability, client-supplied role, missing MFA, self-authorization, cross-scope delegation, cancelled context or unavailable authority can yield positive validation. ValidUntil is the earliest relevant expiry.

**Security boundary:** No independently accredited regulator, identity issuer, active reviewer credential, actual MFA authenticator or production session service is provisioned by these fixtures. The adapter is an explicit interface, *not* an implementation of identity verification or independent legal appointment. In particular, the earlier process-local `PolicyLifecycle.Transition` accepts a trusted actor identity but does not itself call this verifier. Production review/approval endpoints must enforce this fresh authorization before applying transitions and use a durable revision-fenced state store. Until then, C04.2's checks are software-component qualification only, not complete production authorization.

The targeted `TestC042ReviewerQualificationDelegationMFAAndInvalidation`, retained Compliance Go security suite and Go race tests are owned by the app-specific exact-SHA workflow. C04.2 is Level 1; milestone and global closeout are deferred according to the roadmap.
