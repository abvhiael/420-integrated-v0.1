# C04.3 — Federal product and payment-route review boundary

Canonical roadmap: federally applicable product, packaging, quantity-equivalency and promotion requirements; explicitly separate non-medical scope and payment-route legal review.

The code implements a **fail-closed, read-only assessment of externally reviewed candidate evidence**, not a claim that a production-ready federal cannabis law pack is encoded. `AssessFederalProduct` accepts a time-bounded policy identified by policy, source and independent review digests and a non-medical-only product. Exact integer rational equivalency inputs avoid decimal rounding or overflow. Unknown classes, unreviewed or stale policies, missing packaging verification, promotional material prohibited by the externally reviewed candidate, medical claims and unreviewed payment routes require review. The result never authorizes product sale, promotion, delivery or payment. Federal requirements and specific quantitative limits must be sourced, versioned, legally interpreted and independently signed; developer fixtures are not legal guidance.

**External legal acceptance:** C09.3 approved official source collection; C09.9 independent interpretation and federal product/category/packaging/equivalency/promotion case acceptance; C10.1 refreshed jurisdiction counsel review; C10.4 independent payment-rail, processor, bank, card/crypto settlement route legal and partner acceptance. Medical authorizations remain a separately scoped legal system, not silently permitted by recreational/non-medical acceptance.

Retained security tests cover stale/unapproved digests, unknown and invalid equivalency, overflow, medical crossover, packaging, promotions, absent payment review and denied/review-only outcomes. The step is Level 1 code qualification; completion of reviewed real legal rules and production entitlement is **not** implied by CI.
