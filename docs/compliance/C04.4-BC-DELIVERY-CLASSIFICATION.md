# C04.4 — BC delivery classification and authoritative trace matrix

**Canonical requirement:** separate licensee/employee, delivery-person and common-carrier branches; map implemented rules to current authoritative provisions and tests. **Coverage:** non-medical cannabis sold by a BC-authorized retail store only. This is an engineering candidate, not permission to deliver.

**Important distinctions:** a common carrier is not a *delivery person* as defined in CLR section 1. Selling It Right cannot automatically be imposed as a substitute carrier licence; a licensed retailer's permissions cannot be assigned to an independent courier; and a scoped exemption in section 5.1 does not waive the applicable delivery conditions. Local and Indigenous Nation limits, licence conditions, credential provenance, and the actual legal classification of DOOBr are **not determined** from these Boolean test inputs. `EvaluateBCDeliveryMatrix` returns `CANDIDATE_REVIEW_REQUIRED` for internally consistent evidence and **never ALLOW**. Failed deliveries cannot become completed deliveries.
