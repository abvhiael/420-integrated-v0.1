# C04.5 — Vancouver retailer versus DOOBr business classification

**Canonical step:** Vancouver retailer/business permissions and applicable municipal operating restrictions; confirm DOOBr's own business category rather than treating retailer licensing as courier authorization.

Current official Vancouver guidance distinguishes: (1) approved retail cannabis store location/zoning and separation restrictions; (2) municipal development permit; (3) provincial cannabis retail licence; (4) Vancouver municipal retail business licence. Vancouver also requires businesses operating in the city, including out-of-town operators, to determine their own municipal licensing obligations. These retailer rights are **not transferable** to DOOBr.

**Legal and operational boundary:** This implements **fail-closed evidence acceptance**, not a hardcoded claim about the legally proper Vancouver business licence for DOOBr. Zoning locations, precise setback exemptions, local store restrictions, retailer status, Indigenous jurisdiction, and the required independent DOOBr category must be confirmed from current officials and permit/licence records. A category must never be guessed from the cannabis retailer licence. The model always returns REVIEW_REQUIRED or CANDIDATE_REVIEW_REQUIRED, never live ALLOW.
