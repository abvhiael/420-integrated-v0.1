# C04.6 — BC delivery stages, records and returns

C04.6 implements a read-only, fail-closed BC delivery-journey candidate: retailer/order/packing origin, capture of prepayment before dispatch, secure custody/transport, eligible recipient and signed handover, refusal or absence, return-to-origin and records. Every candidate is cross-checked with the three separate BC classes in C04.4. None of these checks yields an operational ALLOW.

**Rule trace / tests**

| Stage | Rule evidence | Test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Retailer-origin and same-store manifest | BC Cannabis Licensing Regulation s.5(2.1), BC retail handbook Part 4 Division 5 | TestC046BCDeliveryJourneyAdversarial: wrong origin |
| Payment complete before dispatch | BC government delivering cannabis guidance, retail handbook | Missing payment |
| Secure transport and chain-of-custody records | CLR s.5(2.1), retail handbook | Missing custody, insecure transport |
| Eligible adult recipient, name/signature | CLR s.5(2.1)(d)-(e) | Missing age, recipient record, signature |
| Failed/refused delivery returned to origin, no delivery completion | BC retail handbook Part 4 Division 5 | Refusal/recipient absent, missing return, same-day non-carrier |
| Purchase/invoice/recipient/delivery/refusal/return records | BC retail handbook Part 4 Division 5 | Missing invoice/recipient, return evidence |
| Carrier-class-specific handover and transport | CLR s.1, s.5(2.1), s.5.1; C04.4 | Unknown carrier, three-class matrix |

Sources for independent review: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/202_2018 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/employment-business/business/liquor-regulation-licensing/cannabis-licences/cannabis-resources-information/delivering-cannabis . These are references, not proof of independent legal signoff.

The implementation is a deterministic testable adapter using externally verified assertions, not durable delivery event storage or a licensed DOOBr operator. Source terms, classification-specific exemptions, same-day return calculation, refusal/record retention and legally mandated provenance must be independently interpreted and tested with real producer/provider records. C09.3/C09.5/C09.6/C09.7/C09.9 testnet and C10.1/C10.2/C10.6/C10.7 production gates remain unmet until actual qualified acceptance. C08.5 Level 3 remains deferred.
