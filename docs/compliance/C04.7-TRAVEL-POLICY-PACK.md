# C04.7 — Travel possession, transport, consumption and venue candidate policy pack

Canonical requirement: travel policy pack for possession/transport/consumption and venue guidance **within validated coverage**; border crossings and unreviewed places remain explicit gaps.

`EvaluateTravelGuidance` implements a non-authorizing decision boundary for externally reviewed travel facts. The domain/jurisdiction, reviewed policy/source/review digests, validity window, recognized activity, validated physical location and venue, adult credential, quantity unit and integer-bound maximum must all match. Transport requires documented secure vehicle storage; consumption while driving or in unreviewed public places is not interpreted as lawful. Any border crossing or overlapping unreviewed Indigenous jurisdiction is explicitly REVIEW_REQUIRED. Unsupported cities, private venues, country borders, provinces, licence or land-use conditions are never inherited from a BC example.

**Authoritative source candidates for independent reviewer validation**: federal Cannabis Act (public possession, cross-border import/export), https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-24.5/ ; BC Cannabis Control and Licensing Act, https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/18029 ; BC government public consumption and motor vehicle information, https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/safety/public-safety/cannabis ; applicable municipal/park/venue and Indigenous local requirements must be identified individually. The fixture quantity is **not** a claim about the actual permitted legal maximum; actual thresholds, legal text, venue restrictions, current age exceptions and activity classifications require approved source-specific interpretation.

Test `TestC047TravelCoverageBoundaries` checks all three activities, border crossing, Indigenous unreviewed territory, unsupported place/venue, public consumption, driver operation, unsecured transport, minor, quantity unit mismatch, invalid/excess quantities, unreviewed activity and expired policy. No result ever confers authorization.
