# C04.9 — Activation impact and cached guidance invalidation

Canonical requirement: activation impact procedure for pending orders and cached guidance; **stop/reassess/return belongs to DOOBr**; preserve immutable previous decision evidence.

`AssessActivationImpact` reads the current authoritative publication fence (strongly consistent store), rejects unobserved / stale publication transitions, invalidates Compliance's local policy hint cache, and generates non-authorizing impact instructions for existing orders. Pending and preparing orders get `STOP_AND_REASSESS`; in-transit, refused and failed-delivery orders get `STOP_REASSESS_RETURN_REVIEW`. No Compliance path dispatches a courier, changes the DOOBr order state, approves an order or automatically returns goods.

The prior immutable receipt is represented by a cryptographic digest of the complete prior receipt, together with original and replacement policy digests and monotonic sequence identifiers. It cannot be silently overwritten by the new policy. Missing, altered, duplicated, mismatched, delivered or unsupported orders fail closed. Cached guidance is invalidated on policy activation and cannot be substituted for authoritative publication reads.

The workflow `TestC049ActivationImpactAndPriorEvidence` verifies all five live/pending order cases, original policy evidence integrity, duplicate rejection, stale publishing, store/cache unavailability and context cancellation.

**Production boundary:** This is a pure software impact planner. Retaining a hash alone does not durably archive original decision evidence. Real DOOBr integration must transactionally preserve signed original receipts in an immutable or append-only durable store, atomically suspend order progression at every stage, reconcile cancellation/reassessment/return and support replay/recovery across instances. Until done, a CI PASS does not establish operational safety.
