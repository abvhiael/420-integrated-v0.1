# C05.10 — Pay/Wallet workflow reference authority split

Canonical requirement: **Pay/Wallet integration boundary: decision references bind to permitted workflows; Compliance does not sign payments, grant custody or independently refund.**

`BindPaymentWorkflowReference` returns only a scoped, non-executable decision reference for an independently authenticated Pay/Wallet consumer. It binds tenant, order, audience, workflow, stage, facts digest, receipt ID, policy digest and expiry. Permitted workflows are `PAY_PREAUTH`, `PAY_CAPTURE` and `WALLET_PRESENT` only, with an exact receipt-action match and fresh independent policy check. References cannot be switched between order, tenant, audience, action or stages, reused after expiry, or changed into refunds. No signing, transfers, key custody, balance changes, payment capture execution or refund API exists here. Pay/Wallet own credentials, payment authorization, replay controls, settlement, and separately reviewed refunds.

`TestC0510PayWalletBoundary` exercises stage, workflow and audience substitution, cross-tenant/order references, stale receipts, facts mismatch, missing policy, refund denial, provider failure and missing authority. The targeted Compliance Level 1 workflow includes these checks under Go race detection alongside retained app-specific regressions.

**Integration requirements remain open:** actual Pay and Wallet consumer code, durable once-only receipt consumption, current external Pay authorization, signed Wallet consent and independently reviewed refund workflows must be deployed and adversarially verified in C09/C10. The injected authority mock is not a live service. Level 2 C05.12; Level 3 C08.5.
