# C05.11 — On-chain publication and attestation suitability decision

**Canonical roadmap:** Optional on-chain publication/attestation adapter only after demonstrated need; reuse Registry/Verify/governance capabilities before considering new contracts.

**Disposition: REVIEW COMPLETE — NO NEW ON-CHAIN ADAPTER OR CONTRACT JUSTIFIED AT C05.11.**

## Existing authority and gaps

- **Compliance** C02–C04 tracks official sources, independent legal interpretation, authorized reviewed publication, effective windows, supersession/revocation, bound receipts and tenant-scoped decisions. Publication is off-chain and its authorization is not conferred by the chain.
- **420Registry** presents canonical `ProtocolRegistry` service/version discovery and historical registration commitments. Registering a service does not certify a statute, an operational licence or a Compliance ALLOW decision.
- **420Verify** compares deployed source/build with bytecode and preserves verification evidence. Bytecode verification neither approves legal interpretations nor certifies real-world authority.
- **420Governance / 420 Civic** owns its own canonical protocol decisions and Timelock-governed actions, not provincial/municipal legal review, licence validity, or the ability to override a stale/denied Compliance evaluation.
- **420Notifications** carries signed invalidation impact events, and consumers must re-read canonical policy. An event digest or chain attestation cannot restart an expired grant.
- **420Oracle** C05.8 concluded that ordinary regulatory source monitoring does not warrant an Oracle feed. Neither Oracle reporting nor on-chain attestations create legal publication authority.

| Candidate purpose | Decision | Existing fit / constraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Read-only discovery of a deployed Compliance service/version | Reuse Registry **only if deployed and needed** | Registry version discovery is not policy approval |
| Source/build provenance for a future deployed adapter | Reuse Verify **only if deployed** | Verifies code correspondence, not legal correctness |
| Protocol configuration requiring chain-authorized action | Use canonical Governance/Timelock | Never authorize legal interpretations from protocol votes |
| Timestamp/hash of a public, independently approved policy | **Defer pending demonstrable external requirement** | Off-chain signed publication and source provenance already preserve the required audit trail; a hash alone cannot prove text is lawful/current |
| Raw regulation updates, licence revocation, private evidence or customer data | **Do not put on chain** | Privacy, correction, expiry and independent issuer authority must remain off-chain |
| Smart-contract gating that cannot use the existing interfaces | **Not demonstrated** | Would require explicit consumer, threat model, on-chain necessity, retention/privacy analysis, new audit, governance approval and integration evidence |

No canonical C05.1–C05.10 consumer currently demonstrates a missing chain-enforced operation. Accordingly C05.11 introduces **no Solidity contract, interface, ABI change, fixed Genesis address, namespace reservation, Registry registration, on-chain legal claim, deploy script, or optional adapter stub**. This is an affirmative architecture/security decision, not omitted functionality.

## Required safeguards for any later adapter

A future separately authorized integration must establish a concrete on-chain consumer and necessity; reuse current `ProtocolRegistry`/Verify/Governance rather than duplicate authority; bind domain-separated policy digest, publication sequence, chain/network, issuer and expiry; prevent replay and stale attestations; omit non-public text, credentials, addresses and identifiable order data; and preserve independently reviewed publication/revocation off-chain as the legal source of truth. Neither attestation nor verification may change DENY/REVIEW_REQUIRED into ALLOW. An expired, revoked, absent or contradictory publication must fail closed.

Any approved future implementation requires its own new executable SHA and targeted/affected contract tests, ABI/address checks, adversarial tests, documented source authority, testnet deployment/config proof and external legal release acceptance. Its gate is C09.2 and C09.5/C09.9, with C10 release approval. This review alone authorizes no deployment.
