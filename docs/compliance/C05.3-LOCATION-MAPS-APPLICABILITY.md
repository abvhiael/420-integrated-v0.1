# C05.3 — Location/Maps applicability and private evidence boundary

Canonical C05.3 requires a trusted Location/Maps applicability adapter, privately held exact-address evidence only when needed, a coarse public region projection, and no GPS-only conclusion of legal eligibility.

`LocationApplicabilityAdapter` resolves separately authorized tenant/operation evidence through a configured `TrustedLocationResolver`. It rejects unavailable, expired, cross-tenant, mismatched-operation, unreviewed-jurisdiction and unauthorized place-association evidence. Exact-address stages additionally demand an authorized place ID, validated address and private address reference. Public-safe result contains **only** a coarse region and non-authorizing `CANDIDATE_REVIEW_REQUIRED`. Coordinates, address IDs and operation references must not enter public Maps/guidance payloads. The software refuses any `GPSOnly` claim, even when coordinates appear precise.

`TestC053LocationMapsPrivacyAndAuthority` covers matched private place evidence, projection serialization, GPS-only denial, tenant/order crossover, withdrawn authorization, stale sources, missing exact address or place, unavailable resolver and optional region-only cases.

**External acceptance remains open:** The injected resolver is a trust boundary and fixture, not proof of authorized 420Location place associations, 420Maps private access control, real location classification or current jurisdiction law. A live authenticated resolver, durable scope-specific authorization and revocation feed, privacy-safe map markers, cross-service adversarial requests, and independent legal boundary checks belong to C09.5/C09.7/C09.8/C09.9 and C10.6/C10.7. This step is Level 1, with Level 2 at C05.12 and Level 3 at C08.5.
