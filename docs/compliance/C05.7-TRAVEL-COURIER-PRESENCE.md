# C05.7 — Travel policy visibility, legal eligibility and qualified courier supply

C05.7 separates three independent dimensions: **policy coverage** (REVIEWED / PARTIAL / UNKNOWN), **legal eligibility** (UNVERIFIED in the public interface, never inferred from coverage), and **actual courier supply** (AVAILABLE only from a qualified, current, nonrevoked external provider whose private cohort meets a configured privacy minimum). Travel presence is informational, not an offer, checkout, location verification or grant of legal delivery eligibility.

`TravelPresenceAdapter` composes the source-backed C05.6 Travel TRANSPORT policy projection with `CourierSupplySource`. It exposes region, policy coverage/version, unverified legal eligibility, aggregate visibility and expiry; it never serializes provider ID, courier count, private addresses, GPS, recipient identity, courier details or DOOBr checkout routes. Partial, expired or unknown policy never produces public supply visibility. Unqualified, revoked, unavailable, stale, expired, mismatched-region or small-cohort supply is suppressed. A failed source returns no visibility. The expiry bound is the earlier of the policy's effective-until and the provider supply expiry.

**Targeted Level 1:** `TestC057TravelPresencePrivacyExpiryAndSeparation` checks distinct policy/legal/supply outputs, no private data in JSON, source outage fail-closed, privacy threshold, policy expiry/partial coverage, source freshness, provider revocation, region mismatch and unavailable supply. The same exact-SHA Compliance suite retains C05.6 guidance/privacy and C05.3 Maps controls.

**External dependencies:** Current policy/legal approval, actual certified DOOBr courier provider availability, multi-instance revocation, privacy aggregation and live 420Travel consumer integration are not established by synthetic test providers. C09.5/C09.7/C09.8/C09.9 and production C10 qualify those integrations. Level 2 stays C05.12, Level 3 C08.5.
