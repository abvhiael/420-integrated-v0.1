# C05.9 — Signed Notifications outbox and canonical-policy invalidation

Canonical requirement: Notifications outbox, subscribed app impact events, signatures, ordering, deduplication, retry and dead-letter recovery. Events invalidate permission; consumers re-read canonical policy.

Implemented `ComplianceImpactEvent` carries only scope, policy digest, ordered publication sequence, revocation epoch, event identifier, issue time and signing key identifier; ed25519 signatures cover the full serialized event. There is no order, recipient, address or credential payload. `PublishComplianceImpact` validates signatures and bounded unique subscriber identifiers; the `ComplianceOutboxStore` contract requires atomic persistence with the related publication, monotonic sequence and unique event IDs, per-subscriber oldest-first leasing, and CAS completion. `DeliverComplianceImpact` retries with bounded backoff and records terminal dead-letter state. `ConsumeComplianceImpact` verifies signatures then re-reads canonical current publication and fails on missing, stale or contradictory sequences, policy digests or revocation epochs. **No event grants ALLOW, policy approval or payment authority.**

The store and sender are interfaces rather than live deployments. Durable transactional publisher+outbox implementation, 420Notifications partner subscriptions and delivery receipts, restart recovery, lease fencing, replay/deduplication at consumer boundaries, actual key custody and rotation, and production dead-letter replay authorization require separately qualified C09.5/C09.7/C09.8/C10 integration. They must not be represented as deployed. CI tests include signatures/forgery, duplicate event/subscription, canonical re-read, stale/failed source, retries, terminal dead-letter and acknowledged duplicate rejection. Level 2 is C05.12 and Level 3 C08.5.
