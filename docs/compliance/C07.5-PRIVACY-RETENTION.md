# C07.5 — Privacy retention, deletion, legal holds and evidence access

Canonical requirement: privacy retention/deletion schedules, legal holds and role-limited evidence access; required delivery records remain under the accountable retailer/app boundary. Repository qualification is Level 1; C07.10 remains the operational Level 2 milestone and C08.5 remains phase Level 3.

## Scope and ownership

The implementation manages the existing Compliance database's private draft/review groups (DRAFT), auditor access records (AUDIT), and completed/dead durable jobs (JOB). It neither copies nor controls retailer delivery/custody/order records, Pay/Wallet records, raw identity images, precise locations or another application's required records. Unsupported owner classes are rejected. C02.9 original-source export/licensing and the accountable retailer/app retention duties remain separate. Public policy/finality/activation receipts and canonical authority maps are not erased by these operations.

## Retention policy and scheduling

There is no adopted legal duration or positive default. Each scope/class requires a versioned configuration signed independently by its data owner and privacy reviewer, with distinct principals and keys, exact scope/class/version/duration/approval digest and expiry/revocation. The 1–3650 day interval is a software configuration bound, not a legal minimum/maximum. Qualification uses a one-day synthetic policy only; real periods, source approval and owner/reviewer keys remain external C09/C10 acceptance. Missing, expired or latest-revoked policy prevents purge; an older approval is never substituted. Updates cannot shorten earlier configured retention, and must carry a greater version and two fresh verified signatures.

RunRetention is a bounded/resumable scheduler entry: Due returns at most 32 scoped references; each purge needs a fresh exact-reference owner-finality grant. A scan grant cannot authorize deletion. Partial batches return a committed count and error; each successful deletion has its own atomic durable receipt, and tombstones prevent rediscovery/reuse. No browser cleanup endpoint or automatically authorized cron is created.

Retention clocks originate from PostgreSQL, not caller dates. Job completion/dead-letter and audit insertion receive server-clock records. Legacy audit enrollment begins conservatively at approved policy installation. Drafts require a separate authenticated owner retirement, a matching head revision and a known locked canonical publication fence with no live policy; retirement permanently freezes further author/review edits. Draft retention uses the latest version/review or retirement time, whichever is later. Pending jobs, active/unknown publication state, unretired drafts and uncertain owner finality cannot be purged.

## Legal holds and least privilege

A separate hold actor can place record or scope-wide holds; holds do not time out. Release requires its own capability, a different principal, the exact case/reference and a matching revision. Release replay, self-release, wrong case/scope and unsupported types fail closed. Scope advisory locks serialize hold, release, retirement and purge. Holds block scans and all purge attempts; a deletion already committed cannot later be represented as retained held evidence. Explicit hold placement is not source/legal-policy approval.

The existing app runtime keeps append-only privileges: no broad DELETE, TRUNCATE, history UPDATE or privacy-function execution is granted. Dedicated preprovisioned privacy roles receive only one operation family, schema usage, migration-ledger read and an own-identity probe. Their role/tenant/domain/jurisdiction/principal/capability/expiry are immutable within an active binding; database role and independently verified live grant must agree. Mutation grants bind operation, exact record/case/revision and, for retirement/deletion, owner finality. Grants are reverified after mutation but before commit; revocation rolls back data and accounting. Real roles, credentials, non-login function ownership with narrowly managed cross-RLS execution authority, IAM and issuer/case mandates must be provisioned and independently accepted in C09/C10; this repository step creates no live access.

Migration eight appends six RLS-protected privacy tables and narrowly exposed SECURITY DEFINER functions with fixed search_path, explicit session-user identity, tenant filters and PUBLIC execution revocation. It preserves the first seven migration constants/checksums. The migration/function administrator is trusted and must have the required migration/cross-RLS authority; this is not an ordinary runtime credential. Privacy bindings refuse superuser, RLS-bypass, role-membership, role-creation or pre-existing app-data privileges. Regular production runtime verification explicitly rejects privacy mutation/scan privileges.

## Actual deletion and evidence access

Purge removes original owned encrypted rows from the primary database, not just a visibility flag. Draft/review versions and their heads are removed together after retirement; auditor rows and terminal job rows use their own class clocks. Data removal, quota decrement and a minimal private receipt commit atomically. Receipt failure, missing accounting, role/policy expiry, legal hold, lock timeout or database/identity outage rolls everything back. Replays observe one receipt and do not decrement quota twice. Receipts hold only private opaque reference, class, policy version, actor, time and aggregate removed row/byte counts; they contain no source text, ciphertext, token, ID image, order or customer facts. Opaque references are not claimed to be anonymous and never enter public/on-chain output. Journals and inventory are capped at 100,000 rows per scope; exhaustion fails closed and requires separately accepted archival/operations planning.

NewC075AuditorService wires C075AuditorRepository into the existing authenticated/redacted auditor service. Encrypted production logs reject an ungated repository. Current scoped policy, original independent auditor/case mandate, record existence, deletion state and legal-hold case/purpose are checked before/after lookup and after durable logging. A holding legal case can inspect retained redacted metadata for legal/incident purposes; another case or source-verification purpose cannot use held evidence. Due unheld records and missing/expired/revoked policy are unavailable. Original bytes remain under the C02.9 owner. Caller scope/purpose never creates an access grant.

## Qualification and deferred acceptance

Mandatory tests cover independent policy signatures/ownership exclusions; real PostgreSQL retention/hold/retirement/deletion and 12-way races/idempotency; exact quota conservation and no reference reuse; case/purpose/role-limited exports and post-log hold races; mutation-time revocation/receipt rollback; latest-policy revocation; bounded scheduling/server clocks; and managed mTLS + restricted verified-TLS PostgreSQL composition with outage/recovery. Retain C07.1–C07.4 and directly affected C03/C04/C05/C06 database, race, authorization, export, replay and failure tests, Go vet, pinned input/module integrity and vulnerability review. Missing/skipped required tests are not PASS.

Primary SQL deletion is not certified physical-media erasure: WAL, replicas, caches, source-owner copies, snapshots and encrypted backups need independent operational disposition. Backup/restore and tombstone replay are canonical C07.6/C09.8 work; restore must reconcile privacy receipts before exposing restored data. Full deployed IAM/case/issuer acceptance, actual retention-law/source review, delivery-record owner acceptance and production scheduler/backup/media behavior remain C09/C10. No live delivery/payment permission or legal retention certification is implied.
