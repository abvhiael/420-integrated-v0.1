# C07.6 — Recovery checkpoint (NOT COMPLETE)

Canonical C07.6: Backup/restore and disaster recovery with policy/publication consistency; stale backup must not reactivate a revoked policy. Ordinary Level 1; operational Level 2 C07.10; phase Level 3 C08.5.

## Implemented
An independently supplied latest-checkpoint comparison rejects mismatched scope, restore generation, policy digest, dependency digest, publication sequence, revocation epoch, expired fence, privacy receipt digest/count and authority failure. Adversarial unit tests cover stale policy/revocation, lost purge receipt, recovery generation mismatch, invalid expiry and external outage.

The comparison is a precondition, **not proof of safe restoration**. The independent authority must be authenticated, durable outside the restored failure domain, non-rollbackable and complete. The checkpoint must not be inferred from an old DB. No production authority or restore orchestration is wired.

## Blocking canonical work still required
- Protected encrypted and integrity-verified base backup/WAL, offsite manifest/custody, recovery key and loss testing.
- Independent, monotonic, durable publication/revocation and C07.5 tombstone journal with exact scope/identity, integrity, continuous recording and no trusted stale fallback.
- Fenced/quiesced restore: halt all serving, SDK, workers, dispatch and durable jobs, safely restore consistent primary, replay externally witnessed privacy deletions and revocations *before* opening any access; reconcile policy and publication; advance generations/leases and invalidate old cache/session/key state.
- Real PostgreSQL crash/PITR/partial/corrupt/stale restore and concurrent revocation/partition/outage tests; prove no resurrected deleted data, no unauthorized replay, no stale policy publication, and sound quota/hold state.
- Approved RPO/RTO and recovery runbooks, measured operator drills; physical backups/replicas/WAL/legal disposition and production acceptance remain later C09/C10 gates.
