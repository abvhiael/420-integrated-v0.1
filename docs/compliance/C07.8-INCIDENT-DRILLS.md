# 420Compliance C07.8 — incident drills and containment evidence

**Canonical step:** C07.8 — Incident drills for false approval, malicious sources, compromised reviewer/key, bad geography, expired credentials and regulator restriction.

**Scope:** repository-only Compliance security drills, Level 1. **Milestone:** C07.10 operational Level 2. **PR:** #602 (remain open and unmerged).

## Scenario / attack / expected containment / evidence

| Scenario | Attack or fault injected | Required security outcome | Automated drill |
|---|---|---|---|
| False approval | previously approved content after an independently advanced revocation epoch; attempted rollback | older approval not served or reactivated; corrected publication only | `TestC078IncidentDrills/false_approval_and_rollback` |
| Malicious sources | unreadable update, collection failure, missing source, stale retrieval, expired independent review, invalid review digest | no source-based authorization or review renewal; escalation to source owner and independent reviewer | `TestC078IncidentDrills/malicious_and_stale_source` |
| Compromised reviewer/key | revoked reviewer delegation or session, removed trusted publisher-signature key | review/publication denied; positive control only with independent current authority | `TestC078IncidentDrills/compromised_reviewer_and_publication_key` |
| Bad geography | unreviewed jurisdiction, forged cross-tenant place attribution | no candidate review progression | `TestC078IncidentDrills/bad_geography` |
| Expired credentials | expired reviewer grant or identity session | authorization denied without cached positive reuse | `TestC078IncidentDrills/expired_credential` |
| Regulator restriction | new revocation epoch, stale cached publication, epoch rollback, independent authority outage | halt stale publication; reject rollback; unavailable authority fails closed | `TestC078IncidentDrills/regulator_restriction` |

The test suite uses real repo authorization, source-health, location and publication-fencing functions, deliberately not production secrets, regulator endpoints or transaction dispatch. These exercises prove local code-path containment only. A simulated regulator restriction is not evidence that an external regulator issued or acknowledged a live restriction.

## Response runbook

1. **Detect and identify scope.** Treat source-health, evaluator, reviewer authority, geographic trust or regulator restriction alarms as signals for an operator. Establish environment, tenant, jurisdiction, policy digest, publisher generation and revocation epoch from trusted records. Do not accept incident reports themselves as a grant of authority.
2. **Contain immediately.** Block new risky decisions/publications or consumer service on untrusted state; revoke compromised sessions, delegation and signing keys through their designated independently authenticated authorities; invalidate stale publication caches via the monotonic revocation epoch. Preserve unrelated lawful cancellation/refund/recovery paths.
3. **Preserve evidence.** Retain scope, timestamp, authenticated reporter, incident ID, impact and revocation identifiers, last known publication, denied attempts, decision/outbox status and tamper-evident audit records under C07.5 access/retention restrictions. Never place PII, private documents, bearer tokens or secret keys in metrics/alerts.
4. **Escalate.** Notify designated security incident lead and independent legal/policy reviewer. A suspected official-source compromise goes to the source owner; geography to the approved location authority; credential compromise to the managed identity/key authority; regulator orders to the independently established regulator/legal contact. Do not manufacture external approvals or treat unacknowledged alerts as delivered.
5. **Recover only under renewed authority.** Acquire independently reviewed current source/policy evidence, restored credentials under externally authenticated control and a fresh signed publication with monotonic sequence/epoch. Verify downstream impact outbox and consumer acknowledgments before reporting operational clearance; monitoring health never authorizes serving.
6. **Validate and close.** Verify replay denial, cross-scope isolation, gate fencing, freshness and public-facing consumer denial/recovery. Record evidence SHA and CI test outcomes. Measure response/propagation against approved thresholds when available. Unapproved or unmeasured targets remain UNKNOWN, not PASS.

## Qualification and deployment boundaries

The Level 1 workflow `.github/workflows/420compliance-c01-level1.yml` must explicitly execute `TestC078IncidentDrills`, retained C07.7 instrumentation/source/alert/PostgreSQL tests and retained directly affected C07.6 authority and recovery tests with race detection and static/security checks. Its exact-SHA run, passing job and retained artifact are mandatory before this step may be marked complete. A workflow committed or queued does not count as PASS.

Real multi-instance response exercises, external source and regulator confirmations, actual key-broker rotation/revocation, on-call alert delivery and consumer propagation/acknowledgment drills require C07.10 Level 2 and C08.3/C09/C10 testnet/production acceptance as applicable. The separately documented C07.6 immutable externally deployed recovery authority/PITR acceptance remains deferred and does not imply production readiness.
