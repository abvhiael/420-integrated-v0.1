# C08.2 — DOOBr and 420Travel consumer integration guide

Consumer contracts are scoped to the existing `C051Client`, `C068ConsumerGateway` and C05 service adapters. No Compliance caller can mint policy authority, approve payment, custody, shipping or legal status.

**DOOBr:** obtain an independently authenticated tenant-scoped service credential. Before each regulated stage, bind tenant, order/operation, action, stage, facts and current policy digest. Only a current, matching, independently supported ALLOW may be considered; separate retailer, delivery-person/carrier, recipient, geographic, payment and stage authorizations remain required. On missing/expired/revoked evidence, changed policy, UNKNOWN/REVIEW_REQUIRED/DENY, consumer outage or stale receipt: STOP new stage progression; do not replay an old ALLOW. Preserve separately reviewed refusal, safe return, refund and custody record paths.

**420Travel/Maps:** guidance/status are informational. No public presence, region match, courier aggregate or policy text is an operational permission. Only publish privacy-safe coarse regions with qualifying minimum cohorts and still-current reviewed legal-source coverage; suppress presence on stale sources, issuer outages, ambiguity or supply failure. Do not expose identity, exact courier paths, private addresses or order state.

**Revocation and signed invalidation and acknowledgement:** verify canonical publisher public key, audience, scope, sequence, epoch, digest and expiry; reload current publication from independent source; record durable delivery/acknowledgement and prevent prior receipt replay. The C07.7 monitor observes lag but cannot independently authorize serving.

**Error contract:** every non-ALLOW, missing digest, mismatch, network or issuer error is fail-closed; never fall back to guessed law, GPS-only province, cached positive decision, an AI suggestion or a governance vote. Record redacted correlation IDs and case-scoped incident references only.

**Acceptance boundaries:** existing service fixtures and browser tests do not establish actual deployed DOOBr/Travel SDK/client acceptance. C09.5–C09.8 own isolated testnet issuer and real app integration, C10 owns production legal and partner sign-off.
