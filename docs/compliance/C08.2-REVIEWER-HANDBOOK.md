# C08.2 — Independent reviewer handbook

1. **Authenticate independently.** Use separately provisioned reviewer delegation, role, jurisdiction/domain and tenant scope, current qualification and MFA session; authors, approvers and publishers must remain distinct. Revalidate on every sensitive transition; never trust a browser-created identity assertion.
2. **Verify the legal source.** Locate signed/authenticated source provenance, immutable snapshot/digest, citation span, publisher/issuer, operative/repeal dates, jurisdiction and applicable Indigenous/municipal/BC/federal instruments. Missing, conflicting or inaccessible evidence is escalated, never filled by AI.
3. **Review the proposed policy.** Compare source snapshot, author revision, evaluator/policy schema, facts and credential requirements; inspect exact digest and deterministic impact. AI-generated proposals remain untrusted and non-authorizing.
4. **Resolve conflicts.** Explicitly reconcile reviewers' disagreements, newer drafts, stale revisions and downstream impacts. Do not overwrite historical signed review/denial evidence. Changed bytes invalidate earlier approval; never self-approve.
5. **Sign within mandate.** Qualified reviewer independently authorizes exact source, policy, revision and domain/jurisdiction digest. The separately authorized publisher may publish only with current independent evidence and monotonic epoch/fence. Browser controls cannot export private signing keys or grant emergency ALLOW.
6. **Escalate and contain.** On suspected malicious source, compromised reviewer/key, false approval, bad geography, expired qualification or regulator restriction, block new regulated activity, notify approved legal/security authority, preserve scoped evidence and consult C07.8 incident runbook.
7. **Archive safely.** Retain minimally necessary case-scoped decisions, immutable citations, access log and retention/hold obligations. Never include sensitive identities, exact addresses, tokens or raw evidence in public guidance or metrics.

**External gate:** actual reviewer legal appointment and current source/legal opinion remain NOT APPROVED; a completed software test or C07.9 intake is not independent legal/security review.
