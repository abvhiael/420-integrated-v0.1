## Entry and exit rules

- **Repository phase:** Continue C01.8 and downstream app work through C08.6 with qualified code, schemas, mocks, migration and runbook tests. C08.3 must retain missing external authority without fabricating credentials. C08.5 Level 3 is still mandatory at its phase boundary.
- **Testnet entry:** Approved isolated services, owners and deployment/change authority. Acquire real source/issuer/partner reviewer contacts and testnet-scoped service identity; absent approvals stay blockers, not synthetic PASS.
- **Testnet exit:** C09.10 independently retained exact deployment/config/policy evidence, genuine provider trust, deployed security and recovery proof. Testnet PASS is **not** regulated delivery permission.
- **Production exit:** C10.1–C10.7 separate legal, retailer/carrier, payment, privacy, signer, support and operational approvals, independently validated immediately before limited activation.
- **Failure posture:** `UNKNOWN`, `DENY` or `REVIEW_REQUIRED` on any missing or invalid mandatory authority. No production ALLOW by software CI or testnet presence alone; lawful separately reviewed safe returns stay scoped.
