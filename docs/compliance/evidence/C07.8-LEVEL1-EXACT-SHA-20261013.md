## Canonical six-scenario drill coverage

1. **False approval:** monotonic publication sequence and revocation epoch invalidate stale approval, deny rollback, accept only independently advanced current state.
2. **Malicious sources:** failed, missing, unreadable, stale, invalid digest and expired legal review source inputs deny authorization and require source-owner/independent-review escalation.
3. **Compromised reviewer/key:** revoked grants and sessions deny review; independently signed approval cannot be reused after trusted public key is withdrawn.
4. **Bad geography:** unreviewed jurisdiction and cross-tenant place attribution deny candidate progression.
5. **Expired credentials:** expired reviewer grant and session are rejected by authorization at the incident evaluation time.
6. **Regulator restriction:** revocation epoch invalidates older publication, attempted rollback is rejected, and independent-authority outage fails closed.

The automated drill is `TestC078IncidentDrills` with six named scenarios and additional malicious-source/credential subcases. The tests exercise existing production authorization, source, location, publication, and fencing code rather than bypassing it.
