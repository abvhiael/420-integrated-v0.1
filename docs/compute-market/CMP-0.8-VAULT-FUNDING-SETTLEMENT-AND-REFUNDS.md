# CMP-0.8 — Vault funding, verified settlement and payer-bound refunds

Status: **PROTOCOL SPECIFICATION ONLY**. No new executable ComputeMarket Vault adapter, deployment, funded CMP transaction, paid worker, settlement or dedicated contract tests are introduced. Authority: [frozen ComputeMarket V1 architecture](../420-COMPUTE-MARKET-V1-ARCHITECTURE.md), [CMP-0 overview](CMP-0-PROTOCOL-SPECIFICATION.md), [CMP-0.2 job schema](CMP-0.2-CANONICAL-COMPUTE-JOB-SCHEMA.md), [CMP-0.3 units](CMP-0.3-DETERMINISTIC-WORK-UNIT-IDENTITY.md), [CMP-0.4 manifest](CMP-0.4-SIGNED-EXECUTION-MANIFEST.md), [CMP-0.5 identities](CMP-0.5-PROVIDER-NODE-RESOURCE-IDENTITY.md), [CMP-0.6 matching](CMP-0.6-OFFERS-REQUESTS-AND-MATCHING.md), and [CMP-0.7 lifecycle](CMP-0.7-AUTHORIZED-JOB-LIFECYCLE.md). CMP remains provider-neutral and registry-resolved after deployment, code-hash verification and authorized publication; it creates no fixed Genesis predeploy or new unrestricted escrow.

## 1. Verified source boundaries — what the existing Vault actually does

The checked-in [`AssetVault420.sol`](https://github.com/abvhiael/420-integrated-v0.1/blob/feature/cmp-0-protocol-specification/contracts/src/vault/AssetVault420.sol) supports `depositNative`, `createObligation`, `releaseObligation`, `cancelObligation`, `claim` and `withdraw`, each with its own state, authorization and replay rules. [`VaultAccounting420.sol`](https://github.com/abvhiael/420-integrated-v0.1/blob/feature/cmp-0-protocol-specification/contracts/src/vault/VaultAccounting420.sol) records aggregate `recordedBalance`, `reserved`, `claimable`, `released` and an immutable obligation `(vaultId, asset, beneficiary, amount, obligationType, sourceRef)` once created. `depositNative()` credits **vault-wide** accounting, not a caller-specific payer deposit ledger. `createObligation` takes an authorized caller and reserves against the **vault-wide free balance**: it does not prove which payer supplied that amount. `releaseObligation` changes a pending obligation into a claimable obligation for its *already recorded* beneficiary; it is **not** an external transfer. `claim` sends the entire claimable amount to that recorded beneficiary. `cancelObligation` returns a pending amount to **vault-wide internal free balance**, without crediting the original payer or transferring to the payer's wallet. `withdraw` draws against vault-wide free balance with self or asset/recipient/action route authorization; a generic withdrawal right cannot, by itself, prove ownership of a cancelled CMP job's balance.

Consequently a shared pooled Vault with the present accounting interface is **not sufficient on its own** for payer attribution and partial per-job earning/refund: it lacks a per-payer deposit balance, beneficiary change, obligation splitting and a payer-specific cancelled-obligation credit. Do not claim that a successful `depositNative`, `cancelObligation`, `releaseObligation` or `withdraw` proves the complete CMP economics. An approved CMP-specific adapter must establish segregated payer-backed credits and enforce every liability below with auditable state and atomic authorized routing, or use independently deployed, verified and registered payer-isolated Vault instances and still prove claim/refund isolation. Any needed Vault primitive or adapter extension is a future audited implementation decision, not a feature asserted to exist. Avoid using a single provider-beneficiary obligation to represent the entire deposit before correctness is established: its unconditional release would make unearned funds claimable.

## 2. Canonical payer and obligation model

Use native $420 (`address(0)`) at Genesis. At requester-authorized funding, bind `chainId`, verified Vault registry/vault/adapter version and code hash, `jobId`, `requestId`, immutable `payer` principal and approved refund destination rule, deposited amount, payer-owned funded-credit reference, maximum authorized spend, funding nonce, asset, and funding evidence. A delegate may fund or initiate withdrawal only under a separately validated object-, destination- and amount-bounded capability; a transaction sender, relayer, scheduler, administrator or provider does not become the payer merely by calling the adapter. The accepted provider beneficiary/reference is established solely by the canonical CMP-0.6 match and must not be replaceable by the funding caller or a later provider account rotation.

Track per-job `deposited`, `committed`, `earnedVerified`, `paidOrClaimable`, `payerRefundClaimable`, `payerRefundPaid`, `disputedHeld`, `feeCommitted`, and `unusedUnallocated` with clearly defined nonoverlapping units. Define an explicit versioned liability ledger: for any job, allocated provider claims + payer refund claims + pending/disputed holds + explicitly authorized protocol fees + unallocated payer-owned remainder **must never exceed** its real payer-backed funded credit. Independently enforce the matching/authorization ceiling: aggregate *earned plus any other charged amount* cannot exceed the accepted quote or `maximumAuthorizedSpend`; no transfer or withdrawal may use another job's/payer's backing. Vault-wide solvency and individual payer liability are distinct necessary checks. Any increase to the requester ceiling or deposit requires fresh payer authorization and a separately attributable deposit; it never edits already accepted provider prices or work-unit rights. Zero amounts, unsupported assets, stale policies or unverified vaults fail closed.

Every CMP operation needs a domain-separated unique operation/obligation ID containing chain, approved adapter/vault ID, job, action type and unique bounded sequence/unit or claim key, and an independent consumed-action ledger. An idempotent duplicate cannot create an additional Vault obligation, reserve, payment, claim, refund or fee. Never conflate a Vault `operationId`, CMP `jobId`, CMP `unitId`, `obligationId`, `matchId` or provider `beneficiary`.

## 3. Funding and reserve gate

`CREATED -> FUNDED` requires *actual* confirmed native deposit attributed to the payer and a CMP job-specific, nontransferable accounting credit in the approved adapter; a generic Vault `NativeDeposited` event or unscoped free balance alone is insufficient. Verify payer request/funding authorization, signature/nonce, vault active status and real custody within one authoritative sequence. A deposit accidentally made by an unrelated wallet cannot be allocated to an existing payer job through an untrusted argument. Payer-owned uncommitted credit is withdrawable only through its bound refund/withdrawal route, never by a provider or scheduler.

Before any paid `ACCEPTED -> RUNNING` transition, atomically verify the frozen match/manifest, payer credit, total bounded accepted quote (including every partition, replica, permitted retry, fee and hop), selected resource capacity, and exact backed reservation. Define a finite policy for tentative matching holds; on failed matching or expiry release the hold once back into the *same payer's* free credit, not the general market's pool. A `FUNDED` job lacking backed job-specific credit cannot be matched into paid execution. The immutable accepted provider-derived beneficiary is not yet an earned entitlement merely because reserve exists. Explicitly prevent concurrent jobs from consuming the same payer credit or provider capacity.

## 4. Earned entitlement and settlement

A worker's signature or completion claim is evidence of attribution only. After CMP-0.9 policy-bound receipt and correctness verification, and after prescribed challenge/dispute finality, compute the exact earned amount from accepted integer-only price/metering policy, fixed rounding, accepted maximum and unique CMP-0.3 unit IDs. Store a consumed entitlement key per unit and, if policy permits multiple distinct beneficiaries/hops, nonoverlapping preaccepted per-party shares; every retry of one payable unit shares its single entitlement. An invalid result earns zero unless a separately **preaccepted objective partial-entitlement rule** proves independently verified work and its fixed maximum. Do not release a provider obligation before this gate.

The approved adapter must construct provider claim obligation(s) *only for actually verified earned amounts*, with the beneficiary derived from the immutable accepted match, and route them via the Vault's applicable `createObligation -> releaseObligation -> claim` sequence under narrowly scoped authorizations. The current Vault releases **the whole obligation**, so split verified earnings into individually backed claims/obligations before release; do not partially release a whole-job provider obligation. A released obligation is claimable but not yet paid; `claim` succeeds only for its immutable beneficiary. Track `claimable` versus externally `paid` separately so retries, payout failures and vault winding-down do not fabricate transfers. Check Vault accounting and balance changes and enforce all liabilities atomically across adapter and Vault calls; perform effects/nonce checks before external calls and implement non-reentrancy across the combined execution route. The job may be `SETTLED` after all required verified entitlements have been irrevocably made claimable under its accepted settlement policy, while any later external claim remains a distinct auditable action, not a job reopening.

## 5. Cancellation, unused funds and real refunds

On lawful cancellation, expiry, failure, resolved dispute or successful under-budget settlement, calculate `refundable = payer-backed funded credit - actual earned/fee liabilities - still contested/pending holds - prior refunds`, using precise versioned policy and checked integer arithmetic. First resolve or explicitly preserve every earned/disputed claim; cancelling an *unreleased pending* Vault obligation via `cancelObligation` only restores **vault-wide internal free balance**. The CMP-specific adapter must atomically re-credit that exact amount to the correct original payer's job/refund liability, not a global withdrawable pool; the adapter must also rule out double-credit against an obligation subsequently released or claimed. Neither a `cancelObligation` event nor a changed `freeBalance` proves a payer refund.

For an approved refund, either (a) create and release an individually backed, immutable **payer-beneficiary** refund obligation and record the actual payer claimable amount, followed by `claim` to the payer, or (b) perform an asset- and recipient-route-authorized `withdraw` to the **bound payer** and prove the external transfer atomically. A refund destination migration is a separate authenticated payer-controlled operation; admin/scheduler/provider cannot choose a destination. Option (a) means `REFUNDED` may denote a genuinely payer-claimable amount under the bound refund policy, but distinguish `refundClaimable` from `refundPaid` and never report wallet transfer before `claim` succeeds. Option (b) requires successful external transfer before reporting `refundPaid`. Both routes must prove the payer's segregated credit before using any vault-wide free balance. Avoid unrestricted route authorizations that could sweep another payer's balance.

`SETTLED` is terminal for further execution and provider spending; returning an unused balance follows a separate payer-refund record without converting `SETTLED` to `REFUNDED` or reopening the job. For unsuccessful jobs, `REFUNDED` is entered only when the accepted policy's actual payer-claimable/paid evidence exists and all remaining provider/dispute liabilities are resolved or explicitly preserved. A provider's suspension, emergency pause, Vault winding-down or migration cannot confiscate earned entitlements or redirect payer money. If the Vault is CLOSED or otherwise cannot perform the required route, do **not** mark a transfer successful; retain reconstructable claims and follow a separately authorized nonconfiscatory recovery policy.

## 6. Authorization and audit boundaries

Restrict CMP adapter capabilities to the exact verified Vault ID, asset, per-action class and bounded amount; further enforce job ID, immutable payer, accepted beneficiary, obligation/operation ID and one-time entitlement in CMP logic. Existing [`AssetVault420.sol`](https://github.com/abvhiael/420-integrated-v0.1/blob/feature/cmp-0-protocol-specification/contracts/src/vault/AssetVault420.sol) `ACTION_RELEASE_OBLIGATION`/`ACTION_CANCEL_OBLIGATION` checks are Vault/action-scoped, not automatically CMP job-, verified-unit- or payer-scoped. A broad Vault grant is insufficient without immutable adapter guards; no direct scheduler, verifier, provider node or ordinary admin obtains a general release/withdrawal/claim authority. An authorized verifier cannot choose a beneficiary or skip dispute finality; a capable release caller cannot invent correctness. A Vault operator must not redirect accepted claimants or reclaim payer deposits.

Publish reconstructable `FundingBound`, `ReserveCommitted`, `EarnedVerified`, `ProviderClaimCreated`, `ProviderClaimReleased`, `ProviderClaimPaid`, `PayerRefundCredited`, `PayerRefundClaimable`, `PayerRefundPaid`, `ReserveReleased` records with job, funding/settlement/obligation references, actor, action nonce, applicable policy and amount. Record only commitments; no private input, dataset, bearer token or unbounded execution metadata becomes canonical state. Distinguish events attesting to obligations, claimability and actual balance-changing transfers. Preserve pre/post custody proofs and replay-safe recovery across adapter downtime.
