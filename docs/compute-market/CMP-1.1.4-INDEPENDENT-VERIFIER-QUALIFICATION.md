## Scope

`ComputeJobIndependentVerification420` is the configured `ComputeJobRegistry420.verificationEvidence` endpoint. Its one-time `bindJobs` checks that the job registry references the same canonical accepted-match evidence. Only a committed result can be evaluated. A designated deployment authority approves explicit verifier profile identifiers, and a signer with the shared canonical registry's job-scoped `ACTION_VERIFY_RESULT` capability may submit a signed EIP-712 verdict. The verdict commits to the chain, verifier-adapter address, job, request, manifest, accepted match, assignment, result, signer, approved profile, decision, expected job revision, expiry and one-use signer nonce. A relayer may submit, but cannot change a signed field. Both positive and negative decisions are atomic with the registry's `recordVerification` transition; neither may trigger settlement without a separately qualified escrow proof.

Reject the job owner, worker, and matched resource operator as verifier, even if they hold a capability grant. Check job-scoped authority, approved verifier profile, expiry, signature integrity, replay and wrong-job/wrong-result/assignment/revision mismatches. The canonical `CapabilityRegistry420` registrar and grant administrator on the target chain, actual verifier-policy/profile governance, real independence beyond on-chain identity inequality, quality/correctness of the verification algorithm, and dispute review are NOT proved by a test-only grant contract or signer. Production must establish those controls independently, with documented deployment addresses, code hashes and privileges.
