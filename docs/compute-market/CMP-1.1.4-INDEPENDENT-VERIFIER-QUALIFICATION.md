## Verification-adapter boundary

`ComputeJobIndependentVerification420` is the configured `ComputeJobRegistry420.verificationEvidence` endpoint. One-time `bindJobs` verifies the registry references the same accepted-match adapter. Only a committed result can be evaluated. The adapter's immutable `bindingAdmin` (the deployment-selected profile authority) may approve or revoke explicit verifier profile IDs. A signer with job-scoped `ACTION_VERIFY_RESULT` in the shared `CapabilityRegistry420` may submit a domain-bound EIP-712 verdict. It commits to chain, adapter, job, request, manifest, match, assignment, result, signer, profile, decision, revision, expiry and one-use signer nonce. Owner, worker and matched resource operator identities cannot verify their own job. The registry transitions atomically for positive and negative decisions. Neither verdict settles payer funds.

## Canonical permission and profile authority qualification

`contracts/test/ComputeJobCanonicalVerifierAuthorities420.t.sol` is a NEW end-to-end canonical-authority test, separate from the original `ComputeJobIndependentVerification420.t.sol` test-only `VerifierGrants420` fixture. The new test deploys the actual `CapabilityRegistry420`, transfers its `componentRegistrar` to a distinct registrar actor, rejects component registration by an outsider, and registers `COMPONENT_COMPUTE` with a distinct designated issuer. It rejects outsider job-scoped `ACTION_VERIFY_RESULT` grant issuance/revocation, grants that scope through the issuer, and executes a signed owner/payer request, an actual payer-origin native-420 reservation, operator-accepted match, worker receipt and a verifier-signed verdict against the canonical permission adapter. Payer funds remain reserved, and settlement evidence is fail-closed.

The suite exercises expiry and issuer revocation of canonical verifier grants, denial of a verdict after each, and absence of job-state or custody mutation. A separate designated profile authority deploys the verifier adapter; outsider approval/revocation is denied. The authorized profile authority can revoke a profile to block new verdicts, approve another profile without reactivating the revoked profile, and restore its approval. These are actual contracts with distinct test actors, NOT a claim that a target network has these governance addresses, privileges or an independently correct workload profile. `bindingAdmin` also controls one-time job binding and is immutable: deployment must use a controlled authority, not an unreviewed EOA.

## Remaining independent-verification and release gates

The current adapter authenticates a verdict and checks on-chain address inequality. It does NOT prove shared beneficial control is absent, establish an actual verifier selection/conflict policy, attest independent numerical correctness, or specify how historical verdicts should behave after a profile is revoked. `verified()` currently checks live `approvedProfile`, so revocation can cause a prior decision to cease being accepted as evidence.
