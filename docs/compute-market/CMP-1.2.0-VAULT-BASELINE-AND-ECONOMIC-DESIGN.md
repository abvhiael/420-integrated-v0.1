## 2. Frozen V1 funding and economics

Genesis asset is native $420 (`address(0)`); any other asset is rejected by CMP V1. The payer is the principal in the dual-signed request, not the relayer or Vault deposit event sender. A unique job receives at most one funded credit in the initial V1 design; top-ups, cap changes and refund-address migration are NOT silently allowed. The payer's refund recipient remains the original payer. Positive actual deposit `D` must satisfy `D <= signedMaxSpend`, and the total *accepted* quote `Q` including all authorized work units, replicas, retry and fee liabilities must satisfy `Q <= D` and `Q <= signedMaxSpend`. No price, SLA, accepted beneficiary or provider rotation may silently change Q. An unconsumed signed authorization may permit funding only while live; later verification/settlement/refund of **already accepted and funded** work may continue after authorization expiry using the immutable funding and accepted-match snapshots, subject to independent verdict validity, timeouts and disputes. Expiry does not authorize fresh jobs, charges or rerouting.

Before implementation, freeze a versioned pricing/settlement policy hash at match acceptance: integer-only fee schedule (V1 default: zero fees until separately qualified), deadline/timeout, a bounded objective price per payable unit or an explicitly supported single-job fixed price, maximum retry/replica exposure, challenge and appeal windows, a recipient derived from canonical provider identity and no unquoted spend. No discretionary unapproved partial entitlement: partial release is possible only for independently verified, uniquely keyed, pre-priced units or an explicitly preaccepted objective partial policy. Incorrect results earn zero otherwise. Exact rounding and dust go back to payer. Early/cancelled-work earned amounts are never invented after the request.

For a job define conserved funding `D = U + H + P_pending + P_claimable + R_pending + R_claimable + F_claimable + X`, where U is still unallocated payer-owned credit; H is an encumbered amount awaiting decision, including challenged/disputed amounts; P/R pending and claimable are **nonoverlapping** provider and payer refund obligations; F is an independently authorized fee obligation (zero by default); X is cumulatively **actually externally transferred** provider/fee/payer funds. `earnedVerified` is informational and must not be added again to this sum if its funding sits in H or an obligation. Check both this per-job identity and aggregate unpaid CMP liabilities <= actual backing held by the chosen Vault. Payer isolation is mandatory even when the global Vault is solvent. `paid` means actual transfer completed, never simply an obligation released.

## 3. Integration decision — dedicated registered CMP Vault + exclusive accounting adapter

**Selected minimal-change implementation target:** a newly registered CMP-dedicated `AssetVault420` instance (the existing 420Vault family, NOT a parallel unrestricted compute escrow) paired with a narrowly permissioned `ComputeEscrow420`/CMP settlement adapter holding an immutable registry/Vault/asset binding. Do not alter existing generic shared Vault semantics merely to allow unfenced CMP releases. The adapter is the *only* principal granted this CMP Vault's create/release/cancel and narrowly bounded withdrawal capabilities; no external ordinary caller is granted CMP-Vault-wide spending. No privileged alternative Vault asset path may encumber its recorded free balance. The Vault remains canonical custody and pays immutable beneficiaries through `claim`. Operator policy/authority changes must not create another path to spend existing payer credit.

**Payer-origin deposit:** payer calls an adapter `fund(jobId)` payable and `msg.sender` must equal the request's signed payer (no generic relayer funding in V1); the adapter snapshots immutable request/payer/maximum/deadline, forwards *exactly* `msg.value` to the registered ACTIVE Vault's `depositNative` within the same transaction, confirms pre/post Vault native balance and accounting delta, and records the job-bound funding liability. In the same atomic call, create a unique **pending payer-beneficiary safety obligation for the entire deposit**, thus actually encumbering this job's Vault assets before a second job can use the Vault's free balance. The `Vault.NativeDeposited` actor will be the adapter; provenance is established by the adapter's payer check and corresponding transaction/transfer and the separately indexed `FundingBound(jobId,payer,amount,obligationId)` event. A prior generic Vault deposit is never accepted as CMP job funding. `funded` must prove active canonical binding, the exact job's pending/claimable backed obligation as applicable, and no double funding.

**Atomic reserve and split:** a pending payer safety obligation is not withdrawable before eligible cancellation/refund. On a finalized authorized settlement, in *one reentrancy-guarded transaction*, consume the unique settlement action and cancel the pending payer safety obligation, create separate exact provider obligations for objectively earned units and a new payer refund obligation for the remainder, and release only obligations permitted by finality. The existing Vault cannot split or partly release one obligation; cancel-then-create must be wholly atomic, have exclusive authorized Vault writer(s), verify post-state/total backing, and revert all operations on any failure. If the whole job remains challenged, retain the pending safety obligation until lawful resolution; do not cancel it and expose its balance as globally free between transactions. A claimable payer-refund obligation cannot be cancelled or repurposed. Each consumed job/unit/attempt/operation ID must be chain-, adapter-, vault-, job- and action-domain separated; retries cannot create a second earning. Zero-amount obligations are never created. If multiple settlement stages are introduced, their original payer residual obligation(s) and exact outstanding totals must be conserved by the same atomic algorithm, never assumed supported merely by this design.

**Refund and payout:** expiration or lawful failure without earned work releases the existing pending payer safety obligation to that payer as `claimable`; the Vault `claim()` completes the actual transfer. Under-budget success creates a separate payer refund obligation for all unearned balance alongside verified provider claim obligations; provider and payer claims can occur later without reopening the terminal job. `refundClaimable` and `refundPaid`, and `providerClaimable` and `providerPaid`, must be distinguishable by exact Vault obligation state or synchronized auditable events. No admin, verifier or scheduler may substitute another beneficiary, direct a withdraw to itself, or treat `cancelObligation` alone as payer repayment. Reverted claim leaves original claimable obligation in place.

**Timeout, dispute, slash:** post-match timeout or failed verification never releases payer money while objective verified earnings or a live dispute remain unresolved. A bounded qualified challenge freezes *only the contested entitlement* and excludes it from provider claim release; recusal and appeal preserve historical evidence without double earning. Existing CMP-1.1 job states have no functional exceptional transitions, so corresponding safe transitions and dispute authority are a required subsequent implementation slice, not presently proven. `slash redistribution` in the original CMP-1.2 scope must be a separate objectively proven transfer from separately backed and actually forfeited CMP-1.5 stake/collateral, not payer deposits, and remain **disabled/fail-closed until stake source, adjudication, and recipient split are independently qualified**; document its cross-phase gate rather than falsely claiming it already works.

**Known deployment blocker:** the CMP-1.1 registry has immutable evidence endpoints; `ComputeJobPayerCustody420` holds deposits in its own balance. A new jointly bound job registry, authority, funding evidence and settlement evidence set must be deployed for CMP-Vault funding. Existing live custody balances (if any) cannot be declared migrated by metadata; explicitly verify there are none or implement and qualify a separate lawful migration/refund before activation. Register the new CMP Vault and CMP service only after code-hash/authority verification and authorized publication; no Genesis fixed-address claim.

## 4. Source-backed Vault operation and lifecycle matrix

| Operation | Vault state allowed by current code | Caller/asset/recipient rule | Economic outcome and CMP restriction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `depositNative()` | ACTIVE | Any sender, positive native value; Vault records immediate sender. | Vault-wide increase only. CMP adapter must receive funds from signed payer, forward atomically and create an exact pending payer safety obligation before returning. |
| `createObligation()` | ACTIVE | Vault/action/amount capability; immutable nonzero ID, beneficiary != 0 and != Vault; enough global free balance. | Moves free -> reserved; only adapter may create CMP obligations with proven per-job payer backing and unique entitlement key. |
| `releaseObligation()` | ACTIVE or WINDING_DOWN | Vault/action capability; obligation must be pending. | Moves full pending -> claimable, **not paid**; CMP requires finality and no applicable challenge hold. |
| `cancelObligation()` | ACTIVE or WINDING_DOWN | Vault/action capability; obligation must be pending. | Moves pending -> global free, NOT payer refund; only in atomic adapter-controlled replacement by backed obligations; no stand-alone cancellation that strands payer credit. |
| `claim()` | Any state except CLOSED | Actual immutable beneficiary, or a caller with Vault/action claim capability; transfer always to fixed beneficiary. | Changes claimable -> claimed and transfers externally atomically. Ordinary beneficiaries need no global claim grant; CMP UI must distinguish pending, claimable and paid. |
| `withdraw()` | ACTIVE | Self-only with vault/action capability or recipient-scoped route grant, available global free balance. | Direct external transfer; do not use it for CMP job refunds in V1; no generic adapter/provider/administrator sweep of reserved/claimable payer funds. |
| FROZEN/CLOSED | FROZEN: no deposit/create/withdraw/release/cancel, `claim` still permitted; CLOSED: no entry and closure requires zero assets/open obligations. | Registry controls state via distinct capabilities. | Freezing is NOT a sufficient substitute for a job-scoped dispute hold; never mark a rejected transfer paid or confiscate claims. WINDING_DOWN may release/cancel/claim but not receive new deposits or create replacement obligations: atomic cancel-and-split requires ACTIVE or a qualified alternate recovery route before wind-down. |

## 5. Implementation prerequisites and acceptance gates

1. **Authority and integrity:** the CMP-specific Vault is registered, active, approved native-only, has intended policies, immutable adapter pointer and audited grants; explicit test rejects any other privileged caller consuming its global free balance. If the actual capability system permits later arbitrary grant issuance, prove authorized governance/monitoring boundaries and fail closed on detected writer expansion. No unqualified shared-Vault reuse.
2. **Exact positive vectors:** two independent signed payers concurrently fund distinct jobs through adapter into the same CMP Vault; both acquire independently pending payer safety obligations; verify actual transfers, Vault native balance, `recordedBalance`, `reserved`, `claimable`, obligation IDs and individual credits. A correctly verified fixed-price job splits its full safety obligation into immutable provider claim plus payer residual; claims pay exact beneficiaries, exactly once. Demonstrate full-refund and under-budget paths.
3. **Exact negative vectors:** nonpayer funding, reused deposit or request, over-cap/over-quote, false funding evidence, third-party Vault spend, altered beneficiary, premature provider release, live dispute, expired match, wrong profile/identity, duplicate unit, partial whole-obligation release, duplicate cancel/claim/refund, malicious recipient/reentrancy, incorrect Vault state, arithmetic/replay and insolvency revert without stranded credit or false job transition.
4. **Lifecycle and migration:** prove historical payer/beneficiary snapshots remain valid for completion after authorization expires while *new spending* is blocked; no claim made of automatic historical verdict acceptance on profile/identity revocation. Prove no post-match refund or settlement until custody-safe versioned job transitions and dispute finality are implemented and tested. No quiet transfer of any old direct-custody funds into the new Vault.
5. **Documentation and checks:** map each CMP-1.2 requirement and applicable frozen CMP-INV invariant to named Foundry and integration tests, captured logs and exact SHA. Reconcile then-current `main`, qualify all Solidity shards, Integrated Qualification and Docs on the exact candidate and verify final `main`. CMP-1.2.0 itself is a **design-only gate**: a passing Docs check is relevant, but no financial implementation or full CMP-1.2 release may be inferred from it.

## 6. Deliberate deferrals and next operation

CMP-1.2.0 produces this audited design and no contract, unit test, funded runtime, main merge or permission grant. CMP-1.2.1 first implements an actual *payer-origin, atomically forwarded and Vault-obligation-backed* admission flow in a new deployment topology, with real two-payer financial tests. Challenged settlement, independently qualified dispute finality, terminal state transitions and collateral-derived slash redistribution remain mandatory later gates; `slash redistribution` must not be misrepresented as funded from payer credit or as complete before CMP-1.5 collateral exists. Operational review, testnet address/code-hash proof and paid production activation remain separately blocked.
