It uses locally deployed test registries, not a published deployment or real worker.

**Security boundary:** `snapshot` is a view, not an atomic reservation or immutable on-chain accepted-match record. Its returned settlement account is only a candidate for *future* acceptance; already accepted beneficiaries must remain frozen in separate match/Vault state. A successful preflight must not be treated as authorization to pay, schedule or claim. A later matching transaction must re-resolve and atomically check all accepted constraints, revisions and availability together with actual capacity reservation and payer-isolated Vault funding; store the bound immutable snapshot in canonical match state. No fixed Genesis predeploy or active ProtocolRegistry entry is introduced.
