# CMP-1.8 — Historical identity-record provenance

**Status: source and tests committed; exact-head qualification pending; no accepted match or production eligibility.** After CMP-1.6 provides current local preflight and CMP-1.7 rejects stale current observations, CMP-1.8 adds a distinct, read-only historical-record check. See `contracts/src/compute/ComputeIdentityHistory420.sol` and `contracts/test/ComputeIdentityHistory420.t.sol`.

`verifyHistorical(observed, expectedCommitment)` recomputes CMP-1.7's chain/contract/registry-bound snapshot commitment, resolves the exact provider, node and resource historical revisions from their canonical registries, verifies their immutable ancestry, and compares every snapshot field to the relevant stored record: prospective settlement account, provider/node manifest, endpoint and expiry, compute class, hardware/runtime/capability commitments and advertised capacity. It rejects unknown revisions and caller-forged fields even if the caller recomputes the digest of the forged snapshot. Prior records remain authenticatable following operator-controlled changes or retirement; an old beneficiary remains only a historical record, never the destination for a payment absent an independently authorized frozen match.

**Important boundary:** this verifies the provenance and consistency of *registry data*, not that a resource was actually active or eligible at a claimed historical time, that a provider had verified stake, that a machine possessed advertised hardware, that any signature or offer was valid, that a requester accepted any terms, or that any job was executed, verified or paid. Historical authentication neither validates timestamps of snapshot acquisition nor creates an acceptance event. The old records are not an economic entitlement, reservation or proof of objective execution. CMP-1.5's independent cross-language vectors, mutation-time capability/signature gates, stake and attestation, registered deployment, and latest-main reconciliation remain open. CMP-0.12 operational proof remains blocked.

**Qualification:** require dedicated Foundry historical-provenance test execution and all three full-repository Solidity, Docs and Integrated workflows on the exact final CMP-1.8 head. The source/test commit alone is not a PASS. PR #369 stays draft and unmerged; no new Genesis fixed address, registry publication or Vault authority is introduced.
