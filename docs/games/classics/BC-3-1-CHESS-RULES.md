# BC-3.1 — Chess rules and variant specification

Rules identity: `bc-chess-standard@1.1.0`. Catalogue ID: `chess`. Proposed source specification, not a playable-engine or release attestation. Source: [FIDE Laws of Chess, effective January 2023](https://handbook.fide.com/chapter/E012023), articles 1–10 and Appendix C. Digital adaptations below are BongGoggles policy, not FIDE tournament accreditation.

## Seats and variants
Exactly two playing seats, White and Black. Human-human (online/private/public and local), human-bot, bot-bot qualification/observation; Easy/Medium/Hard use identical rules. Colour choice or random assignment at setup; immutable once started. Standard orthodox chess only; no regional move-rule changes. Chess960, three/four-player chess, bughouse, atomic, antichess and historical variants are not supported under this identity. Clock profiles are configuration, not different movement rules. No wagers, wallet requirement or reward entitlement. Existing protocol enum CHESS and legacy hashes remain unchanged; account sessions use existing 420ID policy.

## Board and legal moves
8x8 files a–h, ranks 1–8, a1 dark, h1 light. White home rank RNBQKBNR, pawns rank 2; Black mirrored by rank, pawns rank 7. White moves first; alternate exactly one legal move. Rook orthogonal, bishop diagonal, queen either, king one adjacent square, knight (2,1) jump. Sliding pieces cannot jump; no friendly capture. Pawns advance toward opposing home rank, one empty square, optionally two from initial rank if both empty, capture one diagonal forward only. Kings never captured. A move must leave own king unattacked; pinned pieces still attack squares for king safety, including adjacent kings. Check requires escape, capture or block where legal; double check permits only king moves. No pass.
Castling is one atomic move: king e1/e8 to g or c, rook h to f or a to d. Original king/rook must retain rights; intervening squares empty (including b on queenside); king not in check and transit/destination not attacked. Rook may be attacked; b-file attack does not prevent queenside castling. King movement permanently removes both rights, rook movement or capture removes relevant right; returning or promoted rooks cannot restore rights.
En passant only immediately following opposing two-square pawn advance beside capturing pawn; move diagonally to passed square, remove advanced pawn atomically; reject if king exposed. Promotion mandatory on final rank, choose Q/R/B/N regardless of captured inventory; effect immediate, including check/mate. No default queen silently selected; incomplete promotion is not a move and clock continues.

## Outcomes and scores
White win 1–0, Black win 0–1, draw ½–½ (PGN 1/2-1/2), unfinished *. Checkmate wins immediately; stalemate (no legal move, no check) draws. BongGoggles applies a material-based rule for automatic dead-position draws and flag fall. A side is deemed unable to mate if it has a bare king, a single bishop or knight against a bare king, or bishops confined to one square colour against a bare king or bishops of that same colour. Draw automatically when the rule excludes mate for both sides. Resignation awards the opponent a win. Flag fall awards the opponent a win unless the material rule excludes mate for that opponent, in which case the game is drawn. Other positions continue until another result applies. This digital policy can differ from the FIDE result for a position whose geometry alone makes mate impossible. No points for material or check. No rating formula introduced here.
Draw by mutual agreement after both have made at least one move. Offer attached to a legal move, remains open until accepted, rejected, opponent moves, or game ends; no repeated offer spam. Threefold repetition and 50 moves by each side without pawn move/capture (100 plies) are claimable by side to move, including a specified intended legal move that would meet threshold; intended move is not committed on successful claim. Incorrect claim rejected without altering board or stopping clock; digital adaptation does not apply arbiter penalties. Fivefold repetition or 75 moves each (150 plies) automatic; checkmate on threshold move takes priority. Position equivalence includes piece placement, side to move, castling rights and legally available en passant, excludes move counters and clocks; occurrences need not be consecutive; initial position counts. Pawn move or any capture resets halfmove counter. Perpetual check has no separate result. Terminal result immutable; no post-terminal commands except replay/rematch.

## Clock profiles and digital adjudication
Untimed or symmetric initial time plus Fischer increment, frozen at start. Suggested presets (minutes + seconds): 1+0, 2+1 bullet; 3+0, 3+2, 5+0, 5+3 blitz; 10+0, 15+10, 30+0 rapid; 30+30, 60+0 longer play. Labels are UI convenience, not official rating categories. Default 10+0 for timed play. White clock starts on authoritative match start. Only active clock runs; valid move received strictly before deadline commits atomically with elapsed time and increment (except terminal result, no further clock runs). Invalid requests, promotion dialog, reconnect and backgrounding do not pause or reset time. At deadline or later adjudicate flag first; server time/transaction ordering controls online, monotonic elapsed time locally. Never trust client timestamp or balance. Simultaneous timeout callbacks, move, resignation and draw acceptance serialized against same revision; only one terminal event. No automatic loss solely for network disconnect; timed clock continues; untimed match remains resumable. Pre-start cancellation is not a scored game; after start explicit abandonment is resignation. No touch-move, physical clock press, illegal-move penalties or arbiter procedure in digital mode; selection reversible before commit. No undo in competitive live games. Analysis branches never mutate official game.

## State, notation and replay
Persist immutable rules identity/version/hash and clock profile, seat mapping, board, side, castling rights, en-passant target, halfmove/fullmove counters, repetition history, clock balances/deadline, draw offer, revision, status/result and reason. Initial standard FEN: `rnbqkbnr/pppppppp/8/8/8/8/PPPPPPPP/RNBQKBNR w KQkq - 0 1`. Coordinate moves e2e4/e7e8q; server derives SAN (captures x, disambiguation by legal alternatives, O-O/O-O-O, =Q/R/B/N, +/#). PGN export includes standard seven tags and consistent result; FEN alone cannot restore repetition history or clocks. Replay from initial state with ordered accepted move and lifecycle events; validate state hashes/revisions and preserve exact historical versions. Analysis may step backward/forward, branch, display legal moves and optional evaluation after game; analysis never supplies live competitive advice. No client-authoritative scores, clocks, state, moves or result. Reject malformed/illegal/stale/out-of-turn/unauthorized commands without board change; deduplicate retry, reject conflicting command ID. Reconnect restores authoritative snapshot plus contiguous journal; corrupt or unsupported state fails closed.
