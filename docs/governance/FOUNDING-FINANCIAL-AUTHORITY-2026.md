# Founding financial authority and sponsored-validator policy — approved direction, pre-Genesis implementation

Status: **APPROVED-THRESHOLD GOV-PRE-03 IMPLEMENTATION CANDIDATE; LIVE TREASURY DEPLOYMENT AND FOUNDING VAULT FUNDING NOT APPROVED.**

The 42,000,000 $420 modeled supply is unchanged. Actual mainnet addresses, verified founder ownership, the complete Genesis split, development vesting, consensus reward routing, independent retirement membership proofs and live Vault capability grants still require approval and release qualification.

## Financial allocation / immutable founding rights
- Founding Operations: 4,200,000 $420 (10% of modeled supply); native $420 only.
- Single-founder routine ceiling: 105,000 $420 per payment **and** per-recipient conservative rolling-30-day aggregate, except coapproved transactions.
- Any two **distinct** founding wallets may approve exceptional payments over 105,000 up to 420,000 $420; 48-hour public notice. Founder-related payouts require two distinct approvals irrespective of amount.
- Operations aggregate limit: 840,000 $420 over a conservative daily-bucket window of at least 30 days. Lifetime Operations authorization never exceeds 4,200,000.
- Independent Contingency: 840,000 $420 (2% of modeled supply); each incident <=210,000 and conservative rolling-30-day aggregate <=420,000. Emergency use only for infrastructure, application rescue or security; two distinct founder approvals and 48-hour notice, except unanimous three-founder fast-track.
- Every founding disbursement has an on-chain recipient, amount, recorded evidence **and separately derived per-payment purpose hash**, immutable budget identity, unique ID, validity window and exact two independent Vault/Treasury executor grants. The disbursement's purpose commitment includes the chain, authority, nonce-derived payment ID, classification and stable evidence reference, so even identical payment details cannot collide and reuse an old disbursement.
- For Contingency, the **same incident must use the same independently reviewed incident/evidence hash for every recipient/payment**. The controller aggregates committed amounts by that stable hash with a lifetime **210,000 $420 per-incident cap**, in addition to the independent rolling **420,000 $420 / 30 days** cap. Deliberately mislabeling the same incident under different hashes is misconduct detectable in case-review evidence, but cannot be inferred solely from opaque hashes on chain; independent review must classify the incident.
- For Operations, a schedule-time check upgrades split payments to one recipient over 105,000 $420 per conservative rolling 30-day window to two-founder/48h exceptional approval. It also checks the independent 840K aggregate and cumulative 4.2M ceiling. Release-time checks separately prevent delayed settlement from bypassing the rolling 840K or routine-recipient 105K thresholds.
- Founding Treasurer authority belongs to the three immutable, distinct addresses supplied at deployment. **No addresses have yet been supplied or enrolled.** There is no generic Treasury withdrawal function or unilateral grant-issuance authority.
- The authorization contract's one-way retirement requires both verified constitutional readiness and Civic authority activation. The independent verifier must prove >=60 **eligible** validators (not 60 simultaneously active committee members), >=15 verified Committee seats, stability for 30 consecutive days, independent ownership evidence and functioning community governance. Its implementation remains pending.
- After retirement, uncommitted/unspent Operations and Contingency assets must transfer to the community-controlled reserve through governed, auditable Vault movements; an event alone is **not** a transfer. Deployment must arrange all outstanding commitments and capability revocations before declaring custody handoff complete.

## Sponsored validators
- Permit FULL 42,000 $420 of protocol-owned validator credit on founder-approved, purpose-limited enrollment, on top of the existing 21,000/21,000 matched model. Standard validator eligibility, BLS uniqueness, activation delay, slashing and withdrawal hold remain unchanged.
- All actual sponsored principal stays in ValidatorRegistry custody until slashed, replaced dollar-for-dollar by participant-owned collateral or released from a completed exit. Returned principal must reach **CommunityValidatorReserve**, never Founding Operations.
- Fully sponsored validator rewards: 10% of each actual earned reward accrual to the Community fund, 90% to the operator. This must be enforced in the authoritative consensus reward accounting, not merely displayed in Wallet or debited after payment. Sponsor and founder must not select active consensus committee members or fabricate rewards.
- Issuing a full sponsorship must be separately constrained by verified beneficiary and validator ID, remaining reserve balance, sponsorship limits, purpose and independent anti-concentration checks. General Treasury approval does not grant that power by default.

## GOV-PRE-03 — Exact founding settlement boundary

The three authorizations remain immutable: (1) claimed **administrator** for routine payments up to 105K and recipient rolling 105K; (2) any two distinct claimed founders for exceptional Operations up to 420K after at least 48 hours of public on-chain proposal notice; (3) any two distinct founders for strictly classified Contingency up to 210K **per incident** after 48 hours, except that all **three** distinct founders may fast-track a genuine emergency. Unclaimed wallets cannot initiate approval, scheduling or release.

**No founder chooses a Vault sender or grants withdrawal capabilities.** `FoundingTreasuryAuthority420.bindReleaseExecutor` is a one-shot deployment-configurator operation requiring the externally reviewed deployed `TreasuryReleaseExecutor420` runtime code hash and `operator == FoundingTreasuryAuthority420`. The stored runtime code hash must be reconciled with the independent release materialization, not supplied by the recipient or a founder. An unbound or rotated executor fails closed. `executeApprovedRelease` requires the exact scheduled `TreasuryDisbursementRegistry420` payment identity, native asset, recipient, amount, purpose and expiry. A routine release additionally requires the Administrator caller.

At **proposal time**, the controller emits `FoundingPaymentNotice` with indexed payment ID, stable case evidence hash and unique purpose hash, together with proposed timestamp, not-before timestamp, expiry and two-founder classification. The purpose hash is calculated and fixed before the 48-hour public-review period starts; scheduling uses that exact original hash and cannot substitute a new case once notice has run. The incident hash is also observable from the first notice, not only once funds are scheduled.

The **generic TreasuryDisbursementRegistry** preserves Civic transaction semantics for ordinary budgets. For the two founding budget IDs only, `markExecuted` now requires the controller to attest the exact active release and the exact pinned executor caller. The executor independently checks the exact Treasury **disbursement capability** and exact Vault **asset/recipient/action/amount route capability**, live Registry service/codehash binding, vault codehash, recipient address, available funds, exact native/token value movement, pre/post vault accounting, and one-shot operation ID. It then calls `markExecuted` in the same transaction. A missing grant, wrong recipient, expired record, mismatched preview commitment, or fake receipt reverts the entire payment including previously tentative accounting. On success, the state records the definitive Vault release commitment, Treasury budget executed amount and founding released/rolling totals. The controller clears its in-progress marker after success; replay fails.

`scripts/verify-founding-financial-policy.py` checks this policy's numerical constants against Solidity, the existing 42M Genesis ledger, founding reserve earmarks and fail-closed deployment flags. `contracts/test/FoundingTreasuryAuthority420.t.sol` exercises purpose uniqueness, per-incident/recipient/aggregate caps, notices, expiry and forgery rejection. `contracts/test/FoundingTreasurySettlement420.t.sol` executes a **real, local test-chain native transfer** using the production Vault/Registry/authorization/executor code and checks exact delivery, rollback and replay denial. These **local test-chain receipts are not testnet or mainnet evidence**.

The approved 4.2M Operations and 840K Contingency remain **unfunded earmarks in the existing 8.6M ProtocolReserve**; no fresh Genesis tokens or live Treasury credentials were issued. Real three-founder signatures, V4 Genesis plan, reviewed deployment codehash, independent per-route grants and funded live Vault receipts are deferred to the approved testnet integration; this GOV-PRE-03 change does not waive those gates.

## GOV-PRE-04 — Sponsored validator and native reward path (candidate)

The established 21,000 $420 personally owned / 21,000 $420 CommunityValidatorReserve-matched onboarding model remains unchanged; the full 42,000 $420 protocol-credit alternative is a separate, purpose-limited path. The 6.3M CommunityValidatorReserve at 0x0425 owns all matched and full credit until replaced with actual operator-owned collateral, recycled after exit or returned on valid slash. A protocol-owned credit never becomes the operator's withdrawable property, even when the operator controls their validator withdrawal account. The reserve supports at most 150 simultaneous *full* 42K credits before any standard matched commitments are considered; both draw from the same reserve and cannot be double-counted.

New `FoundingValidatorSponsorship420.sponsorFullyVerified` requires a founder who has actually claimed their wallet, a non-retired founding office, an immutable 42K source, a valid beneficiary-controlled 420Identity profile at 0x0436 and an independent issuer's VERIFIED-or-higher beneficial-owner credential of type `420/STAKE/FULL_SPONSOR_BENEFICIAL_OWNER/V1`. Its immutable claim hash is a *beneficial owner group*: only one full founder-sponsored credit may be assigned to each issuer-attested group even if wallet aliases differ. The purpose hash binds chain/contract/validator/beneficiary/profile/credential/group/42K. The older unauthenticated `sponsorFully(bytes32,address,bytes32)` selector reverts, and reserve ownership is transferred only into actual ValidatorRegistry custody. Registered BLS identity uniqueness, owner one-validator constraints, activation and slash/exit delays remain those of ValidatorRegistry. A criminally false issuer or a new alias group for the same human is detectable by issuer/identity evidence, not by guessing from an EVM address; real qualified issuer operations remain a testnet release gate.

RewardController V3 pays genuine native money **in the consensus-execution block**, using exact per-call backing produced from the certified, frozen issuance formula by the node420 Geth `Process420SystemCalls` patch. It splits gross sponsored-origin rewards into 90% operator and 10% CommunityRewardReserve at 0x0426 until all remaining original protocol credit is returned. The physical native transfers to the operator, selected ordinary validators, 0x0426 Community, 0x0421 Attention and 0x0422 Development must account for exactly the realized native increment, without emitting or minting unearned non-participation allocations. Node420 compares the precise RewardController balance before and after each consensus reward: unsolicited third-party native deposits must remain inert and must not be counted as issuance; requiring zero balance would create a forced-ETH consensus denial of service. The old manual funding and second settlement functions revert to prevent double payment. The authenticated archive provides `NativeRewardPaid`, `CommunityRewardBackingReceived`, `CommunityRewardSettled`, `SponsoredValidatorRewardAllocated`, and `RewardApplied` logs for independent source/state/Indexer comparisons.

These are repository implementation candidates, not retroactive upgrades to FROZEN_V3 or real testnet receipts. Founder identities, actual trusted issuer credential and matching reserved runtime, node420 binary and complete mainnet/testnet native reward records remain deferred to testnet. GOV-PRE-04 must not be treated as Genesis production approval merely because focused code tests pass.
