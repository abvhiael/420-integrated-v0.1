# HC-WORLD-01 — Owner-approved 45 settlement lore and regional festivals
**Approval:** 2026-10-26. **Status:** OWNER-APPROVED NARRATIVE BASELINE; not a claim of HC-WORLD-01 Level 1 qualification.

This document supplements the locked HC-WORLD roadmap without changing milestones, capacity, assigned capital/town anchors or canonical Original Sixteen objective IDs. IDs are stable settlement slot references aligned with founding schema `HC-<REGION>-S01..S15`. New history is contextual only, not a rewrite of existing archive canon.

## Emerald Coast

- **HC-EC-S01 · Port Alder (CAPITAL):** West coast harbour and Alder River estuary; municipal greenhouses and dock co-ops. Original Sixteen: Emerald Ghost; Harbour Lights.
- **HC-EC-S02 · Moss Landing (TOWN):** Central navigable river port, houseboats and old cargo manifests. Original Sixteen: Blackwater Haze.
- **HC-EC-S03 · Greyhaven (TOWN):** Southern valley research farms and weather observatory. Original Sixteen: Rainmaker.
- **HC-EC-S04 · Cedar Hollow (VILLAGE):** Eastern mountain homesteads, bridges and Eleanor Wren's remembrance gardens. Original Sixteen: Cedar Widow.
- **HC-EC-S05 · Bracken Falls (VILLAGE):** Southern falls and preservation cooperative. Original Sixteen: Bracken Gold.
- **HC-EC-S06 · Fernwick (VILLAGE):** Northern rainforest sawmill village converted to carpentry and collective gardens.
- **HC-EC-S07 · Tidewatch (VILLAGE):** Outer-coast lighthouse, fishing fleet and maritime storm shelters.
- **HC-EC-S08 · Driftwood Point (HAMLET):** Storm-tossed shoreline; salvaged timber artisans and boat repair.
- **HC-EC-S09 · Rainshadow (HAMLET):** Drier eastern highland slopes and distinctive growing microclimates.
- **HC-EC-S10 · Blackfern Crossing (HAMLET):** Historic covered bridge on the interior river-trade route.
- **HC-EC-S11 · Otter Creek (HAMLET):** Creekside cabins, ponds and restored community waterwheel.
- **HC-EC-S12 · Silvermist (HAMLET):** Misty ridge lookout, woodland artists and remote cabins.
- **HC-EC-S13 · Old Mill (HAMLET):** Repurposed timber mill and makers' workshops.
- **HC-EC-S14 · Foxglove Ridge (HAMLET):** Forest ridge of botanical gardens and small farms.
- **HC-EC-S15 · Southbank (HAMLET):** Southern alluvial flats, orchards and seasonal produce markets.

## Approved festival settings

- **EC-F01 · Harbour Lantern Festival** — Emerald Coast, Port Alder. Annual waterfront gathering honoring working crews and the dockworkers' cooperative; lantern displays and harbour performances.
- **EC-F02 · Riverboat Market** — Emerald Coast, Moss Landing. Seasonal floating market celebrating navigable river trading and safe passage; visiting traders and boatbuilders.
- **EC-F03 · Rainy-Season Botanical Fair** — Emerald Coast, Greyhaven. Greenhouse exhibitions, historic weather observations, and botanical art honoring community agricultural research.
- **EC-F04 · Cooperative Harvest** — Emerald Coast, Bracken Falls. Community harvest celebration anchored to the protected valley seed collection and cooperative traditions.
- **GP-F01 · Agricultural Exhibition** — Golden Prairie, Harvest Junction. Seasonal agricultural fair and machinery demonstration with remembrance of 1977 storm volunteers.
- **GP-F02 · Railway Heritage Week** — Golden Prairie, Stonebridge. Preserved train depot, grain elevator and historic railway workers' exhibitions.
- **GP-F03 · Communal Harvest Gathering** — Golden Prairie, Willow Creek. Open invitation celebration with an empty chair reserved for anyone arriving alone.
- **GP-F04 · Prairie Storm Remembrance** — Golden Prairie, Harvest Junction. Noncompetitive memorial gathering honoring regional rescue and mutual aid during the 1977 storms.
- **RM-F01 · Lantern Celebration** — Red Mesa, Solstice Wells. Evening lights celebrating desert travellers, Isabel Moreno's shelter and late-arriving guests.
- **RM-F02 · Desert Caravan Market** — Red Mesa, Cinder Springs. Travel-route merchants, workshops and historical transport displays.
- **RM-F03 · Miners' Heritage Gathering** — Red Mesa, Copper Ridge. Community remembrance of mine closures and the workers' exchange cooperative.
- **RM-F04 · Water-Sharing Festival** — Red Mesa, Mesa Verde. Irrigation and desert-garden exhibition emphasizing cooperative stewardship of limited water.

### Festival scope and controls
- Festivals are approved **narrative and place anchors**. Timing, scheduling, quests, reward supply, costs, marketplace bonuses and event mechanics are deliberately not authorized by this document.
- Event site must reference its canonical settlement ID and stay within the regional civic/public land allocation; it does not mint land or new property slots.
- Existing Original Sixteen archive record, discovery trigger site names, objective IDs, alternate pathways, collectible entitlements and qualified event authority must be preserved; narrative flavour never substitutes for actual mission/evidence qualification.
- Every settlement retains approved maximum per tier, district realtor and 420Corp co-op access. Four new villages and hamlets per regional roster are not new capacity.
- Atlas positions, geographic boundaries, parcel titles, exact zoning and terrain/art approval remain future scoped deliverables.
