## Three-region topology
The founding world consists of three **geographically distinct** regions: Emerald Coast (ID 1), Golden Prairie (ID 2), and Red Mesa (ID 3). All connect through a **shared travel and property system** with persistent canonical parcel identities, owner/tenant rights, and region/district/settlement navigation. Each region has its **own county atlas and settlement network**. Cross-region movement uses recognized travel gateways and does not require one continuous fully rendered terrain map. This is not authorization to mint parcels, finalize Genesis, or bypass regional privileges.

## Emerald Coast — approved physical geography
- **West:** ocean coastline, coves and harbours; Port Alder's historical harbour must be accessible within this coastal network.
- **East:** mountainous highlands and forested foothills; Cedar Hollow's mountain homesteads belong within this zone.
- **Central:** navigable primary river corridor linking inland catchments and coastal trade; Moss Landing's river-trade history must align to its banks and routes.
- **North:** forest communities, wooded watersheds and temperate rainforest.
- **South:** agricultural valleys with suitable rural home/farm/estate/ranch districts and cooperative settlement history; Bracken Falls' valley co-op identity must remain geographically coherent.
- **Greyhaven:** agricultural weather research and local archive sites must have meaningful access to agricultural districts and historic weather-observation geography, not just a generic decorative landmark.
- Geography should be plausible, navigable and coherent: river tributaries descend from highlands, channels reach the coast, roads/bridges follow settlements and terrain, land-use districts accommodate the approved **8,300 rural homes** without arbitrary geographic scattering.
- Keep Original Sixteen heritage provenance and discovery trigger/objective IDs intact. Canonical origin records include Port Alder (Emerald Ghost, Harbour Lights), Moss Landing (Blackwater Haze), Cedar Hollow (Cedar Widow), Bracken Falls (Bracken Gold), Greyhaven (Rainmaker).
