# HC-WORLD-02: approved lot sizes and schematic cadastral survey
**Design approved** 2026-10-26. **Important:** generated geometry is a **synthetic cadastral reservation**, NOT a terrain-surveyed atlas, notarized title/Genesis issuance, physical road network, or proof of river/heritage clearance.

## Approved footprint schedule
Rural classes per region: Homestead 4,200 × 48×48; Smallholding 2,500 × 80×80; Estate 1,250 × 144×144; Ranch 350 × 256×256.
Urban residential per region: compact 850 × 16×20, standard 650 × 24×24, large 200 × 32×32. Commercial per region: compact 220 × 24×24, standard 160 × 32×32, large 40 × 48×48. Urban mix counts are a deterministic initial schedule; no change to existing 1,700 homes or 420 businesses per region.
One regional **planning grid** is 16,000×10,000 allocatable area units. Its five exact nonoverlapping zonal strips have respectively 65%, 10%, 10%, 10%, 5% of 160,000,000 units. The full-rectangle mask and strip arrangement are a *schematic accounting survey*, not approved natural-world geometry. Ocean, impassable slopes, river channels, roads, settlement boundaries, heritage buffers and access easements must be represented in a later terrain-aware map overlay and reconciled while keeping exact budgets and immutable parcel IDs.
The generator reserves the full 8,300 rural + 1,700 residential urban + 420 commercial property identities per region and assigns each urban/commercial reservation to a named municipal registration. All ownership is null and issuance RESERVED_UNISSUED. Every parcel location remains provisional until road access, terrain suitability and Original Sixteen heritage exclusion checks pass. The "geographicCluster" values are land-use planning intent, not a verified coordinate lookup.
**Generate:** `node scripts/highcountry/hc-world-02-cadastre.mjs /tmp/hc-world-02-cadastre.json`. **Test:** `node --test scripts/highcountry/hc-world-02-cadastre.test.mjs`.
**Qualification boundary:** Count, identity, municipal capacity, topology, no overlapping provisional reservations and exact synthetic zone budgets can qualify as bounded Level 1 planning tests. Final HC-WORLD-02 cadastral qualification must also demonstrate physical allocatable terrain masks, road/easement linkage, watersheds and heritage exclusion; those checks are NOT proven by this schematic survey. Do not declare final HC-WORLD-02 Level 1 or create Genesis issuance from this output.
