## Production steps (ordered, scoped Level 1 checks at each step)

1. **A01 — Canonical art direction and masters (13 assets).** Lock region palettes, illustration treatment, typography hierarchy, card dimensions, alpha/export profiles, filename manifests and standard/Archive Edition packet layouts. Approve one visual prototype for each of Emerald Coast, Golden Prairie, Red Mesa.
2. **A02 — Founding identity and standard seed packets (64 assets).** Produce 16 standard fronts, 16 standard backs, 16 transparent founder seals and 16 archive/key illustrations. Use the approved HC-F01–HC-F16 identity records, appropriate region palettes, and legible package text.
3. **A03 — Heritage evidence collection (80 assets).** Produce 64 distinct illustrated discovery fragments (four for each founder) plus 16 individually illustrated final-revelation artifacts. Respect each canonical object title and fictional historical source; do not recycle generic card imagery.
4. **A04 — Archive Edition packages (32 assets).** Produce 16 front and 16 back Archive Edition packets with clear cosmetic collector differentiation; no genetic/statistical bonuses.
5. **A05 — Botanical art collection (32 assets).** Produce one **individual illustrated bud portrait** per founder (16) and one **vintage botanical plate** per founder (16). Botanical plate prominently features the *exact cultivar name* in ornate legible lettering and verified game-metadata attributes: HC-FNN, region, origin settlement, heritage classification, archive designation, founder seal motif and story descriptor. **Do not invent cannabinoid levels, terpenes, potency, aroma, yield, flowering period, effects, sex, morphology differences, resistance, pharmacology or genetically determined traits:** the 28 loci are opaque in V1. Bud portraits are fictional illustrative interpretation, never evidence of a genome's real-world phenotype.
6. **A06 — Registry, deployment and qualification closeout (0 new visual assets).** Normalize assets and immutable paths, verify all 221 unique objects, checksums, transparent layers, filenames, text legibility, accessibility, responsive rendering, catalogue unlock gating and gallery/seed-package display. Qualify only affected High Country asset serving and collector flows; document exact-SHA Level 1 results per step; Level 2 only at relevant integration milestone and Level 3 once for phase closeout. No redundant Solidity/Genesis/global qualification runs for artwork-only edits.
