# R02.11 — Per-player bonus and optional-event entitlements

High Country bonus regions and optional competitions are classified by their canonical content IDs and remain explicitly opt-in. Registered optional content does not alter gameplay economics, cultivation, BUDS, genetics quality or progression. Founding regions and base events remain ungated.

420 Gaming Protocol entitlement IDs are unique grants belonging to one game profile. A single entitlement ID stored on a region or event cannot authorize two independent players. The legacy registration-time entitlement ID is retained for storage and ABI compatibility, but access enforcement now resolves the **current player's** matching grant through HighCountryGamingBridge420.

The player binds their entitlement to (grower profile ID, entitlement type, content ID), after the bridge verifies account ownership of both the bound grower and shared game profile, correct High Country game/type/content, and current Gaming Protocol entitlement activity. Subsequent access is revalidated on every read through the authoritative Gaming Protocol isActive and entitlement record, so revocation and expiry take effect without cache invalidation. Another account cannot bind, replace or borrow a grant. A formerly revoked/expired grant may be replaced only by an independently valid same-player grant.

BonusRegionAccess420 and OptionalCompetitionAccess420 consume hasPlayerBonusRegion and hasPlayerCompetition respectively. Inactive and unregistered optional content remains inaccessible; no entitlement is required for founding regions or ordinary/base events, which remain outside the optional-content registries.

Repository Level 1 covers two-player independence, foreign-owner refusal, revocation, wrong type/content, optional consumer access, Foundry build, formatting, security lint, retained High Country integration/invariants, source verification and ABI. Testnet deployment and phase Level 3 remain distinct.
