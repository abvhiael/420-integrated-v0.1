# R02.9 — Pending breeding child reservation and recovery

Status: implementation in progress; exact-SHA Level 1 qualification required.

- Every accepted breeding event reserves its proposed child genome ID, preventing a distinct pending event from claiming that ID.
- The reservation is retained until atomic, successful finalization or an explicit cancellation/timeout.
- `BREEDING_CANCEL` is a separately scoped authorization on the breeding event ID. A restricted breeding domain does not prevent legitimate recovery.
- The immutable seven-day deadline permits anyone to expire a stranded event after the deadline; early expiry is rejected.
- Cancel and expiry notify the randomness coordinator as the original requester. The coordinator records permanent cancellation and blocks late fulfillment and consumption, even if entropy was supplied earlier.
- An event ID is never reissued after cancellation or finalization. A cancelled child genome identity can only be reused by a *new* breeding event with a new request context and separately authorized randomness.
- A finalized child cannot be cancelled, reopened, or rerolled. Randomness consumption and child genome creation remain atomic.
- Deployment needs an updated ABI and trusted provenance for the breeding and randomness contracts. Live integration, randomness-provider proofs, operational acceptance and testnet deployment are not asserted by repository CI.

R02.9 targeted qualification covers duplicate reservation, unauthorized cancellation, late entropy, early expiry, timeout recovery, replay denial and retained High Country invariant suites. Level 2 and phase Level 3 remain separately scheduled.
