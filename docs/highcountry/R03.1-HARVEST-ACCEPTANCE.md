# High Country R03.1 — READY harvest acceptance

Canonical step: `docs/highcountry/RECONCILIATION-AND-BUILDOUT-ROADMAP.md` R03.1. This is a **Level 1 ordinary app step**; Level 2 belongs to a meaningful R03 integration milestone and Level 3 to R03 phase exit.

## Implemented authority and lifecycle
`HarvestEngine.acceptHarvest(plantId)` records a single canonical, immutable harvest acceptance for a READY plant. The caller must be that plant's recorded grower and must hold the `HARVEST_ACCEPT` capability on `ModuleIds.HARVEST_ENGINE` for `bytes32(uint256(plantId))`. The engine requires the exact canonical `PlantRegistry`, `CultivationEngine` and authorization roots, a bound nonrestricted cultivation emergency authority, a sealed nonzero phenotype expression, and a nonzero registered ruleset/content-hash seal. It reads the actual plant state and canonical sealed expression. The accepted receipt binds plant ID, genome ID, expression hash, ruleset ID, ruleset content hash, grower, land parcel, public plot and acceptance time. Duplicate admission is rejected and failed calls persist no acceptance receipt. `HarvestAccepted` emits the committed provenance.

**Phase boundary:** This records single-use harvest *acceptance*; no grade, quantity, inventory asset, token, product or capacity-release side effects are fabricated here. R03.2 defines yield/quality/grade. R03.3 registers product and ledger movements. R03.4 integrates one-time plant termination/capacity release and recovery. R03.5 adds whole-journey adversarial qualification. The plant can remain READY after this R03.1 acceptance; no claim of fully completed cultivation-to-product journey is made.
