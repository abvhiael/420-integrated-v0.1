# High Country R03.3 — canonical harvest-to-product registration

## Canonical boundary

R03.3 registers an immutable harvest-derived product receipt and an issuance-only accounting ledger. The roadmap explicitly reserves the general-purpose **InventoryRegistry** and **ResourceLedger** contracts for R04.1; they are not represented as complete here. R03.4 alone owns termination and cultivation-capacity release. No transferable asset, token, cash receipt, resource consumption, or custody is created here.

### Authority split and atomic accounting

- **PlantRegistry** owns planted plant/genome/grower/parcel identity and plant stage.
- **CultivationEngine** owns the sealed phenotype expression, ruleset and environmental quality.
- **HarvestEngine** owns single-use READY harvest acceptance and immutable R03.2 scoring.
- **HarvestProductRegistry** owns the *harvest-only* canonical output product receipt and cumulative issued grams per grower, and global issuance totals.
- **R04.1 InventoryRegistry/ResourceLedger** will own general item inventories, resource categories, transfers, acquisitions and consumption after approved protocol decisions. Do not mistake the R03.3 issuance counters for spendable balances.

`registerHarvestProduct(plantId)` reads **only** authoritative accepted harvest and sealed score from HarvestEngine. Callers cannot supply product identity, grower, quality, grade or quantity. The product ID is domain-separated from chain ID, HarvestEngine address and plant ID; a plant is issued only once. An independent relayer may settle another grower's previously accepted harvest but cannot redirect its output. Effects are performed in one EVM transaction: immutable product receipt, plant→product mapping, grower issuance counter, global quantity and product count. Revert leaves all unchanged; duplicate calls revert. All issuance counters represent monotonic, non-redeemable historical issuance, NOT live stock.

Every receipt preserves plant, genome, expression, ruleset/content hash, grower, parcel/public plot, accepted time, whole-gram quantity, quality, genetic modifier and grade. Emit `HarvestProductRegistered` for offchain indexers; index `productId`, `plantId` and `grower`. Indexers must verify the exact onchain canonical receipt and previous HarvestAccepted/HarvestScored events. No external calls after writing ledger state.

### Dependencies and limits

Deploy HarvestProductRegistry with the deployed canonical HarvestEngine address, after authority/root/phenotype rules are verified. HarvestEngine remains the source of truth; incorrect address binds permanently and requires fresh deployment. Caller privileges cannot mint arbitrary output because the receipt must already exist. Accepted harvest scoring is currently provisional V1 gameplay policy pending the required independent parameter/trust acceptance.

Do not claim R03.3 resource *consumption* or comprehensive inventory accounting: the source-backed seed/clone consumption was implemented in R02, capacity accounting remains in PlantRegistry and release is R03.4, while generalized resources are R04.1. No invented deduction from fungible resources is allowed. Zero-gram harvests result in a registered zero-quantity product receipt and zero minted grams.

Level 1: targeted exact-SHA contract build, format, lint, cultivation→harvest→product/regression, scoring, ABI and verifier workflows. Level 2 cross-component review follows the R03.4 milestone if required. Level 3 comprehensive audit is deferred to R03 phase closeout.
