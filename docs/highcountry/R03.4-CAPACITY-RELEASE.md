# High Country R03.4 — exactly-once cultivation capacity release

## Canonical scope and ownership

R03.4: terminate/release cultivation capacity exactly once; cancellation, failure and restricted-mode paths conserve resources. This is distinct from R03.3 harvest-only product issuance, and R04.1 generalized inventory/resource accounting.

PlantRegistry is the canonical authority for private and public cultivation-capacity counters and the plant lifecycle. The existing READY -> TERMINATED transition in `advanceStage` calls `_advanceAt`, decrementing `activePlantsByParcel` and exactly one of `privatePlantsByParcel` or `activePublicPlants`. It emits PlantCapacityReleased and PlantAdvanced in the same transaction. A second TERMINATED transition is rejected before counters change. Solidity 0.8 checked arithmetic reverts the entire transaction on underflow. Authorization is checked via PLANT_ADVANCE. The transition is allowed during a CULTIVATION emergency restriction so capacity is not stranded; admission and progression remain restricted.

There are two distinct terminal contexts: after completed harvest/product registration, terminating the READY plant releases its capacity without modifying immutable HarvestEngine or HarvestProductRegistry receipts or issuance counters; without an accepted harvest, READY -> TERMINATED functions as cancellation/abandonment and must not mint a product. No earlier growth stage can jump directly to TERMINATED.

## Targeted qualification

`.github/workflows/highcountry-r03-4.yml` runs exact-SHA check, scoped build, format and lint, direct R03.4 tests, retained cultivation/harvest/product regression, and source verifier. Tests cover unauthorized terminal transition; successful post-harvest release; replay rejection; unchanged issuance and product provenance; premature cancellation rejection; restricted-mode READY cancellation; and no product issuance on cancellation.

## Limitations / remaining disposition

The current source design does not force product registration before a READY plant can be terminated. This is intentional for cancellation of unharvested READY plants, but the treatment of an accepted-yet-unregistered harvest requires an explicit protocol disposition to avoid stranded accepted product receipts. The exact semantics of cancellation versus settlement in that intermediate state require an additional targeted adversarial test and, if necessary, a canonical boundary/authorization guard. Public-plot release deserves a separate end-to-end conservation test. R03.4 should not be marked COMPLETE until those edge cases and Level 1 workflows are verified on the final SHA. Level 2 app lifecycle milestone should review integration with R03.3; Level 3 reserved for after R03.5.
