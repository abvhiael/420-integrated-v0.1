# High Country — R04.1 inventory and resource accounting

Status: implementation candidate; **not qualified until exact-SHA R04.1 Level 1 workflow passes**.

Authority remains split. HarvestEngine decides acceptance and score; HarvestProductRegistry retains immutable historical receipts and monotonic issued grams; InventoryRegistry imports receipts only once as fungible-quantity **lot balances**, never interpreting historical issuance as current stock. ResourceLedger alone records available/reserved/consumed per owner and resource/lot. PlantRegistry/CultivationEngine alone own cultivation capacity and cannot be replaced by spendable ResourceLedger capacity counters.

The unit of harvested flower quantity is whole grams, exactly as emitted by HarvestProductRegistry. Other resources must declare a unit (GRAM, COUNT, CAPACITY) when enrolled; operators must never implicitly convert between units or categories. Item identity includes the chain, InventoryRegistry instance and canonical product receipt; a zero-gram receipt creates no tradable lot. Resource identifiers and lot identifiers occupy distinct namespaces by registration and unique commitments; callers must not infer interchangeable financial assets from these IDs.

An administrator may register typed nonharvest resources, freeze new operations, grant or revoke issuers. Authorized issuers may acquire only explicitly registered, active, nonharvest resources. Harvest lots can only be acquired from canonical on-chain product receipts, with owner and quantity taken from that source. Holders may reserve, release, consume or transfer **their own available** balances; reserved stock cannot be transferred, consumed as available, or released by another holder. Every movement uses a globally single-use operation ID; reverting fails atomically and preserves replay eligibility. Global issued and consumed counters are monotonic; per-owner available + reserved + consumed are tracked without negative balances. Consumption destroys spendable stock, not provenance.

These records are on-chain canonical objects. Guest/local game state remains local; the service may only promote guest claims through authenticated R05 migration and approved authoritative issuance paths. R04.1 does not mint tokens, hold currency, implement a market, grant custody or guarantee production deployment. R04.2 equipment, R04.3 processing, R04.5 monetary settlement and R05 authenticated service bridges remain their separately numbered scope.

Acceptance: exact-SHA affected build, formatting, lint, ABI verification, authorization/replay/overdraw/conservation tests, and retained harvest scoring must pass. Broader R04 milestone Level 2 and final R04 Level 3 run later, not on every ordinary step. Deployment/operator/real RPC acceptance remains R07/testnet.
