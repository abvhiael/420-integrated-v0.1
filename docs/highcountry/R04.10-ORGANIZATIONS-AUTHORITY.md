# High Country R04.10 — OrganizationRegistry and OrganizationAuthority

Canonical roadmap: R04.10, **Membership, bounded delegated authority and treasury permissions**. Gameplay specification: authenticated membership and bounded delegated roles; optional canonical rights separated; no administrator role may silently grant member wallet or treasury authority; leaving and removal must resolve pending obligations.

## Implementation and authority

- `OrganizationRegistry` registers immutable organization IDs, owner administration, invitation/consent-based joins and membership states (Active, Leaving, Removed), role-independent administrator succession, and membership events. Self-leave and administrator removal immediately invalidate delegated access.
- Pending obligations are explicitly recorded and block final exits; unilateral administrator deletion cannot clear them. Member-initiated discharge and administrator confirmation are both required before lowering a reserved count. This bilateral record is *metadata only*: the actual ledger, payment service or dispute authority must independently verify settlement. No transfer, escrow, debit or discharge of monetary debt is performed here.
- `OrganizationAuthority` issues issuer-bound grants scoped to an exact organization, delegate, action, target scope, start/end interval and finite use count. Unknown actions are rejected; only the fixed noncustodial action catalogue (CULTIVATION, ROSTER and NOTICES) is eligible, to prevent arbitrary action names from acting as aliases for wallet, rights or treasury authority. Replay/use exhaustion, revocation, administrator rotation, departure, forged identity and out-of-scope actions fail closed. Only the delegate can consume its grants.
- `OrganizationTreasuryPermissions` adds a separate noncustodial treasury policy: an organization administrator proposes a beneficiary/action/scope/expiry/amount cap; an independently configured financial approver must approve; only the immutable designated treasury adapter can consume the precise authorized quantity. Leaving/removal, expiry, revocation, exhaustion or administrator rotation invalidates approval. It does not move funds or grant token allowances. The genuine canonical Treasury must enforce its own custody, operator authentication and accounting on every transaction.
- Treasury, wallet and canonical property rights actions are explicitly **nondelegable** through the general organization authority: `HC.ORG.TREASURY`, `HC.ORG.WALLET`, `HC.ORG.RIGHTS`. No implicit wallet signature, signing power, spending approval, on-chain treasury permission, registry transfer right or payment occurs. Any production treasury operation must be independently authenticated by canonical Treasury with its own scoped capabilities and transaction policy.
- R04.9 leases and licenses remain independently authoritative; membership neither evicts occupants nor bestows RightsRegistry grants.

## Integration limits and phase gates

This step establishes the bounded organization contract seam; it does **not** falsely treat organization metadata as a canonical treasury ledger or an asset-rights transfer. Integration into actual Treasury/Wallet/Identity requires reviewed adapters and explicit independent address-authority grants. Organization ID proof, authenticated signing, production role catalogues and client/API/Indexer indexing need production interface review before launch. Live testnet deployment, real signer rotation, reorg/failover observation, Treasury settlement transaction scenarios and external approvals remain operational/testnet gates. R04.10 is the meaningful membership/authority integration milestone; app-scoped Level 2 is appropriate when canonical treasury/identity adapters are integrated. Full Level 3 is deferred to phase closeout.
