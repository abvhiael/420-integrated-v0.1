# High Country R04.11 — Cooperatives and bounded governance

Canonical definition: `R04.11 | CooperativeRegistry, CooperativeGovernance, GovernanceExecutor | Voting/execution/quorum/timelocks only as specified; no alternate permission escalation`. Gameplay requirements: cooperative membership, proposals, quorum, timelock and scope are versioned; snapshot/replay/exit and treasury protections; no arbitrary wallet calls.

## Audited design

- OrganizationRegistry remains canonical for authenticated (address-based) membership. CooperativeRegistry is immutable organization-linked with member self-enrolment and bounded electorate (64); no administrator can covertly cast votes on behalf of members.
- CooperativeGovernance freezes each proposal's eligible voters at creation, prevents post-snapshot entrants voting, rejects departed voters, and enforces one vote per address. Fixed version-1 policy permits only `HC.COOP.POLICY.SET`, an exact scope and value. Quorum is strict majority of eligible snapshot electorate and positive votes must beat opposition. Voting lasts three days; execution is held a further two days. Proposer can cancel during voting. Proposal IDs are nonce- and chain-specific.
- GovernanceExecutor is restricted to storing noncustodial cooperative policy metadata. It has **no arbitrary target call, wallet action, money movement, asset transfer, upgrade or treasury authority**. Vote outcome is never a substitute for Genesis capability grants or canonical module permissions. Governance calls mark executed before recording policy, atomically; replay is rejected.
- Executor binding is a one-time deployer-authorized seam. Independent address-authority/deployer review and deployment wiring remain required before production.
