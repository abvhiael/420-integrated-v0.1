# High Country R04.12 — Seasons, Templates and Competitions

**Canonical roadmap:** R04.12 SeasonRegistry, CompetitionTemplateRegistry, CompetitionEngine. Gameplay: versioned rules, event entry/close/finalize, eligibility/scoring/evidence/tie/revocation; base wallet-free events and optional scoped entitlement events; no wallet/perk stat or scoring bonus.

- `SeasonRegistry`: nonzero immutable season identity, future opening/closing times, published rules hash and authority; cancellation before season begins.
- `CompetitionTemplateRegistry`: registrar-only version 1 immutable template registration, exact rules hash, optional entitlement scope; base event entitlement scope is zero. No future rules mutation.
- `CompetitionEngine`: organizer must be the season authority, and event interval must be within a noncancelled season. On-chain self-entry once per wallet-free player address in 128-entrant cap; optional templates call externally supplied per-player scoped eligibility adapter, failing closed on denied/missing/revoked scopes. Entitlements never affect the score formula.
- **Verified score:** one independently finalized verified FLOWER harvest per player/event. Score equals exactly `qualityBps` (0–10000), excluding mass, wallet balance and paid perks. Deterministic ties favor earlier finalized harvest and then lower canonical harvest identifier. Attempts to fabricate quality, reuse harvest IDs or submit after closure fail. Canonical source is reverified at finalization to reject later-revoked/finality-invalid results. Independent adjudicator may reject challenged results before challenge deadline; finalization is single-use and can produce no winner.
- No rewards are transferred, no prize promises or cross-game entitlements are issued. The evidence adapter and optional entitlement adapter must be bound to independently reviewed canonical deployment addresses. Mock evidence/entitlements are only unit tests, never production proof.
- **Level 1:** `.github/workflows/highcountry-r04-12.yml` runs exact-SHA checkout, affected build/bytecode, formatting, static/security lint, focused event/adversarial regression suite and canonical High Country verifier. Do not declare COMPLETE without exact implementation SHA and passing jobs.
- **Level 2:** reserved for meaningful High Country app integration milestone (canonical harvest admission, entitlement, provenance, reorg/dispute, service clients and indexes).
- **Level 3:** full inventory, independent Genesis address-authority, integrated/global, Docs and deployment/security reconciliation deferred until R04 phase closeout.
- **Live testnet:** canonical verified HarvestProductRegistry, trusted finality and signer management, organizational event approvals, indexing and replay/reorg monitoring, external entitlement gateways, product integrity, performance/gas limits, security reviews and actual deployment acceptance remain deferred.
