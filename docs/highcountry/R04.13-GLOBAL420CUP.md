# High Country R04.13 — Global420Cup

**Canonical roadmap:** R04.13 Global420Cup. Actual tournament eligibility/results/prize rules, canonical result provenance and scoped cross-game issuance.

The baseline cup is a versioned **four-entrant, two-semifinal, one-final** bracket. All three underlying matches must be registered SeasonRegistry/CompetitionTemplateRegistry competition events with the **same season and same organizer**, with identity/rules published before the cup is registered. Semifinal winners earn final eligibility. Cup finalization requires the finalized winner and **both** entered finalists to be distinct semifinal winners, preventing substituted local/unverified results. The underlying R04.12 competition verifier owns canonical finalized harvest/scoring and quality-only ranking; no paid wallet stat bonus.

Each immutable cup records its competition event IDs, a versioned rules hash, explicit prize-rules hash and cross-game prestige scope. Every event may be assigned to **only one** cup. Prize-rules hashes are commitments only; neither this contract nor its metadata constitutes funded prize issuance, payout authority or a promise that an external prize is funded. No ERC20/ERC721 transfers, arbitrary target calls or issuance to external apps occur.

A finalized result is a deterministic keccak256 commitment bound to chain, contract, cup, rules, prize policy, scope, three canonical event IDs, semifinal winners, final champion and the actual canonical competition scores. Once finalized, it cannot be overwritten. One scoped **nonfinancial cross-game attestation** can be emitted for the champion, and downstream consumers must validate issuing chain/contract, cup ID, champion, commitment and exact scope. This attestation does not grant better cultivation statistics, claim spendable prizes or bypass independent Rights/Identity/Wallet authority.

**External acceptance:** actual service finality and fraud dispute protections depend on R04.12 competition evidence binding; optional cross-game service consumer/issuer authorization and funded/claimable prize settlement require separate audited implementations and deployment qualification. No manufactured funding claims.
