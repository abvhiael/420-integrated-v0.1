# High Country R04.3 — ManufacturingEngine

Status: Level 1 candidate. Do not claim complete before exact-SHA CI acceptance.

Canonical R04.3 assignment: immutable/versioned recipes with resource IDs, two input slots, two output slots, bounded jobs, explicit process duration, cancellation losses/refunds, reserved-before-start inputs, and single-shot completion. Recipes use at most two different inputs and outputs; expanded recipes require a versioned successor rather than silently discarding entries.

Trust and unit boundaries: R04.1 InventoryRegistry remains canonical accounting and enforces explicit per-owner operator grants. The registered manufacturing contract must independently have issuer authority for output acquisition. Input reservations operate on the actual owner's available units, not historical harvest issuance. No local/guest resource may mint canonical inventory without reviewed issuance. The on-chain operator grant is opt-in and revocable; ManufacturingEngine cannot acquire outputs without independently granted issuer status. Resource IDs must be catalogue-approved and their quantity units verified operationally; distinct resources cannot be implicitly compared or converted.

Each recipe ID is immutable, pinned to a version, with nonzero output and input, positive duration at most 365 days, loss BPS 0..10000, no duplicate resource IDs on either side, and an administrative active status for new jobs. An existing started job retains its original recipe across deactivation. On start, reserve all inputs atomically, enforce at most maxJobsPerOwner (1..32), and record immutable job owner/time/recipe. On completion at or after timestamp deadline, consume all reserved input exactly once and issue canonical output in the same transaction. On cancellation, floor-round input loss by BPS, consume that portion and return the rest. Output issuance and consumption fully roll back on any failed downstream authority or accounting. Slots and job states only change on successful transaction. No user-provided completion timestamp, payload, wallet privilege or free premium stat effects.

R04.4 is responsible for durability/repair/recycling and may consume manufactured resources only through reviewed authorization. R04.5 governs BUDS/cash; R05 governs trusted guest/service mapping. Deployment operator grants, live RPC/indexer projection, physical recipes and service integration require separate future acceptance; the contract does not fabricate actual equipment or production deployment.

Qualification: targeted forge build/size, formatting, lint, authorization/adversarial/replay/rounding/failure regression, retained R04.1/R04.2 tests, ABI and canonical verifier. R04.3 contributes to the R04.2-R04.4 Level 2 integration milestone; full Level 3 at R04 phase closeout.
