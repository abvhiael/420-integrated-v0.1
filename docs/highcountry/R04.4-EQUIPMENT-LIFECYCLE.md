# High Country R04.4 — EquipmentLifecycleEngine

Status: implementation candidate. Qualification only after exact-SHA Level 1 CI PASS.

R04.4 implements version-pinned per-equipment-type durability, deterministic wear on authorized use, degradation threshold, broken-state suspension of six-control equipment effects, material-costed repair, irreversible retirement and single-use material recovery through recycling.

EquipmentRegistry remains canonical for equipment ownership, slot accounting, placement and effects. One permanently bound lifecycle controller may disable/re-enable effects and retire a record. A broken item cannot be used or newly placed; repaired equipment resumes only after real conserved material consumption. Retirement releases parcel capacity and deactivates effects; recycling requires retirement and issues approved recovered resources at most once. Repair policy is immutable by equipment type, including maximum durability, use cost, degrade threshold, restore increment, resource cost and recycle yield. No negative durability, duplicate retirement/recycling, currency custody, wallet stat modifier or unchecked client claim is accepted.

Use recording requires a trusted simulation verifier rather than a player-supplied unproven gameplay event. Owners alone perform repair, retirement and recovery. InventoryRegistry enforces per-owner opt-in operator authority and independent output issuance approval. Changes fail atomically on downstream inventory errors. Version upgrades require a distinct equipment type and lifecycle policy; no mutating live costs.

These canonical contract mechanics are not a substitute for R05's authenticated game-service journey, physical product catalogue, production issuer grants, or R07 live deployment acceptance. R04.2–R04.4 form a retained app-level Level 2 milestone; the final R04 Level 3 remains reserved for entire R04 phase closeout.
