# High Country R04.6 — SkillRegistry, ResearchRegistry, DiscoveryRegistry

R04.6 provides three separate registry namespaces for progression, research and discovery. Immutable version-pinned tracks contain a canonical prerequisite, required points, maximum points per unique proof and bounded metadata reward. Only explicitly approved independent verifier identities may attest points; local save imports cannot attest, mint tokens or claim a skill. Unique proof IDs cannot be replayed within their registry, completion is one-time, and points never exceed the configured exact target. Prerequisites require previously completed tracks for that player. Registered tracks cannot be overwritten, and each independent registry maintains its own verification and progression authority.

The metadata reward is not BUDS issuance, $420, a payment, or a protected gameplay advantage. The canonical R04.5 economy requires a separately approved gameplay source catalogue and authenticated economy issuer before any actual credit. No unapproved numerical catalogue values are asserted: test numbers are fixtures. Catalogue commissioning, trusted service-verifier deployment, progression-user accounts, wallet-free gameplay and client/Indexer/service integration are required under R05/R07 for production use.

Level 1: affected High Country build and size, forge formatting, lint, focused permission/prerequisite/boundary/replay/isolation tests, canonical verifier against exact implementation SHA.
