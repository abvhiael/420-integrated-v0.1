# High Country R04.7 — MissionEngine

The original roadmap requires Eligibility → Activation → Completion → Claim/Failure/Expiry with duplicate/replay resistance. The game specification requires immutable objective/reward, predeclared economics, unique evidence, and no self-trade reward farming.

The on-chain MissionEngine registers immutable versioned templates, bounded mission targets/durations, prerequisite-completion requirements, one-shot owner activation, independent trusted verifier proof attestation, global proof uniqueness, exact completion, owner-only claim, verifier failure and time-based expiry. Each instantiated mission freezes its objective, version, threshold, deadline and reward-unit metadata. No local-save claim or client assertion is accepted as authoritative proof. Completion/claim emits state only; it does not mint BUDS, native $420, or an ERC20 and does not escrow unapproved currency. No cancellation fee, refundable stake, or confiscation is invented: missions have no deposit or cost and failure/expiry releases nothing.

Important boundary: independently authorized verifier rules and real gameplay source proof validators, numerical catalogue approval, anti-self-trade validation and service-side bounded reward issuance must be integrated as R04.5/R04.7/R05 production prerequisites. A fake proof passed by a privileged testing verifier is *not* production evidence. Market settlement and rewards are integration-owned by R04.8 and the R04.5–R04.8 economic milestone; R07 deployment and live acceptance remain open. Level 1 is focused on this contract, tests and verifier; full Level 3 remains at R04 phase end.
