# High Country R04.8 — ordinary market integration

The canonical V1 ordinary market is **registered-player, internal-asset, zero-fee, fixed-price, full-fill only**. No partial fills, native $420, swap/bridge or canonical on-chain custodial exchange. The independently controlled ecosystem asset market remains a separate boundary; this repository-side ordinary market makes no claim to implement player-signed canonical asset settlement.

`services/highcountry-economy/src/market.js` introduces `HighCountryMarket` backed by the **same serializable transaction state** as R04.5 BUDS. Authoritative inventory receipts require independent `requireAssetIssuer` and `verifyAssetReceipt` checks; no local/guest balance is authoritative. A listing reserves the seller's admitted units and freezes fixed price, exact quantity, seller, asset and expiry. Its buyer is authenticated through `registeredPrincipal`; purchase atomically transfers available BUDS and reserved asset to their counterparties with zero fee. The market admits no partial fill, self-trade, duplicate listing, replay or double purchase. Cancellation and expiry return exactly the original reserved units; a spent listing cannot be reversed into a second payment. Failed operations roll back the transaction, including operation ID and inventory changes.

The Postgres state adapter now round-trips market assets, listings and receipt replay keys in the **same row-locked SERIALIZABLE transaction** as BUDS, with backwards-compatible default fields for older schema-one data. In-memory transaction fixtures remain tests only. No real RPC/Indexer or on-chain resource transfer is claimed; production `verifyAssetReceipt` must reconcile with real R04.1 InventoryRegistry authority and prevent forged or stale transfer provenance. A locally tested fake issuer is not a real gateway. Production readiness requires independently authenticated service routes, durable database credentials, concurrency/failover acceptance, bounded proof/catalogue policy, on-chain/off-chain inventory reconciliation, and complete SDK/client/operator integration under their canonical R05/R07 owners. The optional canonical ecosystem-asset market must remain disabled until its separately reviewed wallet signing, custody, fees, reentrancy and Pay/rights dependencies are defined and qualified.

Targeted R04.8 Level 1 qualifies affected economy code, BUDS service regressions and adversarial market tests against one exact SHA. R04.5–R04.8 retained economic integration is the natural Level 2 milestone. R04 phase Level 3 (Solidity, independent Genesis authority, global integration, Docs, app + services, security/config) remains at full R04 closeout.
