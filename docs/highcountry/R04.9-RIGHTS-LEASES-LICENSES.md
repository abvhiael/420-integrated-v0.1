# High Country R04.9 — rights, leases and licenses

Canonical scope: `LeaseRegistry`, `LicenseRegistry`, `RightsRegistry`; identified parcel/asset control, bounded term and scope, explicit issuer/owner authorization, transfer, revocation, expiry, fees as **metadata only**, active-plant safety, and explicit dispute boundary.

- `RightsRegistry`: administrator registers a unique asset and owner; owner grants scoped time-bounded use, may revoke and transfer owned asset. Frozen assets immediately disable delegated effective rights. A transfer changes the owner, but grants issued by an earlier owner must not become accepted as the new owner's authority; integration consumers should validate canonical owner/issuer binding.
- `LicenseRegistry`: asset owner issues bounded non-statutory game-use licenses with explicit fee metadata. Expiry/revocation/freeze/owner change makes license unusable. A current licensed holder may transfer only while live; no entitlement-based implicit role grants.
- `LeaseRegistry`: owner approves bounded tenant term/scope/fee metadata. Explicit Active→Disputed/Revoked/Closed transitions; expiry and revocation block new plant placement. The activePlants count preserves ongoing obligations; when nonzero, `exitPending` requires authorized remediation rather than silently evicting/deleting plants. Disputes freeze new use and are resolved by the lessor; external arbitration is a separate authority.
- No fees are debited, escrowed, or promised: future Pay/BUDS funding and rent settlement require their own reviewed authority. Neither on-chain rights nor game-use license constitutes a real-world statutory license, deed or land title.
- The plant counter is write-restricted to the immutable deployment-defined plantAuthority (not tenant self-reporting), but this must be wired to the canonical cultivation/occupancy event source to provide cryptographic provenance. R04.9 cannot be considered production activated until canonical High Country plant placement/harvest/removal authority (and disputed-plant reconciliation) is wired and independently qualified in the appropriate app integration phase. Dispute resolution is not a court or statutory arbitration mechanism.
- Level 1: `.github/workflows/highcountry-r04-9.yml` checks exact SHA, High Country build/sizes, formatting, lint, adversarial/negative lifecycle tests and canonical verifier. Do not treat queued, skipped, or failed checks as passing.
- Level 2: rights/plant integration milestone when canonical live occupancy/plant authorizations and R04.10 organizations converge. Level 3 after R04.14 on one reconciled exact merge candidate with Solidity-owned full Foundry inventory, independent Genesis authority, global, Docs and deployed/security checks.

## Canonical R02 land / R03 plant integration and production boundary

R04.9 now includes `CanonicalLandPlantGate`, with immutable verified `LandRegistry`/`PlantRegistry` references. The constructor refuses a plant registry not bound to the same land authority. An actual parcel is identified by `parcelAsset(uint64)`, and the gate consults `LandRegistry.getParcel` for canonical owner, occupant, LEASE kind, occupancyRef and capacity; `PlantRegistry.activePlantsByParcel` supplies the authoritative plant count. A parallel caller-controlled shadow lease cannot be created after the canonical gate is bound.

`LeaseRegistry.createCanonical` admits a lease only for an existing occupancyRef and matching real owner and tenant. `usable` fails closed if ownership, occupancy, parcel, freeze, lease status or term changes. Canonical `updatePlants` rejects shadow accounting; dispute, revocation, expiry and transfer use real canonical occupancy/plant state; canonical tenant transfer is deliberately rejected without a properly authorized LandRegistry occupancy transition; `reconcileCanonicalExit` requires an actual zero plant count, and closing a lease does **not** silently call privileged LandRegistry.clearOccupancy.

`PlantRegistry.bindCanonicalRights` is one-time and requires distinct `ActionIds.PLANT_BIND_RIGHTS` authorization; it verifies the bound lease gate's land and plant contracts. Private and public seed/clone admission inspect canonical LandRegistry occupancy. Existing lease occupancy must be bound to the matching *usable* R04.9 lease; missing, revoked, expired, disputed, stale or forged leases fail closed. LICENSE-kind occupied cultivation is also fail-closed unless `LicenseRegistry.issueCanonical` confirms the precise LandRegistry occupant, owner and occupancyRef, the independently authorized `PlantRegistry.bindCanonicalLicenses` verifies both contracts, and the active nonrevoked license carries scope bit 0 (`1`) for cultivation. Generic shadow license issuance/transfer is forbidden after canonical activation; expiry/revocation terminates new admission while preserving existing plants for normal termination. This pathway remains unqualified until the dedicated integration checks pass. Termination/harvest paths remain allowed so existing plants can exit.

`LandRegistry.bindCanonicalPlants` requires distinct `ActionIds.LAND_BIND_PLANTS` authorization and verifies the plant contract points to the same land registry; `clearOccupancy` requires the bound canonical counter to be zero. No module may clear occupied land with live plants merely by asserting a stale R04.9 local count.

Affected unit and integration tests are added to the R04.9 Level 1 fast workflow (rights, land, cultivation, and real R02/R03 capacity/admission fixtures). This expanded targeted qualification must pass an exact executable SHA before repository-side production integration can be accepted.

Remaining deployment/testnet acceptance: grant and lock the new binding capabilities under approved Genesis authority, bind actual deployed contracts, verify enforcement with real seed/clone/lease/plant transactions and RPC/Indexer, exercise upgrade/failover and incident recovery, and independently approve security and legal/statutory scope. No client-facing permission, collected rent or real-world licensing is implied by fee metadata.

The owner must authorize `PLANT_BIND_LICENSES` independently of `PLANT_BIND_RIGHTS`, and `LICENSE` and `LEASE` occupancy cannot be conflated or self-elevated. An actual canonical parcel license checks the same canonical `LandRegistry`/`PlantRegistry` tuple and cannot transfer licensee identity without a separately authorized canonical LandRegistry occupancy transition.
