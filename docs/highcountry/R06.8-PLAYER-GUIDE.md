# High Country — Browser Player Guide (R06.8)

## Start a guest game
Open the approved High Country site, select **Play**, **Start guest game**, and **Plant locally**. You can start without an account or wallet. The cultivation stage changes over elapsed time; **Recheck elapsed time** refreshes the display. Environment controls are local simulation inputs; set temperature, humidity, light, water, nutrients and airflow. Once the plant is ready, choose **Harvest local preview**. The result is explicitly **nontransferable** local FLOWER preview, not on-chain inventory or a claim to BUDS.

Your guest save stays in this browser's local storage. Returning to the same browser/profile should preserve progress. Private browsing, clearing site data, different devices, and inaccessible storage may lose progress. If the app reports a corrupt save, do not interpret it as an authenticated cloud snapshot. Browser clock rollback is rejected rather than silently creating progress.

## Optional account and cloud save
The **Account** page remains optional. A genuine configured identity issuer and service are required for registration, cloud save, backup and restore. Login does **not** automatically replace the guest save. After independent game-profile registration, read the cloud revision, review the configured consent policy and opt in before publishing a guest preview. Revision conflicts and revoked/expired credentials must be resolved before retrying. Cloud restore makes a new cloud head; it never silently overwrites local guest progress. A saved guest preview is not independently proven achievement, economic balance or on-chain entitlement.

## Optional wallet and on-chain actions
Do not connect a wallet to use local cultivation. Where the operator deploys validated chain/address configuration, wallet linking requires explicit consent and independently verified chain identity and scope. Disconnect/unlink does not erase local guest gameplay. Market/reward activity requires separate issuer policy, escrow/finality evidence and approved signatures. Submission or a displayed hash is **not** a settled purchase, asset delivery or payout. Sensitive operations require reviewed wallet/passkey escalation; no general-purpose signing delegation is enabled by default.

## States and recovery
**Offline / unavailable:** guest play remains accessible; authenticated cloud/indexer/RPC calls may fail closed. **Wrong network:** switch only after reviewing the approved operator chain. **Awaiting signature / submitted / pending:** do not assume completion. **Reverted / reorged:** no asset or BUDS credit can be inferred. **Recovered-unverified:** after a browser restart, known transaction hashes must be rechecked against the real RPC and independent finality witness. Never automatically repeat an ambiguous financial operation. **Cloud save conflict:** reload the authoritative revision and obtain fresh consent before an explicitly reviewed retry.

## Fairness and anti-pay-to-win boundary
There is no wallet-dependent progression bonus in the guest gameplay formulas. Local quality/mass depends on the fixed six environmental controls; the preview has no transferable entitlement or economic authority. Wallet ownership, funded balances, marketplace purchases, and cloud access cannot transform untrusted guest progress into canonical rewards. Operator deployment must separately verify real ruleset, permissions and finality.

## Release and device status
The R06.8 automated browser suite operates against a real Chromium browser and actual local HTTP/static transport. Existing R06.3/R04/R05 retained suites cover PostgreSQL, issuer-backed cloud, contracts and economic authorities. Local controlled services/fixtures do not establish deployed testnet/production, signed hardware-wallet transactions, live contract state, DNS/TLS, phone/tablet assistive technologies or independent operator approval.
