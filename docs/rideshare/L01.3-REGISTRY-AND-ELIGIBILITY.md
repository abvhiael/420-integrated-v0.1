## Signed provider contract

Server configuration pins approved driver-registry, vehicle-registry and transport-eligibility issuer/audience/public-key roots. Reuse strict RS256 verification with RSA >=2048 bits, approved kid, exact purpose, integer issuance/not-before/expiry, max 30-second lifetime, bounded JSON schemas and no remotely supplied key URLs. Transport signing keys cannot reuse registry signing keys. Driver/vehicle scopes may use separate purposes under an approved Registry provider.

Each new-risk transition obtains a fresh random challenge and independently signed current projections; there is no positive eligibility cache. Common request/response bindings: nonce, tripId, driverId, vehicleId, routeRef. sub binds driver projection/transport approval to driverId and vehicle projection to vehicleId. Signature verification is real; test-only keys are synthetic.

| Projection | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Driver | Canonical opaque recordRef/revision, accountId, active=true, suspended=false |
| Vehicle | Canonical opaque recordRef/revision, active=true, suspended=false, authorizedDriverId and sufficient integer capacity |
| Transport eligibility | Exact driver/vehicle recordRefs and revisions, driverAccountId, routeScopeRef/revision, country/region/municipality/service, operatorRef, approved=true, revoked=false, validUntil |

Transport authority must independently validate its underlying driver, vehicle, operator, insurance and jurisdiction evidence before issuing passenger approval. A caller-supplied approved flag or a delivery-purpose token is never accepted. Raw licences, insurance documents, addresses and identification numbers are not part of the schema and cannot enter public discovery through this adapter.

The gateway must resolve opaque record references to canonical Registry provenance and current lifecycle; signed projection fixtures are not proof of live Registry finality. Persistence of projection history, replay ledgers and canonical reconciliation are later owner work, not a local second Registry.

## Vancouver jurisdiction and route scope

Initial scope is country CA, region BC, municipality Vancouver, service liftd-passenger. Reject other municipalities/provinces/countries/services, airport=true and crossBoundary=true. This does not approve all Metro Vancouver or YVR. Expansion requires a separately adopted scope and qualified transport authority.

A required SERVER-OWNED routeScopeFor(routeRef) adapter supplies scopeRef/revision, exact route binding, passengerCount, expiry and airport/cross-boundary classifications. It must resolve canonical Location evidence; callers cannot submit these classifications. Missing, expired or contradictory route scope fails closed. L01.5 owns concrete production Location integration; no client-provided geography is promoted to authority here. Independent transport approval additionally binds that exact scope revision.

## Runtime integration and privacy

createEligibilityAdapter exposes immutable restricted project(trip, command) results for internal review: driver/vehicle provenance, transport scope/operator evidence and bounded admission. No public listing endpoint or administrative permission expansion is added.

Wire createTripService with eligibilityAdapter. Its admissionFor is re-evaluated on assignment, arrival and start. Driver operations must also bind authenticated session accountId to driver registry accountId. Final admission expiry is the earliest driver/vehicle/transport proof expiry, transport validUntil or route expiry; slow upstream calls cannot extend earlier proofs. Registry, route or transport outage denies new risk. Suspension/revocation blocks new risk while existing cancellation and completion do not require renewed transport approval.

Production assembly example (approved configuration/credentials omitted intentionally):

```js
const eligibilityAdapter = createEligibilityAdapter({
  driverRegistry, vehicleRegistry, transportAuthority,
  resolveDriver: createSessionClient(approvedDriverEndpoint),
  resolveVehicle: createSessionClient(approvedVehicleEndpoint),
  resolveTransport: createSessionClient(approvedPassengerEligibilityEndpoint),
  routeScopeFor: approvedLocationScopeAdapter,
});
const service = createTripService({authenticator, store, eligibilityAdapter, isPaused});
```

Defaults remain paused with absent admission. Raw admissionFor is an internal testing/server seam, not an HTTP input; production composition must use the approved eligibility adapter. HTTP schemas reject client eligibility/projection/role fields. Do not expose project() as a public driver dossier.
