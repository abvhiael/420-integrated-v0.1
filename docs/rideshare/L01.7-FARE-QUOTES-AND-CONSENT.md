## Exact CAD arithmetic and frozen quotes

Fare math uses BigInt rational arithmetic internally; JSON/SQL amounts are nonnegative safe-integer CAD cents. Distance and duration components round half-up independently to the cent. Base 400 cents; distance round(180 * meters / 1000); duration round(45 * seconds / 60). Transportation fare is max(1000, base + distance + duration, independently approved minimumFareCents). Minimum adjustment is separately recorded. Taxes and external charges are independent disclosed line arrays, each code unique per category; neither is commissionable fare. Total is checked with BigInt against the safe-integer bound. Negative/fractional/string/overflow money, duplicate charges and unsupported currency fail closed. Booking/surge are zero. Tips/promotions/waiting additions are not hidden quote fields.

The independently signed fare proof binds nonce, rider, route/provenance/revision, tariff/accepted transportation amount, policy v1, CAD and exact Vancouver passenger scope. Proof lifetime is at most 30 seconds. Its rules include current minimum driver/allowance rates; statutory higher amounts override policy floors. Live authority must validate applicability, taxes and external charge computation; fixture assertions are not legal acceptance.

Quote snapshot commits random quote ID, rider/account, opaque private route, source metrics/provenance/revision/passenger count, tariff/rule versions, rounding rule, line-item transportation/tax/pass-through/total amounts, issued/expiry times and complete cancellation terms. SHA256 canonical digest commits every field. Payload/hash are append-only PostgreSQL records; endpoints/addresses are absent. Configure quoteSeconds explicitly (1–300); actual deadline is the minimum of that bound, fresh Location/fare proof expiry and private retention expiry. No silent deadline renewal or repricing. Historical reads/retries return the original snapshot/deadline.

Ordinary traffic changes do not change accepted rider price. Fresh passenger/pickup estimates may increase required platform funding to preserve driver obligations. New destination/stops require a new private route, new quote and affirmative consent; this step exposes no in-place amendment to an accepted trip. Active-trip route changes need the later safety/UX owner and cannot bypass the preserved immutable route/state boundary.

## Protected API, consent and atomic trip creation

Production assembly MUST supply quoteService plus the existing durable trip store, private Location, authenticated session, signed eligibility and Dispatch adapters; createTripService rejects a quote assembly without durable dispatch/eligibility. Retained test assembly without quotes remains isolated legacy qualification, not a production listener.

- POST /v1/rideshare/quotes: riderId, routeRef, commandId/Idempotency-Key; exact signed rider quote.create account grant. Own-account retained private route required. Produces server-only immutable prices/expiry/hash.
- GET /v1/rideshare/quotes/:quoteId: exact quote.read account grant and owner account; returns the frozen snapshot, including historical expiry. Other rider/driver/operator/unauthenticated access denied.
- POST /v1/rideshare/quotes/:quoteId/consent: riderId, commandId, quoteHash, currency CAD, totalCents, accepted true. Exact signed rider quote.accept account grant; full hash and total must equal server quote and deadline must remain live. False/implicit/mismatched consent cannot create acceptance.
- POST /v1/rideshare/trips: includes quoteId. Actual quote owner/account/route, affirmative immutable consent and current deadline checked inside the same trip/event/command/private-route transaction. Unique trip_quotes prevents quote reuse; private route binding prevents route reuse. Failure rolls back all writes.

Actor-command locking and append-only results make parallel issue/accept retries idempotent. Payload/account changes conflict; replay never refreshes deadline, reaccepts expired data or reopens a trip. Historical command responses remain distinguishable by their original times. Session/account/pause/expiry are rechecked after lock/provider waits and immediately before mutation. Quote consent binds price; it is not Wallet signing, a debit approval, payment capture or a regulatory launch approval. Wallet consent/Pay intents remain L01.8.

## Explicit pilot cancellation/waiting policy

Repository policy version v1-free-pretrip: rider cancellation before IN_PROGRESS costs CAD 0; driver cancellation and unaccepted assignment expiry cost the rider CAD 0. No separate no-show or waiting charge is enabled. No automatic no-show declaration/charge is invented. Waiting, destination or stop changes cannot silently add a fee. Positive fees require a later explicit versioned product decision and applicable rider consent. This is the implementation choice for previously unset cancellation/waiting amounts; no claim is made that the owner previously adopted positive charges.

IN_PROGRESS cannot use ordinary cancellation; bounded safety resolution remains L01.9. Pre-trip exits and overdue expiry remain usable during pause. Each cancellation/expiry writes a single append-only assessment against the accepted quote/hash/terms in the trip transaction. Zero rider charges do not waive earned driver compensation, allowance or statutory obligations; platform-funded actual reconciliation is L01.8. Neither completion nor cancellation captures/refunds money here.

## Pickup viability and funding boundaries

For current passenger plus pickup estimates, allowance is ceiling(max(45, current required cents/km) * engaged meters / 1000). Guaranteed earnings are ceiling(max(2500, current required cents/hour) * engaged seconds / 3600), excluding allowance/tips. Preliminary driver earnings conservatively round up 80% of the nonnegative fare-minus-allowance remainder. Required subsidy is max(0, allowance + max(preliminary earnings, guarantee) - transportation fare). Taxes, pass-through charges and tips cannot cover this deficit.

Fresh pickup proof binds nonce, exact trip/driver/vehicle/route/quote/action and passenger estimates; original private geometry never leaves approved routing access. If required subsidy is positive, a separate independently keyed funding authority must attest a live reserved amount >= deficit, platform payer and reservation ref against the same binding. Location cannot mint funding approval. A funded reservation ref is immutably bound to one trip, preventing cross-trip subsidy replay. Deterministic matching skips an unfunded long-pickup candidate and considers the next otherwise eligible driver; unavailable authorities still fail closed. Unavailable, forged, stale, mismatched, unfunded or undersized projections deny assignment/arrival/start. Current higher fare floors or changed taxes/pass-through amounts require a new quote/consent rather than repricing an existing accepted trip.

Accepted fare remains fixed after its initial request deadline; new-risk stages refresh current rules/eligibility/viability and reserved subsidy. Trip viability snapshots are appended atomically alongside each assignment/arrival/start revision; failures roll back trip/lease/event/command/viability state. Actual distance/time, earned compensation, top-ups, refunds/finality and conserved 80/20 platform/driver and 20% development allocations remain L01.8. Estimates are planning constraints, not payments or payroll statements.
