## Intent and explicit Wallet authority

Payment intent creation needs fresh exact rider pay.prepare permission for trip:<id>, the actual accepted quote owner/account and noncancelled trip. Client chooses only commandId and voluntary nonnegative tipCents. Canonical Pay witness binds generated intent ID, trip/account/quote hash, CAD total + all tips, exact original payer smart account/controller, chain, approved asset, invoice/payment/conversion quote IDs, payer nonce, asset quantity, tip quantity, target, calldata and expiry. PaymentRegistry's exact Keccak ABI domain/tuple derives the payment ID; exact PaymentRouter direct-token calldata sends the full accepted collection to the approved merchant. No arbitrary contract call/recipient can replace it. In this direct-settlement subset, the accepted invoice amount includes the voluntary tip; tipUnits is the gateway valuation of the app tip allocation within that total, not an additional native charge or a claim that the Registry tipAmount equals it. Canonical registration must use the committed total settlement amount without charging the tip twice.

Quote acceptance is not spending consent. pay.consent requires independently pinned Wallet approval against the entire immutable intent hash, original payer/controller, current authorization epoch and deadline. Wallet and Pay keys cannot be the same. Wallet consent is append-only. The consuming canonical Wallet SDK checks chain/controller/smart account, approved target, full expected intent hash, exact calldata and explicit affirmative consent. It prepares a session request only; it neither sends it nor holds private keys. Session keys are public addresses; signer authority stays with Wallet/provider/device.

Canonical submission must occur within intent and Wallet consent deadlines. Later valid finality can be mirrored without reopening a cancelled/completed trip; cancelled collection requires original-payer refund. An expired intent cannot obtain new spending consent. A canonically FAILED, uncaptured attempt may be superseded with a new invoice/payment/intent and new Wallet consent only after a fresh independent failure witness. Old attempts/consents/events stay immutable; captured/finalized attempts cannot be overwritten or reset. No app endpoint broadcasts or executes transfers.

## Finality and reorganization

Fresh signed Pay projections are nonce/digest/subject/intent bound and validate canonical chain/registry, exact payment/invoice/payer/merchant/asset/amount/tips and block/receipt identifiers. PaymentRegistry statuses FINALIZED(4)/SETTLED(5) plus canonical finality are required for collected funds; INCLUDED/CERTIFIED or arbitrary success flags are insufficient. Canonical REFUNDED(6)/PARTIALLY_REFUNDED(7) state is accepted only after captured funds and exactly matching finalized refund quantities; it does not erase the original collection or free payout budgets. Stable event digest, globally bound receipt owner/type and monotonically increasing Pay authority revision prevent replay/equivocation/stale updates.

Pre-finality failure records recovery. Post-finality reorganization freezes new financial outflows; it does not delete prior capture, operation reservations, finalized payouts or trip history. Fresh canonical recovery against the original immutable binding and an independent recovery ref can unfreeze the payment. Operation finality reorganization similarly holds its consumed budget; it cannot masquerade as a refundable pre-execution failure. Restoration does not reset paid capacity. Actual finality/reorg/current checkpoints require independent canonical RPC/indexer/Pay acceptance under LT-03; synthetic signed tests prove consumer behavior, not a live chain.

## Actual compensation and period statements

Trip creation/assignment/terminal timestamps are committed with the same trip transaction. Financial preparation and trip lifecycle share a financial lock before trip mutation, avoiding cancellation/intent interleavings. Independently signed actual engagement proof must bind trip/driver/vehicle/quote, original assigned account, exact assignment-to-terminal elapsed time, actual motor distance, online time, evidence ref and bounded pay period. Independent approved compensation rules supply applicable higher earnings/allowance minima. No client-reported meter or dispatcher estimate becomes actual pay authority. Once accepted, the full actual meter digest is immutable; a fresh contradictory meter fails closed.

Conservative guarantee starts at reservation/assignment, including pickup before the existing arrival acceptance boundary; no driver loses pickup time through that UX limitation. Allowance = ceiling(max(45, required cents/km) * actual engaged meters / 1000). Completed-trip driver earnings = ceiling(80% of max(0, transportation fare - allowance)). Commission is the exact residual. Guarantee = ceiling(max(2500, required cents/hour) * actual engaged seconds / 3600); mandatory top-up cannot use allowance/tips. Cancelled trips charge no transportation fare but preserve allowance and required engaged-time guarantee. Completed-trip refunds do not claw back earned driver amounts or collected tips; deficits remain platform obligations.

Taxes/pass-through are separate net liabilities, excluded from commission. All collected voluntary tips belong to driver; cancelled refunded tips are not collected income. Development = floor(20% * positive commission after attributable fare refunds, mandatory top-ups and allowance shortages). Remaining commission is platform remainder; negative adjusted revenue produces zero development/platform distribution and an explicit funded deficit. Every CAD projection conserves net customer funds + required platform funding against driver/pass-through/development/platform obligations. No development allocation is made on uncollected funds.

Driver read API exposes separate earnings, allowance, tips, top-ups, engaged/online time and period. Period statement aggregates latest per-trip projections by immutable original driver account and independently established period. Per-trip guarantee rounding/coverage is conservative relative to an aggregate period floor; there is no period clawback or online-time guarantee. Actual statutory period, wage/expense/tax applicability and employer/contractor acceptance remain independent LT-04; this is not payroll/legal certification. Projection rows have monotonic sequence order, not wall-clock ordering for latest statements.

## Original-payer refunds, reserved payout budgets and recovery

Refund approval must come from canonical Pay/RefundManager, return only to the original payer and use the original asset/chain. Per-component refund totals (fare/tax/pass-through/tip) and aggregate native units cannot exceed capture. Reserved and finalized refunds both encumber budget; failures release it only before finality. Cancellation requires zero rider fees; independent refund authority remains necessary even during pause.

Driver recipient comes from a fresh independent Wallet payout binding for the original driver/account. Platform/pass-through recipients are independently pinned canonical controllers. Pay-owned operation proofs bind kind, exact CAD entitlement, quantity, recipient, chain/asset and canonical executor. Aggregate RESERVED/FINALIZED/RECOVERY_REQUIRED operation quantities cannot exceed canonically recognized collection plus separately finalized platform funding. CAD entitlement buckets likewise prevent duplicate driver/platform/pass-through distribution. Ledger/source failures roll back all app reservations.

Mandatory platform funding requires a separate canonical Treasury proof for the current plan/refund deficit and needed native units, correct chain/asset/merchant, finalized receipt and platform payer. Funding receipt/ref cannot be credited twice. Previously distributed discretionary revenues which become refundable are accounted for in required replacement funding. A provider cannot substitute an unexecuted promise or reserved-only balance for finalized available asset budget. Actual transfers, conversion, aggregate canonical custody/escrow and operation execution remain Pay/Treasury owners; LIFTd mirrors verified evidence and holds projections.

Pause blocks new intents/Wallet consent and discretionary platform outflow, while preserving canonical reconciliation, original-payer refunds and already earned driver obligations. Gateway must independently enforce canonical system-safety/capability policy; app pause permissions do not bypass it. Completion/cancellation never auto-capture, refund or pay.

## Protected API and database boundaries

Under /v1/rideshare/trips/:id/payments:
- POST prepare: commandId, tipCents; exact rider pay.prepare.
- POST consent: commandId, intentId, intentHash; exact rider pay.consent plus independent Wallet witness.
- POST reconcile: commandId; exact rider/operator pay.reconcile and fresh canonical Pay evidence.
- POST plan: commandId; exact operator reconciliation grant, terminal trip and actual meter/rules.
- POST reserve: commandId, kind, amountCents; own-rider refund or operator grant; canonical approval and recipient/entitlement/budget checks; development denied by LC-01.
- POST operation: commandId, operationId; own-rider refund or operator grant; exact receipt/current-state/finality reconciliation.
- GET base: participant-scoped pay.read; driver receives own statement only.
GET /v1/rideshare/drivers/:driverId/statements/:periodId needs exact own-account driver pay.statement.

No caller supplies receipt flags, arbitrary asset/recipient, payout controller, private key, mint permission, meter or finality. All HTTP responses are no-store. Actor-command results, Wallet consents, receipt bindings, payment/operation events, projections and funding are append-only. State rows have database revision/lifecycle guards, unique canonical IDs and one-current-intent constraint. One database transaction holds command/replay/budget/financial locks and fresh session checks around authority I/O; accepted projections and cash obligations survive process/database restart.
