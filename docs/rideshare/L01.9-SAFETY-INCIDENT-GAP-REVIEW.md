# L01.9 — Safety incident workflow, active-trip resolution, pause/recovery, bounded operator permissions and privacy

Status: IN PROGRESS — repository inspection and gap review only. **NOT QUALIFIED**. No implementation or CI PASS claimed for L01.9.

Canonical scope is the L01.9 row in LIFTD-ROADMAP.md. Preserve the existing qualified L01.1–L01.8 behavior. L01.9 precedes M-L01-B (dispatch + economics + safety Level 2), followed by L01.10 (one phase Level 3).

## Observed baseline
- The immutable trip kernel rejects ordinary active-trip cancellation with ACTIVE_TRIP_REQUIRES_SAFETY_RESOLUTION, but provides no safety incident or active-trip resolution transition.
- The PostgreSQL trip lifecycle allows IN_PROGRESS -> COMPLETED only. Existing event/command history, command idempotency and immutable triggers must be preserved; no retroactive mutation of old migration files.
- Operator grants are currently limited to trip.assign, trip.expire and pay.reconcile/read. They do not authorize an incident resolution or indiscriminate participant/location access.
- The protected HTTP handler has trip/payment/quote/location endpoints, but no authenticated incident interface.
- Pause already rejects new assignment/arrival/start and new financial risk; pre-trip exits and ordinary driver completion remain available. A bounded emergency path must retain safe exits, not globally fail closed on refunds or previously earned driver obligations.
- L01.8 actual compensation/finality has immutable terminal timing, original-payer refund controls and operator-only settlement projections; a safety termination must preserve those without auto-capture or automatic refund.
- Canonical matching/viability, lease expiry and original driver binding must not be bypassed by safety intervention.

## Required implementation and negative-test gates (not yet satisfied)
1. Independently authenticated rider/assigned-driver incident reporting, private incident identifiers, deterministic idempotency/replay/conflict handling and bounded narrative/category input; never accept arbitrary evidence URLs, participant identities, location overrides or fabricated proof as authority.
2. Durable append-only incident events with reporter, trip, revision, state, evidence digest, trusted timestamps and separately scoped case metadata. PostgreSQL atomic transaction and recovery after process crash; immutable historical audit evidence and safe redacted reads.
3. Explicit **IN_PROGRESS** intervention and resolution policy. Ordinary cancel remains denied. Only a separately scoped operator incident resolver with independently established incident authority and fresh trip/driver/vehicle binding may invoke a terminal safety outcome. Preserve the route and original participants, prohibit reopening, stale revisions, forged resolutions and operator impersonation.
4. Pause and recovery with independent bounded permissions and fresh authorization immediately before commit. Block new matching/arrival/start/consent/risk; permit valid pre-trip cancellation, incident reporting, already earned driver recovery, canonical refunds and finality reconciliation.
5. Coordinate, session and medical/incident privacy: role-specific redaction, no-store HTTP, no public incident discovery; no internal evidence in rider/driver summaries; operator case reads require exact case-scoped permission, never blanket account access.
6. Financial integration: safety termination records actual engagement through the terminal event, preserves tips/allowance/earnings/top-ups, does not automatically capture or transfer, and requires independently authorized original-payer refund and paid-budget reconciliation.
7. Negative/adversarial integration: cross-trip/cross-account operators, revoked/expired grants, concurrent resolver/reporters, replay/forged incident proof, double terminal action, data tampering, paused recovery, dispatch lock races, new-process PostgreSQL restart, payment finality/refund conservation, and HTTP privacy.
8. Exact implementation SHA Level 1 fast qualification and durable certificate. M-L01-B app Level 2 only after L01.9; do not dispatch Level 3 until L01.10.
