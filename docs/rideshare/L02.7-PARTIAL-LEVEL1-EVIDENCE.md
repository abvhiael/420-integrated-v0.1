## Full L02.7 Level 1 blockers — cannot mark COMPLETE

The actual backend must expose signed-session server-authoritative `GET /v1/rideshare/drivers/:driverId/availability`, `GET /v1/rideshare/drivers/:driverId/offers`, and `POST /v1/rideshare/drivers/:driverId/offers/:offerId/response`, with bounded schema/authorization and private participant projections.

L01 currently provides only `POST /v1/rideshare/availability` for availability and a dispatch assignment lease with PENDING/ACCEPTED/CLOSED states. Driver `arrive` marks the lease ACCEPTED; it MUST NOT be reused for offer acceptance since that falsely advances the passenger trip. Rejecting an assigned offer must atomically release/resolve the underlying assignment in a canonical, idempotent, compensable and rider-safe way, without rewriting the trip as arrived, double-matching the vehicle, silently cancelling a paid ride or stranding a trip. Define a distinct offer-to-assignment handshake, negative responses and safe dispatch reassignment or explicit rider-visible termination according to the canonical trip/quote/payment rules.

Real PostgreSQL security and race tests are mandatory for: online/offline fresh/stale CAS, independent active session/vehicle/eligibility admission; pending/expired offers, late accept, simultaneous accept/reject and duplicate/replay; participant/role isolation and command-scope mismatch; cancellation/dispatch concurrent conflicts, capacity under multiple assignments, pause/revocation, outage and reconnect; and quote/payment lifecycle preservation.
