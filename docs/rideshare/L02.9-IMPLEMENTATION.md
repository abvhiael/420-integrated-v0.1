# LIFTd L02.9 — Communications and notifications implementation

**Canonical requirement:** Trip-scoped rider/driver communication and updates; membership expiry, delivery preferences and sensitive-information redaction.

## Authority boundaries

- `rideshare/src/trip-communications.mjs` is a server adapter requiring the **existing L01 independently authenticated session** and current server-owned trip record. Assigned participants only, on ASSIGNED/ARRIVED/IN_PROGRESS. No public, terminal or unassigned conversation.
- `trip.message.read` and `trip.message.send` are explicit rider/driver session grants, scoped to `trip:<id>`; no operator or broad wallet rights. The L01 Authenticator must verify active session, device and recovery epoch.
- The adapter requests a short-lived trip/revision/member/recipient-specific grant from the canonical **420Messenger** owner. It refuses a missing, mismatched or expired grant and checks membership/revision again. The Messenger transport MUST independently enforce grants at acceptance/delivery and implement durable idempotency, revocation on reassignment or terminality, retention, block/mute and moderation; a LIFTd client never self-issues a delivery capability.
- `GET/POST /v1/rideshare/trips/:id/messages` use the existing strict bearer, HTTP verb/content type/body-size and no-store boundaries; responses are private and fail closed when the owner is absent.
- `notifyTripEvent` is an explicitly authenticated projection hook for a revision in the actual trip store, not a client-supplied live trip event. `420Notifications` remains separate, delivers only permitted opted-in email/SMS trip-status updates, with no coordinates, phone numbers, participant identifiers within event, arbitrary text, full receipts, device credentials or payment information. Dispatch and retries, durable deduplication and delivery finality belong to the Notifications provider. No success of independent transport is implied.
- Rider and driver message panels appear only in existing protected trip workspaces. Controls remain unavailable when session, verified trip membership or external messaging service is absent. Browser messages are rendered as text, never HTML; POST acknowledgement is not proof of delivery.

## Local scope and retained external acceptance

The repository can qualify LIFTd client/service authorization and adapter contracts but **cannot qualify actual encrypted 420Messenger deployment, independently approved keys, push/SMS/email delivery or end-to-end moderation/retention** without deployed owner instances and credentials. Preserve `LIFTd-TN-MSG` and related Identity/Compliance/device gates **NOT ACCEPTED** in `LIFTD-TESTNET-ROADMAP.md`. No synthetic Messenger service is represented as an operational provider and no live messaging permission is granted by merge.
