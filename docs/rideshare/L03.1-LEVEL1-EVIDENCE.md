## Security / external acceptance

Trip records and independently signed Identity/session authority remain the source of participant authority. A case does **not** itself perform Pay/Wallet refund, financial settlement, transport licensing, insurance approval, public dispatch or operator safety override; these remain with their separate owners and required acceptance gates. Raw files, precise location, tokens, issuer credentials and vault contents are not stored in support case metadata; only a digest/class reference is accepted.

Production-grade private evidence vault operation, actual legal/operator handling, deployed identity/service integrations and any independent passenger-transport, privacy or operational acceptance remain **NOT ACCEPTED** in the existing [LIFTd Testnet Roadmap](LIFTD-TESTNET-ROADMAP.md) and [Production Acceptance Register](L02-PRODUCTION-ACCEPTANCE-REGISTER.md).
