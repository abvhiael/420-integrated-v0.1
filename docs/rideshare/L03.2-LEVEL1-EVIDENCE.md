## Requirements and implemented boundaries

- `rideshare/migrations/013_trip_reviews.sql`: private durable one-per-trip-per-author-role reviews, canonical completed-trip FK, rating bounds 1–5, scoped subject role, unique actor/command ledger, immutable review moderation events, moderation state, revision CAS, no public table privileges.
- `rideshare/src/reviews.mjs`: only the actual rider and driver from a *COMPLETED* trip may author a review of the other participant; independently authorized operator alone may approve, hide or reject; revisions and state transitions prevent stale writes and replays; all mutations are transactional with advisory locks and fresh session reauthorization.
- `rideshare/src/auth.mjs`: separate granular signed-grant capabilities for participant submission/read and operator moderation. Review actions cannot create transport eligibility decisions.
- `rideshare/src/http.mjs`: protected versioned review/approval/moderation/participant-reputation routes using the existing strict bearer, body, verb and error handling.
- `rideshare/test/postgres/reviews.test.mjs`: real PostgreSQL trip progression (REQUESTED → ASSIGNED → ARRIVED → IN_PROGRESS → COMPLETED), premature review denial, exact command replay vs conflicting command, duplicate-role review rejection, denied participant moderation, pending review secrecy from subject, approved read and aggregate, hide/reject effects, no reopening of rejected reviews, opposite participant review, subject scope, immutable history, revoked access and HTTP protections.
- Reputation is computed strictly from **APPROVED** reviews, with domain-scoped participant/authorized operator access, and explicit `transportEligible:null` / `eligibilityAuthority:'independent'`. Review/reputation is never referenced by transport issuer, independent Registry/vehicle/driver approval or dispatch admission.
