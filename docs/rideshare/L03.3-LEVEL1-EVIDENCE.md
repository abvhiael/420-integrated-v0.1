## Implementation and scope

- `rideshare/migrations/014_abuse_fraud.sql`: private, trip-FK-constrained durable case records; five canonical categories `account_misuse`, `fabricated_trip`, `location_manipulation`, `coordinated_reviews`, `economic_replay`; immutable event and command ledgers; account/command idempotency, evidence SHA-256 reference, revision CAS, one-appeal maximum, bounded active hold and revoked public privileges.
- `rideshare/src/fraud.mjs`: participant/operator scoped reports and redacted reads, operator-only adjudication and release, one bounded participant appeal, immutable audit events and command replay prevention, session revalidation and real-PostgreSQL transactions. **An appeal cannot lift a hold on its own**; only authorized adjudication or expiry lifts enforcement. Maximum operator hold duration 24 hours.
- `rideshare/src/auth.mjs` and `rideshare/src/http.mjs`: separate signed grant actions and strict bearer/method/JSON/field checks for `/v1/rideshare/fraud/cases/:caseId`.
- `rideshare/src/dispatch.mjs`: live assignment selection fails closed on an active trip-bound adjudicated hold.
- `rideshare/src/reviews.mjs`: submissions, publication approval and reputation projections fail closed while applicable trip holds are active, preventing known-held coordinated review publication.
- `rideshare/src/payments.mjs`: *new-risk* financial operations fail closed on active holds. Existing independent signed financial receipt authority, duplicate/replay/finality, refund recovery and authorized reconciliation remain intact; fraud cases cannot themselves redirect funds, issue a refund or authorize capture.
- `.github/workflows/liftd-l03-3-level1.yml` and `rideshare/test/postgres/fraud.test.mjs`: scoped regression of all five categories, anti-replay, revision races/negative paths, policy-bound hold duration, redacted operator/participant reads, operator-only decisions, appeal without automatic release, immutable records, expiry/release, active hold denial of review and dispatch, and invalid unauthenticated HTTP methods. Retained payments/location/reviews/support/dispatch suites also passed.
- This is a **human-adjudicated fraud incident and bounded containment system**, not a claim of autonomous detection of all fraud patterns, nor an independent transport eligibility or account revocation authority. Existing signed Identity, verified location, financial finality, quote and Registry/transport gates retain their original responsibilities.
