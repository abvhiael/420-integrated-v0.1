# LIFTd L03.5 — privacy, retention and evidence controls

**Repository-scope implementation policy; not live legal, privacy, regulatory or production acceptance.**

The canonical step remains: “Implement retention schedules, access/export/deletion workflows, incident evidence restrictions and redacted telemetry.” This document records where each requirement is implemented. It does not override independently authorized legal holds or permit ledger mutation.

| Class | Repository retention/handling | Enforcement and qualifications |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Private route coordinates | Encrypted/sealed, never returned in public telemetry; configurable 1–2,592,000 seconds (maximum **30 days**); one-way `sealed=NULL,tombstoned=true` upon authorized expiry or unheld deletion | `location.mjs` `purgeExpired()`, `privacy-retention.mjs` `runPrivacyRetentionSweep()`, `002_private_locations.sql` lifecycle trigger; protected incident/fraud/support/financial holds prevent timed erasure; no resurrection or row deletion |
| Subject privacy request | Open request, subject/role/trip binding, immutable command and event receipts; deadline **30 days** after request | `015_privacy_retention.sql` and `privacy-retention.mjs`. Overdue requests must be reviewed, not auto-approved or silently dropped |
| Support/dispute evidence | Metadata references to a separately controlled private vault; support-case retention minimum **90 days**, appeal window **14 days**; no raw contents in exported responses | `012_support_cases.sql`, `support-cases.mjs`; unresolved or incident-linked cases block private-location deletion |
| Incident and abuse evidence | Incident/fraud evidence remains restricted, immutable and subject to separately authorized legal/operational retention decisions | Protected hold in `privacy-retention.mjs`; absence of a live vault or regulatory approval is not simulated |
| Financial and payout records | Immutable transaction/receipt history retained by canonical financial authority; deletion request cannot erase or alter financial records | `payments.mjs` and `005_payments.sql`, protected-record denial in privacy service |
| Privacy export/access | Only subject of completed `access`/`export` request may retrieve redacted disclosure; operator decisions are separately authorized | Dedicated signed `privacy.export`, `privacy.fulfill` and `privacy.read` grants; incident counts only and `REDACTED` coordinates/other-party identity |
| Telemetry | Allowlist only: `event`, `domain`, `status`, `count`, `elapsedMs`; no subject/trip/device ID, GPS, receipt or raw evidence | `redactedPrivacyTelemetry` rejects unknown keys or unbounded values |

**Service boundary:** Repository unit and PostgreSQL qualification can verify access control, erasure constraints, timing, allowed export shapes, immutable history and no direct ledger deletion. It cannot certify a live privacy officer, actual vault access decisions, executed timed retention jobs, off-system erasure/backup lifecycle, government disclosure, statutory legal holds or provider logs. Those are **NOT ACCEPTED**, and production enabling remains gated.

**Operations acceptance required:** For deployed environments, an independently authorized operations owner must prove (1) a scheduled `purgeExpired` job runs, (2) expired sensitive coordinates become unrecoverable across primary/replicas/backups per approved retention policy, (3) due privacy requests are escalated without auto-denial, (4) evidence-preservation holds remain effective across consumers and a real private vault, and (5) no raw personal identifiers appear in logs, analytics or export transports. Record the real deployment SHA, provider authorization, retention schedule, redacted run/receipt and independent reviewer. Do not infer live completion from local fixtures.

**Canonical sequence:** L03.5 Level 1 follows qualified L03.4 PR #688; M-L03-A Level 2 follows L03.5, then L03.6. Neither milestone is triggered by this document.

## L03.5 retention runner — repository scope

A trusted backend scheduler can call `runPrivacyRetentionSweep({pool,clock})` without exposing it to the public HTTP API. It atomically tombstones only expired and unheld sealed locations, leaving protected evidence untouched, and reports the count of overdue privacy requests for escalation without silently approving, denying, or deleting them. The existing `createPrivateLocationStore().purgeExpired()` enforces the same evidence holds. These are executable entry points, **not proof a deployed schedule is running**.

Deletion fulfillment is bound to the original requesting rider's private location data; non-owner or driver location-erasure attempts fail closed. Actual disclosure includes the authenticated subject's trip state and own request history, with restricted incident, financial, GPS and other-party material omitted. Repository acceptance does not imply third-party-provider, backups, vault or regulator-controlled personal data has been erased or disclosed.
