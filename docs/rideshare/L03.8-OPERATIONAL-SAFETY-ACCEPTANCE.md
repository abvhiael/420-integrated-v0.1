**Status: BLOCKED — INDEPENDENT ACCEPTANCE NOT ESTABLISHED.** Repository artifacts and automated tests cannot satisfy the word *independently*. ### Explicit negative requirements

No consent or emergency mechanism may depend on a sole unavailable operator, expose protected rider location to unrelated staff, allow an ordinary support worker to mint dispatch/transport authority, erase protected incident holds, or resume passenger operations merely because a technical readiness endpoint is green. Emergency access requires independent audit and expiry. An incident exercise must not contact actual emergency services or involve live passengers without separate authorization.

## Acceptance record: immutable evidence, not self-assertion

For **each** five gates record: `gate_id`, `environment`, `image_digest`, `implementation_sha`, `configuration_digest`, `authority_fingerprints`, `scenario_ids`, `redacted_evidence_uris`, `measured_actuals`, `reviewer_id`, `reviewer_independence`, `reviewer_signed_at`, `approver_id`, `approval_signature`, `approved_expiry`, and `disposition` (PASS/FAIL/NOT ACCEPTED). ## Repository-side executable safeguards and operator procedures

The new `rideshare/src/operational-safety.mjs` is an **internal gate library**, not an automatic production authorization or an independently deployed service. Its bounded checks reject missing/expired duty coverage or single-person duty chains, route an unacknowledged case toward backup and supervisor escalation, disallow self-approved or overlong emergency permissions, and verify independently signed deployment-bound SAFE gate evidence against an explicitly pinned approver key. The calling backend must perform fresh Identity/IAM authorization for every actual operation and must persist incident/acknowledgement data in an authenticated audit owner. A repository verifier cannot confirm the real-world independence of a person merely because a fixture contains `reviewer_independence: true`.

### Runbook: incident and support triage

1. **Before any shift:** verify the separate primary, backup and supervisor roster; source these identities from an independently controlled roster, test each contact channel without messaging emergency services, and record the current coverage window and on-call acknowledgment SLA. If the roster is incomplete, block operational admission rather than invent an available person.
2. **On a safety incident:** pause affected assignment as permitted by existing safety authority; preserve the case and protected evidence under retention/hold rules; classify severity, owner and time-to-acknowledgement; grant only case-scoped access. Do not expose private rider coordinates in general support logs.
3. **On missed acknowledgment:** evaluate elapsed time against the independently approved limit; re-route to the distinct backup and then distinct supervisor; record the actual delivery/acknowledgment receipts from the provider. Computing an escalation route is not proof that a human was notified.
4. **On follow-up:** audit operator decisions, retries, appeal rights and severity-dependent communication. Require a separately authorized reviewer for protected evidence export and prohibit a support account from minting dispatch or transport authority.

### Runbook: emergency controls and recovery

1. **Declare exercise scope:** isolate to explicitly labelled nonproduction identities, document exercise commander and observer, establish immutable release/config digests, and forbid contact with live passengers or public emergency services.
2. **Pause:** require an authenticated emergency-operator action and independent different-person approval with expiry not more than 15 minutes; reject self-approval, expired/revoked grants, non-safety overrides and any attempt to create a new trip or pay authorization. Retain independent audit receipts.
3. **Inject fault:** demonstrate real provider failure, worker termination, stopped instance and database unavailability in the separately provisioned test environment; verify unsafe requests fail closed. Use the previously qualified L03.7 isolated recovery scripts only as engineering baselines.
4. **Restore:** roll back by immutable digest, restore encrypted backup in an isolated environment and verify event/hold/financial record integrity; record observed recovery times against independently approved targets.
5. **Reopen:** require fresh IAM/transport authority, safety lead and separate operator authorization, verified evidence and the full controlled release decision. Health/readiness alone must never reopen public traffic; missing evidence means remain paused.
