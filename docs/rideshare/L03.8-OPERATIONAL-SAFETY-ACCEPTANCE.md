# LIFTd L03.8 — Operational safety acceptance: controlled verification packet

**Canonical requirement (unchanged):** Independently verify incident procedures, support responsibilities, escalation, operator access and emergency/recovery drills.

**Status: BLOCKED — INDEPENDENT ACCEPTANCE NOT ESTABLISHED.** Repository artifacts and automated tests cannot satisfy the word *independently*. Production passenger dispatch, payments and transport operations remain disabled. This document defines the required evidence and fail-closed release conditions, not an acceptance certificate.

### Explicit negative requirements

No consent or emergency mechanism may depend on a sole unavailable operator, expose protected rider location to unrelated staff, allow an ordinary support worker to mint dispatch/transport authority, erase protected incident holds, or resume passenger operations merely because a technical readiness endpoint is green. Emergency access requires independent audit and expiry. An incident exercise must not contact actual emergency services or involve live passengers without separate authorization.

## Acceptance record: immutable evidence, not self-assertion

For **each** five gates record: `gate_id`, `environment`, `image_digest`, `implementation_sha`, `configuration_digest`, `authority_fingerprints`, `scenario_ids`, `redacted_evidence_uris`, `measured_actuals`, `reviewer_id`, `reviewer_independence`, `reviewer_signed_at`, `approver_id`, `approval_signature`, `approved_expiry`, and `disposition` (PASS/FAIL/NOT ACCEPTED). A missing field, expired authority, unsigned proof, open critical exception or mismatched SHA means **NOT ACCEPTED**. Signed approvals must be verifiable against independent approved authority, not plain JSON or CI fixtures.
