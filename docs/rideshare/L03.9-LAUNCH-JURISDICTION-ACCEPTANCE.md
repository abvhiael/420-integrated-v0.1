## Repository-side engineering

`rideshare/src/jurisdiction-acceptance.mjs` enforces five separate, current, cryptographically signed, independently provisioned authority attestations against the same operator, named driver, vehicle, geographic route scope, service, and immutable deployment digest. Missing/duplicate approvals, forged issuer signatures, stale/expired/revoked proofs, mismatched insurance, route and driver bindings, and unsupported municipality fail closed. **The verifier deliberately returns `productionAuthorized:false` even for technically valid fixtures**: legal licensing and insurance authenticity must be established outside the test harness. The module is not yet an independently installed real-authority service and does not alter the separately qualified dispatch owner.

## Acceptance dossier requirements

For each gate require: approving authority name/role, named approved operator, driver and vehicle, scope and conditions, jurisdiction ID, permit/policy/vehicle record reference, issuance, validity/expiry, revocation retrieval mechanism, independently verifiable signature/fingerprint, immutable redacted evidence URI and SHA-256, privacy/legal reviewer and dated independently signed decision. Link it to the **same** exact admitted deployment release SHA, artifact/config digest, identity/registry/transport authority fingerprints, insurance policy and route scope. Reject unavailable or contradictory records and expired attestation without discretionary default PASS.

No production eligibility should be granted by a Git commit, mock issuer, self-issued receipt or a technical PASS alone. Do not commit personal licences or insurance documents; store them in an independently managed restricted vault, using redacted references in Git.
