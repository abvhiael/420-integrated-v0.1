## Source-backed findings and repairs

| Finding | Confirmed root cause | Correction and observed regression |
|---|---|---|
| **R01 — signed gas-budget mismatch** | EntryPoint validated and executed UserOperations without enforcing the declared gas limits used to reserve sponsor funds; callbacks could consume relayer gas beyond their accounting budgets. | `EntryPoint420` bounds signed account validation and account execution gas, shares the signed verification budget between account and paymaster, caps advisory paymaster postOp, and requires both limits nonzero for sponsored operations. Gas-bound failures release reservations, do not double-charge, and cannot replay authorizations. Tested in `WalletAdversarialEconomics420.t.sol` plus retained paymaster accounting, hostile callback, replay, fuzz and invariant suites. |
| **R02 — nonstandard session asset amount unmetered** | `SmartAccount420._tokenSpend` decoded only canonical ERC20 transfer/approve/transferFrom amounts; arbitrary selector calldata was previously charged zero or native ETH only, even when the call represented a larger custom asset transfer. | Capped grants now fail closed on noncanonical selectors with encoded arguments or zero native ETH value. Unmetered arbitrary selectors require an explicitly unlimited grant (both limits zero). Canonical ERC20 selectors retain amount-based limits; positive native ETH calls with no ABI arguments retain native-value metering. Added fuzz rejection of custom asset payloads, mixed native/asset payload rejection, no-argument zero-value rejection, and an explicitly unlimited positive control. Updated the pre-existing nonce-lane fixture to use its intended unlimited custom-selector scope; no test assertion was removed. |
| **R03 — malformed session ABI could revert during validation** | `abi.decode` on signed `executeSession` calldata bubbled malformed-envelope reverts rather than yielding clean validation failure. | Wrapped ABI decoding in a pure self-call with `try/catch`, returning failed validation on malformed input. Negative tests verify nonce, grant usage and execution state are unchanged. |

**Security boundary of the remedy:** General-purpose smart-contract calls may have indirect effects that cannot be measured solely from `msg.value` or calldata. A session grant is explicitly scoped by owner, target, selector and authorization epoch. A quota limit can only measure recognized ERC20 numeric arguments or native ETH, not every arbitrary target's possible side effects. To prevent *silently* zero-counted custom ABI asset amounts, capped grants reject these unsupported calls; the owner must consciously choose unlimited authority for custom payloads.
