# W15.6-M3 — Multichain network management

**Status:** Repository implementation candidate; testnet validation remains explicitly blocked by the official network manifest and contract deployment evidence.

## Scope and canonical authority

- Extension source: `wallet/extension/core/network-registry.js`, `service-worker.js`, `popup.js`, `popup.html`.
- The canonical deployment inventory declares chain ID **420** (EIP-155 `0x1a4`) for 420 Integrated. The official testnet RPC/explorer/manifest endpoints are not yet available in `wallet/deployment-inventory.json`; no endpoints are invented.
- A static, trusted *chain identity* does not mean the RPC is trusted or that SmartAccount420 has signing authority on that chain.

## Implemented behavior

1. Built-in chain identity registry: 420 Integrated (chain 420), Ethereum Mainnet, Ethereum Sepolia. Disabled until verified RPC endpoints are configured. User-supplied EVM networks are stored under their unique canonical EIP-155 chain ID.
2. Explicit, locally verified network addition and switching; multiple user-approved RPC endpoints, native currencies and HTTPS explorers. Each endpoint is checked against `eth_chainId` before use and when checking health; mismatches fail closed and failover never crosses a chain ID. A five-minute MV3 alarm stores endpoint health snapshots.
3. Chain switches update `chainChanged`, native currency, selected asset metadata, balances, network warnings, and the transaction review. Per-dApp chain selections live separately from the wallet-wide selection.
4. `wallet_addEthereumChain`, `wallet_switchEthereumChain`, and `wallet_watchAsset` are approval-gated, parameter-validated, and chain-bound. Added networks do not silently switch the chain; watched ERC-20 tokens are scoped to their selected chain, with contract-code verification. The provider's `eth_chainId` and `net_version` reflect the dApp's chain.
5. Browser account permissions include the chain ID; changing a dApp's chain invalidates its prior permission and emits account/chain events. A wrong-chain account grant is never reused as a signer.
6. Chain switches increment a monotonic context epoch, even when a site switches away and back. DApp approvals, RPC requests, local authority windows and popup transaction previews carry both chain ID and epoch. A stale signature, simulation, preview or nonce context is rejected.
7. Read-only on-demand/automatic asset aggregation shows each enabled chain's native balance and verified configured ERC-20 balances separately. No cross-chain balances are confused with transferable single-chain funds.
8. SmartAccount420 execution **fails closed** on networks without independently verified deployed contracts, owner/passkey and policy authority. A custom chain cannot inherit 420Integrated or another chain's signer. Hardware and EOA authority remain separate from SmartAccount passkeys.

## Test scope

- `wallet/web/test/w15-6-m3-networks.test.js`: registry normalization, spoofed/mismatched RPC identity, failover, per-network currencies/tokens, asset validation, chain-bound worker and popup source assertions.
- `wallet/extension/scripts/check-release.mjs`: package security, manifest, CSP, network routing and vendor integrity.
- `wallet/extension/scripts/qualify-popup.mjs`: packaged Chromium popup with a 420 Integrated local fixture; a synthetic factory does not authorize SmartAccount execution, read-only EOA/watch-only balance and Receive are exercised.
- Existing Wallet Web and extension tests cover provider/approval, local authority and cross-client wallet core.

## External gates

- Official 420Integrated testnet RPC/explorer/manifest and verified deployments must exist before live multiservice acceptance or SmartAccount execution is enabled.
- Third-party EVM endpoints are configurable; submitting an endpoint does **not** establish its authenticity, token legitimacy, data privacy, or SmartAccount deployment authority.
- This milestone does not implement the later EOA/hardware signing or account recovery/asset-price services. Neither a chain switch nor `wallet_watchAsset` authorizes a transaction.
