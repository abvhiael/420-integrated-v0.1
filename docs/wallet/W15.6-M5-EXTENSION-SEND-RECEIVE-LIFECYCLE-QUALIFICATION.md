# W15.6-M5 — Browser extension Send/Receive and transaction lifecycle

**Status: PARTIAL IMPLEMENTATION — NOT PHASE QUALIFIED.** This record intentionally does not claim a live or production-ready send lifecycle.

## Implemented on the W15.6-M5 feature branch
- Send and Receive with canonical verified addresses, locally generated QR, chain-scoped recipient book, exact ERC-20 Max and explicit user approval.
- Supported on-chain ASCII ENS on Ethereum mainnet and qualified canonical Names420 client, with displayed resolved address and independent re-resolution immediately before execution; unavailable chain/deployment manifests fail closed.
- EIP-1559 Economy, Standard, Priority and custom max/priority fee policies, user-reviewed fee cap snapshot and stale review invalidation.
- SmartAccount420 ERC-4337 bundler gas estimate for call/verification/pre-verification gas (with bounded 20% safety margins), worst-case fee exposure, pinned owner nonce, and independent re-estimation before passkey signing.
- Target-call simulation in preflight, plus mandatory signed EntryPoint420 `handleOp` simulation and gas estimation before actual broadcast; no unsigned preview is misrepresented as an executable signed simulation.
- High-risk ERC-20 allowance, NFT-operator and arbitrary-contract-calldata warnings for approved dApp sends, with additional high-risk acknowledgment.
- Locally decrypted and EIP-1559-signed EOA transactions (password-gated in dedicated authority window, signed envelope verified against exact user-approved intent before raw broadcast; no keys sent to RPC, worker or dApp).
- EOA same-chain/same-sender/same-nonce replacement, 25% fee bump preview, same-call speed-up and zero-value self-cancellation, exact local envelope validation.
- ERC-4337 same-EntryPoint/sender/full-nonce replacement, independent passkey signature and final simulation, 25% fee bump, verified EOA no-code no-op cancellation target.
- Durable popup and approved dApp-origin activity with UserOperation hash, nonce lanes, EntryPoint identity, fee caps and 200 retained entries; receipt polling rotates older pending operations.
- Canonical transaction inclusion/reversion/reorganization, two-block confirmation and replacement evidence (EOA signer/nonce and AA UserOperation inclusion); absent receipts and timeouts never imply automatic rebroadcast or definitive drop/cancel.
- Existing and new Node regression suites cover fee caps, named recipient validation, nonce replacement rules, signed EOA envelopes, AA gas-estimation boundaries and reorg scenarios.

## Gates before production qualification
1. **Live testnet proof is missing:** cannot truthfully mark this work fully qualified until the approved chain, authenticated RPC/bundler, deployed SmartAccount420/EntryPoint420/Names420, test wallets and reproducible receipt evidence are available.
2. Independently qualified contract deployment manifest and ENS resolver provenance/normalization for any additional ENS names/chains. Unsupported names and off-chain ENS CCIP-read are intentionally blocked.
3. Full browser-based EOA/AA signing exercises (passkey and password prompts), user-initiated EOA/AA replacements, fee fluctuation, nonces, 420Names retargeting, transaction reorgs and worker-suspension recovery on real nodes.
4. On-chain paymaster/sponsorship fee display and native SmartAccount Max remain fail-closed without verified funding semantics; EOA/native Max and cancellation behavior must be independently exercised before enabling release.
5. Dropped-transaction status remains unknown without strong chain, nonce and mempool corroboration; a timeout by itself is never proof of drop. UserOp cancellation is not guaranteed before canonical inclusion of its same-nonce replacement.
6. Independent QR scanner parity verification, third-party security review, full release/CI qualification and testnet receipts for all user-facing operations.

## Security non-negotiables
- Never send imported EOA keys or passkey secrets to any RPC, website, backend or analytics endpoint.
- Wrong-chain, stale epoch, missing contract and no verified execution authority fail closed.
- A pending transaction or UserOperation must never be silently rebroadcast.
- No production-ready or completed-phase signoff until independent qualified evidence exists.
