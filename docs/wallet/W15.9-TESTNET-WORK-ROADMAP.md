## W15.10 — Non-negotiable independent security review and final distribution

**W15.10 is separate from W15.9:** independently commission a qualified reviewer to assess and sign off custody, entropy/KDF/vault, key handling and exfiltration, secure device storage, EOA/SmartAccount/passkey authorization, phishing/origin/session grants, RPC/nonce/replay/replacement, cryptographic operations, privileged-extension interactions, supply chain/vendor binaries, mobile consent and push, and cross-browser transaction execution. Track findings, severity, remediation and independently validated exact-SHA retest.

Authorize **public release only after** all applicable W15.9 evidence (including signed Android/iOS physical-device closeout, real browser acceptance, live-chain settlement, and independently reproducible signed artifacts), W15.10 third-party audit findings/sign-off, and relevant authorized Play/TestFlight/Firefox/Chromium distribution approvals. An unsigned internal test APK/AAB or iOS archive may be retained as CI evidence but cannot be called a store-installable signed release.

No extra roadmap phase or release-approval process is introduced; this register only consolidates the **original W15.7, W15.9 and W15.10** obligations and links to their pre-existing detailed acceptance records.
