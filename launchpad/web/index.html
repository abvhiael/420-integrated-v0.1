Step 1 of 5 — Project information

2. Crowdfunding model and entitlements

Reward / preorder tiers

Define what supporters will receive. Prices are minimum contribution amounts in base units of the settlement asset. Tier quantities are commitments in metadata, not automatically enforced on-chain.

3. Funding target and contribution controls

All amounts are unsigned integers in raw ERC-20 units. The payment asset must not be the zero address. The configured payout address receives 420Pay settlement.

4. Schedule and risk review

Closing must follow opening. Claims cannot begin before funding closes. Delivery evidence and actual Pay refunds remain separate canonical obligations.

5. Canonical commitments and governance review

Review the finalized draft, metadata commitment and all three governed calls. A signed review request does not send an on-chain registration transaction.
