Security

Passkeys, devices, recovery, sessions & alerts are managed by canonical Wallet/Identity authority. Mail does not hold signing keys or shorten recovery timelocks.

Wallet actions

420Mail can prepare bounded intents & verify canonical results. Wallet owns simulation, review, signing & submission.



External integrations
Provider-neutral connector framework. Provider authorization stays in the deployment connector adapter; Mail does not ask for raw access tokens or client secrets.
