
# Public Testnet Post-Launch Operations

## Daily
- review finality/participation;
- review client-version distribution;
- inspect Engine errors;
- inspect RPC/explorer/faucet health;
- review incidents and validator onboarding.

## Weekly
- publish testnet health summary;
- verify genesis/release hashes remain published;
- perform validator restart drill;
- review dependency/security updates;
- test backup/restore procedures.

## Before upgrades
- publish exact hashes and activation epoch;
- meet governance/readiness requirements;
- give required notice;
- test upgrade on a separate staging network;
- publish rollback/abort conditions that do not rewrite finalized history.

## Incident transparency
MAJOR and CRITICAL incidents should receive a public summary and postmortem after resolution.
